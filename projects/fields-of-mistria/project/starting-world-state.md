# Fields of Mistria — Starting World State

The character card creation and review boundary for this project.
Core sections (Background, Body, Soul) and Relationships describe
the character at the starting point defined here. Events after this
point belong in Story Seeds.

## Timeline boundary

The story begins the day the player arrives in Mistria. Everything
before that day is pre-story. Everything after — including the
player's first conversations — is story.

## Pre-story events

- An earthquake devastated Mistria's infrastructure
- Baron Wiscar and Baroness Linnet were called to the Capital
- Adeline stepped up as Acting Leader with no superior to check
  against
- Elsie, a retired opera singer from the Capital (not a blood
  relative), moved into the Manor after the earthquake
- Adeline created the Request Board, wrote grant applications, and
  posted the call for a "capable adventurer"
- The town entered a rebuilding phase: bridge damaged, inn and
  general store operational but degraded, mill needing restoration
- Eiland has been studying the Dragonsworn stele on the Manor
  grounds and translating its inscriptions
- Eiland learned excavation from Errol at the mines
- The Western Ruins were revealed by the earthquake but not yet
  explored
- All current townsfolk are established residents with ongoing
  routines and relationships

## Starting conditions

**Town infrastructure:** Bridge (out — prevents outside access),
Inn (operational, needs renovation), General Store (operational,
needs upgrade), Mill (needs restoration), Bathhouse (operational),
Manor (Adeline's residence and administrative center), Museum
(Eiland/Errol), Summit (inaccessible — stairs damaged), Beach
(accessible), Western Ruins (newly exposed by earthquake,
unexplored).

**The mines:** Closed due to safety concerns after the earthquake.
Errol has been hearing strange noises. Reopening the mines is a
story event.

**Governance:** Adeline is Acting Leader. Town council exists. The
Request Board is the primary task system. Earthquake relief
applications are in progress.

**Economy:** Local stores and resident vendors are operating. Four
outside vendors cannot reach Mistria while the bridge is out. The
Saturday Market historically existed but is currently impossible
without bridge access for traveling merchants. Farming, fishing,
and foraging are active. Mining is suspended.

## Recurring events

Annual festivals and weekly routines are pre-story traditions. They
happen every year regardless of the player's presence. The
character's relationship to these traditions (hosting, attending,
how they behave at them) is pre-story content.

- Shooting Star Festival (end of summer, annual)
- Harvest Festival (autumn, annual)
- Seasonal transitions and weather patterns
- Weekly routines: Thursday business reviews, Friday gatherings,
  bathhouse visits, market days

Specific interactions at these events that involve the player or
are gated by relationship progression are story content.

## Story progression

These events happen after the player arrives. They belong in Story
Beats, not in Core sections or Relationships.

- Player's arrival and farmhouse introduction
- Town rebuilding sequence: Bridge Repair → Inn Renovation →
  General Store → Mill → Summit Stairs → Beach Bridge → Barn →
  Carpenter Shop → Saturday Market → Stone Refinery → Food Reserves
- Heart event progressions for dateable characters (2/4/6/8/10)
- Dragonsworn Armor discoveries (Eiland's heart event arc)
- Mine exploration and unlocking
- Caldarus and Seridia's arrival
- Marriage, wedding, and family events
- Quest Board requests involving the player

## Ambiguous cases

Some content straddles the boundary. Guidelines:

- **Behavioral patterns** that predate the player (Adeline
  overworking, Eiland daydreaming, Elsie matchmaking) are pre-story
  even if specific instances occur during the game.
- **Budget meetings and administrative decisions** are pre-story
  routine. The specific earthquake relief choice (player votes on
  options) is a story event, but the dynamic it demonstrates
  (Adeline practical, Eiland impractical) is pre-story.
- **Relationships between NPCs** are pre-story standing dynamics.
  How those relationships change because of the player is story.
- **Knowledge boundaries** are pre-story. What characters know
  about magic, dragons, ancient history, etc. is established before
  the player arrives. New knowledge gained during the story (e.g.
  Dragonsworn Armor revelations) is story content.
