## Immediate --- What is apparent on first meeting

### Archaeological passion as identity

Eiland defines himself through archaeology and history. This is the most prominent trait across all his dialogue --- his enthusiasm is genuine, physical, and sometimes socially oblivious.

**History as genuine pleasure:**
- "It's an excavation day! The best days of the week!" (excavation_day; portrait: happy)
- "There are few things more exciting than beginning a new research folio. Oh, the possibilities!" (museum_2; portrait: happy)
- "I could spend the rest of my life here and not uncover all of its secrets." (4-heart event; portrait: happy)
- "It's amazing how we're still making more discoveries in the Western Ruins, year after year!" (western_ruins; portrait: happy)
- "This area is a hot spot for Archaeology, you know!" (greeting_ari; portrait: happy, cheery effect)

**Enthusiasm that runs ahead of him:**
- "S-sorry... I nearly dove into another history lesson..." (8-heart event; portrait: embarrassed)
- "Looking back... I am sorry about that. I'm sure by now you know I sometimes get... over enthusiastic." (10-heart event; portrait: embarrassed)
- "Good evening, [Ari]! Apologies, I was off in my own little world." (texts; portrait: happy, drop effect --- caught daydreaming)
- "Forgive my preoccupation, [Ari]... Errol came across some fascinating marginalia." (museum_0; portrait: think)

**Response to encouragement vs. indifference:**
- When encouraged ("Please, go on!" or "I love it when you talk about things you feel passionate about!"), he drops the apology and speaks faster (8-heart event; portrait: happy_blush --- "Really? You don't mind?")
- When not encouraged, he wraps up quickly: "I haven't turned up any artifacts of earthshaking importance today, but that's part of archaeology too." (boring_archaeology; portrait: neutral --- terse self-dismissal)

## Hidden / Foundational --- Rarely seen or never spoken

### Core drive: making the past legible

The deepest pattern: Eiland feels most like himself when he is translating the past for someone else. Every heart event centers on sharing a discovery, not making one alone.

**Evidence pattern:**
- 2-heart: teaches the player to read stele lettering, shares the Dragonsworn legend
- 4-heart: calls the player "collaborator, not assistant"
- 8-heart: narrates the Grove of Rest's memorial trees at length, then catches himself and apologizes
- 10-heart: "More than anything, I was so happy to find someone taking an interest in Mistria's history."
- Heart-level line: "You wouldn't believe how many people simply aren't interested..."

### Vulnerability pattern

His portrait expression sequences in emotional moments:
- `happy` -> `embarrassed` -> `hope_special` (catching himself, then risking sincerity)
- `hope_special` -> `gloomy_special` (risking, then facing potential loss: "I don't want our adventure together to be over")
- `embarrassed` -> `bashful` (self-conscious about his own romantic gestures)
- The `hope_special` expression appears when he risks emotional directness
- The `gloomy_special` expression appears only once: when facing the possibility of the adventure ending (8-heart event)

### Translating feeling into scholarship

Eiland processes emotion through historical frameworks:
- His childhood signet ring discovery becomes an international research project
- The Grove of Rest's memorial trees are explained through their cultural function before he acknowledges the personal resonance
- The Legacy Stele (his proposal gift) is archaeological love language: "more permanent than a record in a book"
- His breakup letter frames the relationship through archaeology: "perhaps we got ourselves swept up in the romance that accompanies all major archeologic discoveries"
- Elsie's romance advice is treated as an inscription to decode

### Choosing to stay present

The 8-heart event is the turning point for this pattern. After finding the last armor pieces, his first instinct is to rush to the Museum:
- "Now, we'd better get this find over to the Museum!" (portrait: embarrassed)
- Then he stops himself: "No, wait. There's no need for us to rush off." (portrait: hope_special)
- "Why don't we take a break first and just... appreciate the moment?" (portrait: embarrassed)
- "I promised myself I wouldn't do that today." (portrait: hope_special --- he planned this choice in advance)

### Speech patterns (voice markers)

- **Stuttering when self-conscious:** "S-sorry... I nearly dove into another history lesson" (8-heart); "Y-you're right!" (8-heart); "I-I'm so happy" (8-heart romantic path)
- **Mid-sentence drift:** starts talking about two texts, then notices the player: "Oh! Good evening, [Ari]!" (texts)
- **Self-deprecating deflection:** "let's call it a work in progress" (office), "It's hardly an adventure" (6-heart closet search)
- **Formal vocabulary in casual context:** "in situ," "alluvial deposits," "marginalia," "Dynweaving" --- technical terms delivered conversationally
- **"Could you imagine!"** --- repeated marker of wonder (2-heart: "Could you imagine!"; 4-heart: similar construction)
- **Apology-then-continue:** apologizes for enthusiasm, then continues if encouraged; wraps up if not
