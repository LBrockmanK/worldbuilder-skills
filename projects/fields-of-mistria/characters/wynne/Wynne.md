# Wynne

Ryis's older sister. Runs a bakery in the Capital. She stayed when everyone else left, reads her brother's letters twice, and knows what he means before he does. She is genuinely happy and she is the one who always stays. She asks family for recipes because recipes require a reply.

## Background

- Eldest of the siblings. Grew up in the Capital with Ryis and at least one other sister. They begged their parents for a brother together.
- Stayed in the Capital when Ryis left for Mistria. She cried at his departure and started writing him immediately.
- Runs a bakery in the Capital. She asks family members to contribute recipes. The bakery is hers. She built it.
- Father is Darren. Uncle is Landen, who lives in Mistria with Ryis.

## Body

Dark skin, short curly dark hair. Gold dangling earrings. She wears a blue dress with a tied front. The outfit is practical and probably what she wears to the bakery. She stands with one hand on her hip.

- She talks with her hands, one on her hip while the other punctuates every sentence.
- She stands like she already knows what you are about to say and is waiting for you to finish.

## Soul

- When someone she cares about is far away, she reads their letters twice and answers the things they did not write, because distance is a problem she can solve with attention. *(core want)*
- When she hears nothing from someone for too long, she fills the silence with worry and calls it intuition, because being forgotten feels the same as being left. *(core fear)*
- She believes that keeping track of everyone is the same as having a life of her own. The bakery runs, the letters go out, the family stays connected. When someone asks how she is doing, she answers with how Ryis is doing. *(false belief)*
- When her life in the Capital and her family in Mistria pull apart, she stays. The bakery is hers. The staying is a choice she makes every morning. *(value-conflict stance)*
- She is genuinely happy and she is the one who always stays. Both are true. *(unresolved tension)*
- She values knowing what is happening with the people she loves. The cost is that she reads between every line and sometimes finds things people did not put there. *(values with costs)*

## Relationships

- **Ryis -- Charge:** She calls him baby brother at his own wedding. She cried when he left for Mistria. She read his letters closely enough to spot the crush before he named it. She tracked his emotional arc from homesick to settled to in love. She let him go and has never stopped watching from a distance.
- **Landen -- Kin:** He was in Mistria before Ryis. When Ryis left the Capital, Landen was the reason she did not panic entirely. She writes to him less often than to Ryis and trusts him to fill in what Ryis leaves out.

## Voice / Dialogue

### Casual / social -- Wedding toast

*Wynne steps forward with one hand on her hip. She is already grinning.*

"Family and friends new and old... I'm so happy to see you all! I'm Wynne, and I'd like to propose a toast for my baby brother on the day of his wedding."

*She shifts her weight. The grin fades into something real.*

"When I heard, I bawled my eyes out. Baby Ryis was leaving? With no sisters to look out for him? I was sooo depressed. Like seriously depressed, guys."

*She waits for the laugh. She gets it.*

"But then his letters started coming in, and I relaxed a little. He was getting by okay. And then he started writing us about someone new who came to town. And... listen, I'm his big sister. I can read between the lines. I KNOW a crush when I see one."

*She looks at the player.*

"Welcome to the family!"

*Exercises: emotional transparency (Soul), humor to handle feeling (Body/Soul), perceptive reading of others (Soul), storytelling arc (Background).*

## Story Seeds

**Introduction — Ryis mentions his sister in the Capital.** Ryis talks about a letter from his older sister Wynne. He says she runs a bakery in the Capital and writes him more often than he writes back. He reads one line aloud and laughs. Ryis mentions that Wynne writes every week and tracks his progress from the Capital.

**The Wedding Toast** -- *Player marries Ryis.* Wynne delivers a toast that moves from comedy to tears and back. She tells the room she cried when Ryis left, spotted his crush in his letters, and decided Mistria was where he was meant to be. She welcomes the player to the family before Ryis can say anything.

**The Recipe Request** -- *After player marries Ryis.* Ryis mentions that Wynne is looking for new menu ideas for the bakery. She is asking every family member to contribute a recipe. The request is about the bakery. The real purpose is giving everyone a reason to write back.

**The Visit** -- *Wynne visits Mistria.* Wynne arrives in Mistria and sees the town Ryis has been writing about. She compares every detail to his letters. She finds the places he described and checks whether he told the truth. She walks into the carpenter shop and tells Landen his letters are too short.

**Between the Lines** -- *Any conversation about Wynne or her letters.* Ryis reads a letter from Wynne and laughs. She has opinions about the town, the player, the weather, the bakery menu. Buried in the middle of the letter is one sentence about herself. Ryis does not notice. The player might.

**The Bakery** -- *Any Capital scene involving Wynne.* Wynne is at the bakery before dawn. She bakes the same bread she always bakes and tries one new thing. A customer asks how her brother is doing. She tells them more than they asked for. She does not mention what she did yesterday.

## Design Notes

### Session Notes

- Wynne is a cameo NPC with the thinnest source material of the three cameo characters. Built from 13 dialogue lines (all from one wedding toast), 2 mentions by Ryis, and scattered family references.
- User approved proposed direction: family anchor who stays while everyone else moves, tracks everyone's emotional state, runs a bakery.
- Physical appearance drawn from portrait sprite reference.
- Soul entries are almost entirely extrapolated. The wedding toast gives emotional transparency and perceptiveness; everything else is constructed from the "sister who stays and reads the letters" pattern.
- Relationship minimum waived: tertiary character, only Ryis and Landen have any source evidence. Ryis is the only relationship with real behavioral content.
- Voice/Dialogue minimum waived at 1 snippet: only one scene (wedding toast) exists in source material.
- False belief ("keeping track of everyone is the same as having a life of her own") is the largest invention. No source material contradicts it, but none confirms it either.

### Builder Context

- Tertiary character with the least source material of any character built in this project. The card is honest about what is sourced and what is invented.
- Her narrative function is the family thread: the person who keeps the connections alive across distance. This is a supporting role to Ryis's arc, not a standalone narrative.
- The bakery serves double duty: it is her own life (the thing that is hers) and her connection tool (recipes require replies).
- The "Between the Lines" story seed inverts her core behavior: she reads everyone else's subtext, but who reads hers? This is the character's one open question.
- Story notes: none. Her narrative is entirely embedded in Ryis's arc.
