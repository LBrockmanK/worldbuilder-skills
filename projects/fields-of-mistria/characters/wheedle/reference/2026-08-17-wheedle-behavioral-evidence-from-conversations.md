## Cutscene Dialogue (Wheedle's lines only, for behavioral context)

### Balor Six Hearts Event

Wheedle interrupts Balor and the player at the Inn to pitch a business deal:

> "Well doesn't this look cozy! Mind if I join?"
> "Splendid!"
> "Why I thought you'd never ask!"
> "I represent a consortium of merchants who have taken an interest in your work in Mistria, Balor. We're all very impressed."
> "You've certainly made lemonade out of lemons."
> "This paperwork transfers your Mistrian contract rights over to my business partners, and you walk away with..."
> "Well, to call it a tidy sum doesn't do it justice, does it?"
> (If asked about Mistrian business contracts staying the same:) "I'm afraid that's not for you to decide."
> (If asked about the player's cut:) "And why would you need such a thing?"
> "Well I won't overstay this warm welcome. Think about it, Balor. It's everything you've ever wanted, isn't it?"

**Behavioral notes:** Wheedle is a corporate dealmaker here, representing a consortium. He ignores social cues (Balor clearly does not want him there). He dismisses the player's stake entirely. He frames a hostile takeover as doing Balor a favor. Manipulative and smooth.

**Behavioral notes:** Wheedle turns vicious when his deal falls through. He reveals knowledge of Balor's criminal past and uses it as a weapon. He calls Mistria a "backwater." He tries to turn the player against Balor. When definitively rejected, he storms out without paying his drink tab. This is the clearest view of Wheedle without the salesman mask -- petty, vindictive, and willing to use personal secrets as leverage.

## Overall Behavioral Summary

Wheedle's core identity is transactional. Every interaction, including birthday gifts and personal relationships, is framed through the lens of deals, prices, and commerce. His speech patterns include:
- Carnival barker rhetoric ("Step right up!")
- Excessive flattery toward customers
- Self-aware humor about his shady reputation
- Distinctive laugh: "Huhuhu!" and "Hu hu!"
- Farewell: "Tata!"
- References to "the Capital" as his base of operations
- Third-person self-reference ("That's the Wheedle guarantee!", "Wheedle's circus of deals")

He is connected to: the Aldarian Merchant's Guild (high-ranking member), a consortium of merchants (business partners), "very reputable suppliers" (implied to be disreputable), and Louis (commissions clothing from him). He has a contentious history with Balor and knows details of Balor's criminal past. The town tolerates him as a vendor but does not trust or like him personally. He has open tabs at the Inn, tried to pay people with rocks, and drinks from the town fountain.
