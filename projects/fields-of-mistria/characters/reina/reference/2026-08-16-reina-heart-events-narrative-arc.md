## 6 Hearts — Identity Crisis & Sous Chef Role

Source: `reina_six_hearts.c.toml`

Location: Inn. Reina asks the player to be her sous chef. Balor arrives with news that the final judge is Taliferro, a famously critical chef.

Key dialogue:
- Reina [embarrassed]: "Will you... be my sous chef? For the contest? I don't think I can do it without you!"
- Balor [think]: "It's Taliferro..."
- Reina [serious]: "There isn't a chef more acclaimed in all of Aldaria..."
- Family taste-tests elevated dishes (sweet curry, spicy chocolate cake, agar drink). Maple complains: "Just make some of the stuff you normally cook, Reina!"
- Reina [think]: "Maybe Maple is right. We didn't really showcase Mistrian cooking today, did we?"
- Reina [sad]: "I'm not really sure which direction to go in anymore. This is going to be even harder than I thought."

Reina faces a fundamental identity conflict: elevated cuisine to impress the prestigious judge, or authentic Mistrian traditions. Her family grounds her — Maple's honest criticism helps her realize she has lost sight of what matters.

## 8 Hearts — The Aldarian Star Victory

Source: `reina_eight_hearts.c.toml`

Location: Sleeping Dragon Inn during formal judging. Three-course meal: Dragon Horn Mushroom & Thyme → Mistrian Vegetable Curry → Wildberry Pie.

Key dialogue:
- Reina [happy]: "Welcome everyone! To start your meal, my sous chef and I have prepared for you Dragon Horn Mushroom & Thyme. These ingredients were foraged from the woods and fields of Mistria just yesterday."
- Taliferro [think]: "What mundane plating. I'd prefer something more unexpected..."
- Reina [neutral]: "For your main, we've prepared our signature Mistrian Vegetable Curry. The vegetables in this dish were grown with love and care on the sous chef's own farm."
- Taliferro [think/mad]: "A... respectable depth of flavor, considering... Hmph, well... let's see how it leads into the next course."
- Reina [happy]: "For dessert, we've prepared our local specialty, Wildberry Pie... The berries were selected for their sweetness, indicated by size and brilliance of color."
- Taliferro [ugh]: "Er, no... Well, I must concede... it's pretty good. I pride myself in my taste as a chef. To deny good food is to deny my own abilities."
- Vera [happy]: "We are delighted to award the Sleeping Dragon Inn and its head chef Reina, an Aldarian Star."

Post-judging walk:
- Reina [think]: "My family was trying to be nice when we debuted those new dishes, but I knew something was off... I got to thinking that even if we won with them... so what? None of them were really true to Mistrian cooking, you know?"
- Reina [happy/embarrassed]: "So I decided I'd rather win or lose on more authentic dishes, ones I've shared... [with you]"

Branching:
- Best friend path: Reina [gloomy_special]: "I'm glad to have a best friend like you too, [Ari]."
- Romance path (requires shooting star attendance + romantic choices): Reina [blush_special]: "With the person that I care about. With... you" → confession and dating status update.

Reina chose authenticity over technical prestige. She won by staying true to Mistrian tradition and her own vision.
