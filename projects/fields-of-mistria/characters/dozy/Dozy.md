## Background

- Juniper's golden retriever familiar and co-owner of the Bathhouse. He runs the daily operations. Juniper defers to him on policy decisions, including the ban on wine in the bath.
- He cannot talk. He communicates through body language: tail wags, eye contact, sleeping, and selective attention. Juniper understands him. Others interpret his behavior and act on it. His in-game presence is narrated through stage directions, not dialogue.
- He arrived in Mistria with Juniper before anyone else knew them. They had "the Bathhouse and a cauldron and nothing else."
- He makes Juniper do the grocery shopping because he cannot reach the top shelf. He has considered platform boots but is not sure he can pull it off.
- Juniper buys him premium gold-star dog treats. "He doesn't have fancy tastes, but he deserves the best."

## Body

A golden retriever. Brown and cream coat, black eyes, rounded build. Compact and well-groomed. Clean and fluffy in all weather.

- He opens one eye when someone enters the room, assesses them, and either wags his tail or closes the eye again.
- When he wags his tail, he keeps wagging until the person leaves or until he falls asleep.
- He has 2 distinct sleeping positions and transitions between them without waking up.

## Soul

- When the Bathhouse is open, he is at the front desk. When Juniper is overwhelmed, he is running the operation. When nothing needs doing, he sleeps. He works vigorously when the work is there and rests completely when it is not. *(core want: to keep the Bathhouse running and Juniper afloat)*
- When someone new enters the Bathhouse, he decides whether to trust them on his own schedule. He warmed up to Valen immediately, and Juniper called him a traitor for it. He reads people through proximity and returns, not through what they say. *(false belief: trust is earned on his terms or not at all)*
- He banned wine in the bath. Juniper follows the rule without argument, even though she is a wine enthusiast. He has operational authority in the Bathhouse and exercises it without explaining himself. *(value-conflict stance: the rules exist because he says so, and they are non-negotiable)*
- He is discreet about Juniper's personal life. He has been with her since before Mistria, and he keeps what he knows to himself. He stays loyal by keeping things close. *(values with costs: discretion over openness)*

*Waived: core fear, unresolved tension. Dozy's behavioral range is consistent rather than conflicted. He knows what he wants, he has it, and he maintains it.*

## Relationships

- **Juniper — Kin:** She is his person and his business partner. She credits him with holding the Bathhouse together. She follows his rules. She buys him premium treats because he deserves the best. When he warms up to someone faster than she expects, she calls him a traitor and does not laugh. They built the Bathhouse together from nothing.
- **Valen — The Trusted Newcomer:** He warmed up to Valen before Juniper was ready for it. He sneezes (dust allergy) and Juniper calls Valen to examine him. Valen prescribes sweeping. On the next visit, Dozy runs to greet her. Juniper watches this. The word "traitor" comes up more than once.

## Story Seeds

**Introduction — The player visits the Bathhouse and meets the dog at the front desk.** Dozy barks once from behind the front desk. His collar reads "Dozy." He tilts his head at the newcomer, then wags his tail. The tail does not stop.

**The Front Desk** — *Condition: Someone visits the Bathhouse.* Dozy is at the front desk. He may be asleep or watching with one eye. Juniper says he knew the visitor would show up. She had her doubts.

**The Wine Ban** — *Condition: Someone asks about Bathhouse policies.* Juniper explains that Dozy will not allow wine in the bath. She is a wine lover. She follows the rule without argument. The reason for the ban has never been stated.

**The Valen Situation** — *Condition: Valen visits the Bathhouse.* Dozy sneezes. Juniper calls Valen to examine him. Valen prescribes sweeping. Then she stays and Dozy wags his tail at her. Juniper watches this happen. On the next visit, Dozy runs to greet Valen before Juniper can intervene.

**The Animal Festival** — *Condition: Animal Festival event.* Dozy takes the stage. Juniper cheers: "Take the stage, Dozy. You deserve it!" He wags his tail.

**Grocery Day** — *Condition: Daily routine.* Dozy sends Juniper to do the grocery shopping because he cannot reach the top shelf. He has considered platform boots. He is not sure he can pull it off.

**Dozy's Dreams** — *Condition: Dozy is asleep at the Bathhouse.* Juniper wonders aloud what Dozy dreams about. Sticks, maybe. Small business grants.

**The Bathhouse at Night** — *Condition: Evening, Bathhouse empty.* Juniper talks to Dozy about the day. She does not perform for him. He listens, or sleeps, or both. This is the version of Juniper that only Dozy sees.

**The Checkup** — *Condition: Dozy has a dust allergy flare-up.* Valen examines Dozy and declares him healthy. "Just a little sleepy." Dozy gives Juniper and Valen a drowsy look, as though asking if he can go back to bed.

## Design Notes

### Session Notes
Dozy is not a comedy character who happens to run a business. He is a business operator who happens to be a dog. He cannot speak. He communicates entirely through body language — tail wags, eye contact, sleeping, and selective attention — and the town treats these signals as legitimate communication. Juniper defers to his operational decisions. Caldarus respects him. Seridia cannot negotiate with him. The name "Dozy" is ironic — Seridia herself notes he dozes sparingly and works vigorously. The sleeping is what he does when the work is done, not what defines him. His in-game text is narrated in parenthetical stage directions: "(Dozy wags his tail.)", "(Dozy opens one eye.)", "(Dozy appears content.)"

### Builder Context
- Animal NPC, non-dateable, golden retriever familiar
- Juniper treats him as a co-equal, not a pet
- Banned gifts: alcohol, caffeine, chocolate, bombs — real-world dog toxicity
- Animation cycles: tailwag, sleep_1, sleep_2, doze_1, doze_2, happy
- Valen dynamic triggers Juniper's jealousy — this is a pre-story dynamic
- Seridia and Caldarus dynamics are post-arrival Story Seeds
- Dialogue is narrated in parenthetical stage directions, never spoken words
