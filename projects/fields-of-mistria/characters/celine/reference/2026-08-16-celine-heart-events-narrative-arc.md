## 2 Hearts — The Codex Mistria and the Lost Seed

Source: `celine_two_hearts.c.toml`

Location: outdoors, then cottage ruins east of town. Celine meets the player with an old book called the Codex Mistria, lent to her by Errol. She remembers seeing a rare flower from the book during childhood near the cottage ruins.

Key dialogue:
- Celine [neutral]: "[Ari], you came! Is this about my letter?"
- Celine [think]: "Errol lent this book to me. It's called the Codex Mistria, and it's very old."
- Celine [blush_closed_eyes]: "[Ari], have you ever seen this one?"
- Celine [gloomy]: "But I've never seen it since then. Maybe I should try hunting for seeds in the area..."

They dig near the cottage ruins and find a mysterious seed together. Celine takes it home to plant.

Follow-up: Dell mentions they "went on an adventure without me." Nora says Celine is "over the moon about that seed."

## 4 Hearts — Cultivating the Sprout

Source: `celine_four_hearts.c.toml`

Location: Celine's home. The seed has not sprouted despite her efforts. She has translated parts of the Codex about growing conditions (soil type, watering, nutrients).

Key dialogue:
- Celine [neutral]: "So here's what we know already... it seems like the type of soil and the amount of watering are most important."
- Celine [gloomy]: "But there seem to be other extra special conditions as well. Oh, I'm just not sure!"
- Celine [think]: "I can't translate this word here. [Ari], could you tell me what you think it says?"
- Celine [happy]: "I guess it's silly of me to think anything would actually happen right away—"

Player choices about fertilizer amount and watering. After the player waters the seed, it immediately sprouts into a visible bud matching the Codex illustration.

- Celine [very_happy]: "[Ari]! It looks just like the sprout from the book! It IS the flower from my childhood!"

Celine asks to make notes of everything they did while memory is fresh.

## 6 Hearts — The Sprout Stops Growing

Source: `celine_six_hearts.c.toml`

Location: Celine's home, then her parents' house. The sprout has stopped growing despite extensive care (lighting, humidity, temperature adjustments). Dell arrives inviting them for cherry cobbler at the parents' house.

Key dialogue:
- Celine [sweat]: "But it's not growing at all."
- Celine [neutral]: "It's one of a kind, so I don't want to try anything too drastic, but at this point I'm running out of ideas."
- Celine [angry_blush]: "Why don't you run ahead and let Mom and Dad know we're on our way?" (after Dell teases her about the player)

At the parents' house, Holt makes a pun: "Do you think your plant would like some cobbler? Just so it knows we're all rooting for it." The player responds with a pun. During the family's laughter over the puns, the sprout suddenly grows.

- Celine [very_happy]: "[Ari]! It looks just like the sprout from the book! It IS the flower from my childhood!"
- Holt [think]: "A plant that grows on puns, huh? Maybe I missed my calling!"
- Celine [think]: "No no, I don't think it was that... I should get home to write all this down!"

The event suggests the flower responds to emotion or connection rather than purely physical conditions.

## 10 Hearts — Proposal and the Revived Flower

Source: `celine_ten_hearts.c.toml`

Location: summit. Triggered by engagement ring. Celine reflects on their entire journey.

Key dialogue:

Arc reflection:

- Celine [embarrassed]: "Researching the Codex Mistria gave me direction, but everyone considered it an eccentric hobby at best... Well, until you arrived."
- Celine [think_special]: "You were the first person to really take my interest in it seriously. I'll never forget that, [Ari]."
- Celine [embarrassed]: "But together... we made it happen! Bringing that seed back from the brink of extinction should have been impossible..."

- Celine [shy_blush]: "I took a cutting of it, and to propagate it, well... I sat with it and... um. Thought about you. And it worked, another flower bloomed!"
- Celine [embarrassed]: "I wanted to grow that Revived Flower for you... So when you look at it, I hope it reminds you of my feelings for you."

Proposal:

- Celine [very_happy]: "There is nothing I want more."
- Celine [teary_special]: "I love you, [Ari]!"
