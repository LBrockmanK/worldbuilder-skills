## Personal Thread — Unavailable Nails

Source: `Threads/Ryis/unavailable_nails.c.toml`

Three-part thread, requires 3-7 hearts. Ryis asks the player for help with a nail shortage affecting town repairs.

**Part 1:**
- Ryis: "[Ari], got a moment? I'm stuck on a problem."

- Ryis [ugh]: "They're getting pretty scarce. Seems like we've never got enough!"

**Part 2:** Neither option works.
- Ryis [ugh]: "Talking to March and Adeline about the nail shortage didn't pan out."

- Ryis: "There are all kinds of ways to build things..."

- Ryis: "[Ari], hey! After we talked, I read up about ancient architecture from across the world."
- Ryis [happy]: "Now I'm experimenting with new kinds of joinery! The kind that jigsaws together, no nails required."
- Ryis [wink] [effect: sparkles]: "It's gonna take a lot of pressure off of Adeline and March. It'll take some time to get it right, but I'm on the right track."

- March: "Ryis came by looking for nails. He's my friend and I don't like telling him no, but he's gotta get in line."
