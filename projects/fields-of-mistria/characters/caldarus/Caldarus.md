## Background

- Dragon guardian of Mistria. He has watched over the land for millennia, first as a dragon, then sealed inside his own stone statue in the deep woods.
- Sealed Seridia, the Witch Queen, in the Gateway Between Worlds to stop her from destabilizing the world through unchecked magic. The act drained his own power over centuries and cost him his memories.
- While living in the deep woods, he acts as a guardian from a distance. He leaves written orders for Balor in an archaic hand, asking for "wheat powder" when he means flour and paying in ancient coins. He protects people near his territory without being seen.
- He can communicate through dreams with those who have magical affinity. In statue form, this and his invisible interventions are his only contact with the living world.
- His home is a scholar's retreat deep in the woods: scrolls on every wall, a firepit at the center, a tended dragon statue. He reads, plays lute, and tends the land around his temple. Even sealed, he maintains the space as though expecting company.

## Body

**In statue form:** A carved stone dragon in a clearing deep in the woods. The statue is tended and the surrounding area is maintained. A disembodied voice, formal and measured, speaks from the statue or through dreams.

**In human form:** Long teal hair that falls past his shoulders, with small pale horns curving upward from above his ears. Pale skin, light-colored eyes. Tall and slender. He wears a loose turquoise robe over a dark blue garment, with a red scarf draped around his neck. Red ornamental drops hang from his hair. The horns are the only visible sign of what he is.

- He speaks with formal precision, choosing each word as if translating from a language with no equivalent for casual speech.
- He once used 3 full bottles of soy sauce for a single dish because the recipe said "a generous amount." He left the onions in the soup whole and unpeeled because the recipe did not say to cut them.
- He picks up the lute after good conversations or meals and puts it down before he finishes a song.

## Soul

- When someone visits his home, he prepares food, clears a seat, and asks them to stay. He wants to stand in the world alongside others, not apart from it as a guardian watching over them. *(core want)*
- When he senses distance from someone he cares about, he grows formal and retreats to his role as protector. He is afraid that without his purpose as guardian, he has no reason to be near anyone. *(core fear)*
- He acts as though understanding an emotion requires naming it first. He asks for help articulating what he feels, treating the word as the missing piece, when what he wants is permission to feel it without explanation. *(false belief)*
- He sealed Seridia to protect the world and would do it again. The cost was centuries of his own power and memory. He does not regret the choice, but he stopped sleeping near the statue afterward. *(value-conflict stance / values with costs)*
- He has guarded Mistria for so long that the role and the self are the same thing. He wants to be more than a caretaker, but every gesture of closeness defaults to service: cooking for visitors, granting spells, offering protection. He gives because he does not yet believe he can simply be present. *(unresolved tension)*

## Relationships

- **Seridia — The Sealed Rival:** He sealed her centuries ago and would do it again without hesitation. He does not trust her instincts. He watched her draw power through the Gate Between Worlds, raise towers, and arm vassals, and he saw where it was heading. The sealing cost him centuries of strength and his memory, and he accepted both. If she is freed, he watches from a distance first. If they reconcile, he trusts the binding vows more than he trusts her. If someone he cares about chooses her, he says he cannot recommend it, pauses, then concedes that he is pleased to see them happy.
- **Hayden — The Farmer (arrival):** When Caldarus appears in town, Hayden sees someone who reminds him of "a new colt." Hayden worries about whether he is eating right. Caldarus is ancient and powerful. Hayden wants to make sure he has lunch.
- **Juniper — The Witch (arrival):** Juniper senses his presence through leylines before they meet. She follows the resonance to the Deep Woods and hears a disembodied voice say "a little to the left." She is unnerved. He is trying to be helpful. When they meet properly, he visits the Bathhouse and gets along with Dozy better than Juniper anticipated. He calls Dozy "a calming and stabilizing presence." She asks what that makes her.

## Story Seeds

**The Invisible Guardian** — *Condition: Caldarus in the deep woods, not yet in town.* Caldarus protects people near his territory without being seen. Celine almost falls into the Deep Woods pond and invisible arms pull her back. She thinks it was a ghost. It was not a ghost.

**The Mysterious Orders** — *Condition: Caldarus in the deep woods, not yet in town.* Balor receives orders written in an archaic hand, left by the Deep Woods stairs. Items are described strangely: "wheat powder" for flour. Payment is in ancient coins worth more than the goods. Balor leaves the deliveries on a designated stump and does not ask questions.

**The Awakening** — *Condition: Someone repairs the broken dragon statue.* Caldarus contacts them through dreams. He requests Magic Essence, offering skill upgrades in return. He speaks as a guardian first: formal, purposeful, transactional. The pact is the first meaningful connection he has had with a living person in centuries, and he treats it as an exchange because he does not have a framework for anything else.

**The First Meal** — *Condition: High trust, Caldarus invites a visitor to his temple.* Caldarus has prepared a meal. The onion soup has whole, unpeeled onions. The rice used 3 bottles of soy sauce. He asks if the result is palatable. He has read about hosting customs and followed the instructions with the same precision he applies to ancient texts.

**The Ache in His Chest** — *Condition: Deepening relationship.* Caldarus describes a feeling he cannot name: an ache when he is alone, warmth when someone he trusts visits. He asks for help putting it into words. The answer can be friendship or romance. Either is new to him.

**The Dragonsworn Statue** — *Condition: Deep trust, romantic or otherwise.* Caldarus sculpts a statue for the first time in a millennium. He carved it thinking of the person it represents. He is embarrassed to show it and suggests placing it near his own, "so that no matter how much time passes, a part of us will remain there together."

**Standing in the Garden** — *Condition: Caldarus ventures into town.* He treats shopkeepers with the same formal gravity he uses for ancient rituals. He once described his role as "tending to the garden of Mistria while being held slightly apart." Going into town is choosing to stand in the garden instead.

**The Lute at Dusk** — *Condition: Visitor arrives at the temple courtyard in the evening.* Caldarus is playing lute by the firepit. He stops when he notices he has an audience. If asked, he admits the song is one he composed but has never finished.

**Breath of Flame** — *Condition: Romantic path.* Caldarus describes his feelings in the only terms he has: warmth rising in his chest "like a breath of flame." He says it as a simile. It is not a simile.

**The Reconciliation** — *Condition: Both Caldarus and Seridia are free and present in Mistria.* They swear binding vows to protect the land together. Caldarus means every word. He also does not stop watching Seridia for signs of old ambition.

**Shooting Stars** — *Condition: Shooting Star Festival.* If Caldarus is still bound to the deep woods, he watches from a secret path because he cannot safely travel to the summit. If the restriction has lifted, he goes to the summit and experiences the festival alongside the townsfolk.

**The Full Restore** — *Condition: Caldarus has recovered enough power.* He grants a spell and frames it as a practical gift, not a personal one. When asked about the name, he replies that being granted magical powers by a dragon should be "cool" enough on its own.

**Human Form** — *Condition: Post-story, if Caldarus chooses to take mortal shape.* Everything is new. Meals, songs, emotions all have a dimension they did not have before. He wanted this and does not yet know how to live it.

## Design Notes

### Session Notes
Caldarus is a dragon learning to be something other than a guardian. His comedy comes from formal precision applied to mundane tasks (cooking, hosting, naming emotions). His depth comes from a character who has served for so long that service is the only relationship pattern he knows. Source material shows a character whose formality is not distance but a lack of vocabulary for closeness. He is earnest to the point of vulnerability. The pre-reveal phase (statue form) establishes him as an invisible presence: protective, archaic, already caring about people who do not know he exists.
