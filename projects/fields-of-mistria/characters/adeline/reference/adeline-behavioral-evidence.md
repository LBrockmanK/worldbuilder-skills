## Immediate — What is apparent on first meeting

### Administrative passion as identity

Adeline defines herself through work. The game shows this through dozens of specific behaviors — this is her most prominent trait and the foundation of both her appeal and her arc.

**Work as genuine pleasure:**
- Dreams about running out of paperwork and calls it "horrible": "I had the strangest dream last night! I ran out of paperwork." → "How horrible! I mean, is that even possible?" (strange_dream; portrait: sigh → sad)
- "Don't you just love the smell of ink and Paper?" (loves_paperwork; portrait: happy)
- "There's nothing cozier than paperwork on a rainy day." (rainy_cozy_paperwork; portrait: wink)
- "There's nothing better than doing paperwork outside, under the sun!" → "Oh, I wish this filing would last forever!" (sunshine_paperwork; portrait: happy → wink, cheery effect)
- Returns eagerly to work: "I've got a mountain of paperwork to get back to... oh I'm not complaining! I can't wait!" (travel_to_paperwork)
- Triple-checking tax documents is "so satisfying" (2-heart event; portrait: sly)

**Scheduling as love language:**
- Schedules bath time: "And that's why I put it on my schedule!" (bath; portrait: wink)
- Three minutes behind schedule causes distress: "Pardon me, [Ari]! I'm exactly three minutes behind schedule, so I'm in a bit of a rush!" (travel)
- Clipboard evangelism: "Have you ever thought about using a clipboard to help plan your day?" → "Yeah... I don't want to brag, but this thing can take SO many notes." (clipboard; portrait: happy → wink)

**Cannot stop working even when she tries:**
- At the beach "checking shoreline for erosion": "I'm not working! I'm just... checking the shoreline for signs of erosion." → "Oh [Ari], why am I like this..." (beach_work; portrait: sad → sigh)
- Dropped a pencil in the bath: "I told Juniper I dropped my pencil in the bath, and she gave me the most withering look." → "I had to jot down a really good idea! Civil infrastructure can't wait!" (bathhouse variant)
- Sunday denial: "I promise I'm not working on a Sunday! I'm relaxing! Really, [Ari]!" → "But let me know if I can help with anything! I don't mind at all!" (sunday_work; portrait: embarrassed → wink)
- Reina intervention: coffee after sundown makes her "crazy" — her friends staged an intervention: "Is this an intervention? It was just the one time, you guys!" → "Well. Maybe twice." (balcony group; portrait: concerned → embarrassed)

**Others recognizing the pattern:**
- Valen: banned her from all-nighters (all_nighter)
- Elsie + Eiland: forbade office napping (office_work variant)
- Reina: "You should work less and play more" — which Adeline notes is hypocritical since Reina works late too (reina_says_take_it_easy)
- Nora + Josephine: "No clipboards allowed!" at Friday nights (friday_help group)

### Cheerful competence as default social mode

Her low-relationship dialogue establishes an energetic, encouraging baseline:

- "I've got big hopes for you, [Ari]!" (0-1 hearts)
- "It seems like you're settling into the swing of life here in Mistria... I'm so glad it's to your liking!" (2-3 hearts)
- "You're always so busy, [Ari]. It keeps me motivated. Let's keep working hard for Mistria's sake!" (4-5 hearts)
- "Oh, [Ari]! It always makes my day when you come to say hello." (8+ hearts; portrait: blush)

She mirrors the player's energy back as motivation. The progression from "hopes for you" to "makes my day" shows her shifting from evaluator to peer to emotional investment.

### Practical care expressed through action

- Gives you chocolate when your stamina is low: "You look exhausted. I appreciate all you're doing for the town, but please mind your energy levels. Here's a snack!" (low_stamina — triggers at 50% health, gives chocolate item)
- Offers manor garden roses to the player freely (manor_garden)
- Recommends Inn breakfast "strongly — all of it" (inn_breakfast)
- Tells player to use the request board but frames it as mutual benefit, not assignment

## Hidden / Foundational — Rarely seen or never spoken

### Core drive: self-worth through usefulness

The deepest pattern across all her dialogue: Adeline cannot separate her value as a person from her value as an administrator. She experiences guilt when not working.

**Evidence pattern:**
- Scheduling relaxation (bath, outdoor work) — she needs permission structures for enjoyment
- Cannot take a day off even when told to: Elsie suggests beach day → "I can't take the day off, but I can get started on my beach improvement plan!" (elsie_encouraging_truancy)
- Rainy day guilt: "I do really need to get through this paperwork... These approvals won't sign off on themselves." Elsie: "Rainy days are for living, dear girl!" (rainy_day_paperwork group)
- When asked to prioritize herself at 1-heart: "A break? B-but I have so much to do!" → "Sorry, [Ari]..." → "I'll take a break after, okay?" (1h_advice)
- 6-heart: deflects Landen's concern with "I'm okay!" but portrait + drop effect betray her
- 8-heart: works to exhaustion, faints
- 10-heart: explicitly names the pattern — "I couldn't take time for myself anymore without feeling like I was being selfish"
- She likes working at the Inn specifically because "sometimes it's nice to be around people, even if you don't have time to chat" — connection framed through work proximity (travel_working_at_inn)

### Vulnerability pattern

Her portrait expression sequences in emotional moments follow consistent paths:
- `embarrassed` → `hope_special` → `gloomy_special` → `embarrassed` again
- The `hope_special` expression appears only when she risks showing what she actually wants
- The `gloomy_special` expression appears when facing potential loss
- When caught being emotionally open, she resets to `embarrassed` and often deflects

### Relationship with authority

- Seridia (the Witch Queen) suggests ruling with an iron fist. Adeline responds: "I really just want everyone in Mistria to be safe and happy." Seridia misreads this as "shrewdness" (seridia_quest_board group)
- Seridia writes a request: "Bring me 200 stone or perish." Adeline: "Well... it gets the job done." (seridia_quest_board group)
- Maple (child) wants "political machinations against neighboring fiefdoms." Adeline: "This is the real royal stuff, Maple. A good leader works hard!" (machinations group)
- She is outranked by Queen Maple and plays along: "I mean, she's a queen. I'm outranked!" (queen_maple group)
- Birthday hint: "A Lady is not supposed to speak of her birthday, so I won't." → "It's tomorrow." — uses noble protocol as playful framing (birthday_tomorrow; portrait: think → blush)

### Speech patterns (voice markers)

- **Stuttering when emotional:** "I-I've really been enjoying your company" (6-heart); "I-I'm sorry" (8-heart)
- **Deflection to work:** emotional moments redirect to administrative topics; concern about herself → "but there's still work on my desk"
- **Exclamation marks for work topics:** "Get excited! We're talking civil infrastructure!" — genuine enthusiasm reserved for administrative achievements
- **"Oh!" as startle marker:** "Oh! I'm doing great too" — Nora asks how SHE is doing, not the town, and the distinction surprises her
- **Self-correction:** "I suppose I have been spending a lot of time in the office..." (when children say Reina noticed)
- **Formal-to-warm register shift:** "Lady Adeline" in formal contexts, but drops formality rapidly with friends; "you guys" with Celine and Reina
- **All-caps for genuine shock:** "OH HELLO [ARI]. WE WERE JUST TALKING ABOUT. THE REQUEST BOARD." — caught discussing the player (aris_cute group)

## Social Network Map (complete, from 76 group conversations)

### Close friend trio: Celine + Reina (9 conversations)

The most developed friendship. They share childhood memories (candy theft, castle ramparts game on the balcony), hold interventions (coffee ban), do beauty routines together (hair oil, bathhouse), play truth or dare, host "work parties" in Adeline's office, and discuss the player behind their back.

**Dynamic:** Reina pushes Adeline to relax; Celine provides gentle support and practical help (berry ink, flower arrangements, foraging invitations). Adeline is the earnest one they lovingly drag away from work.

### Family unit: Eiland + Elsie (8+ conversations)

Dinners at the Manor with political discussion (Adeline excited, Eiland bored, Elsie solving it with wine). Beach excuses (both siblings need work pretexts). Family favorites (Elsie: "My favorite niece and nephew!" Eiland: "Aren't we your only niece and nephew?").

Extended household dinners include Errol, Valen, and guests (Balor, Juniper). These scenes reveal family history: the Manor as a "fixer-upper," Wiscar's Summit misadventure, Elsie's opera career.

### Administrative partner: Nora (12 conversations)

Professional respect and mutual competence. Nora's books are "so clean" — she's Adeline's inspiration. They work on ledgers, forms, and market budgets together. Nora sees through Adeline's work-framing: "I was asking how YOU were doing." Adeline sometimes visits the General Store just for potatoes.

### Authority figure to children: Dell + Luc + Maple (9 conversations)

Adeline takes the Dragonguard seriously. She reviews their reports ("Ooh, I love a good chart!"), lets Maple hold court in the manor entry hall, gets dragged into inspections. The children humanize her — she can't say no to Queen Maple, plays the quiet game (which fails immediately), and discovers Dell has been filing "spy reports."

Maple is her administrative heir: "She's getting better and better at writing proposals. She's got a promising career ahead of her!" (snack_funding)

### Business relationships: March, Ryis, Landen, Balor, Olric

Each has a distinct pattern:
- **March:** respects his capability, knows he won't ask for help, needs to ask twice
- **Ryis:** trusted carpenter, compared favorably to the player
- **Landen:** reliable foreman, she checks he delegates to Ryis
- **Balor:** appreciates but slightly suspicious; can't convince him to get a permanent storefront
- **Olric:** cheerful intermediary; relays March's actual needs

### Caldarus (dragon-turned-townsperson)

Beautifully awkward. Caldarus confesses to bypassing the request board ("I am prepared to accept whatever punishment you decide"). Adeline: "That's not really how I do things..." He compliments her leadership as "a well-tended garden." She's genuinely touched.

### Seridia (Witch Queen)

Comic philosophical contrast. Seridia advocates iron-fist rule; Adeline advocates community. Seridia suggests decorating with battleaxes and bone chandeliers; Adeline: "I think I'm going to leave everything as it is!" Seridia's verdict: Adeline's kindness must actually be "shrewdness." They drink together at the Manor with Elsie.

### Core want (Soul, required doctrine)
**Proposed:** To make Mistria safe and happy — expressed directly to Seridia: "I really just want everyone in Mistria to be safe and happy."
**Evidence strength:** Stated explicitly; supported by every infrastructure project, business review, and care gesture.

### Core fear (Soul, required doctrine)
**Proposed:** Being useless / falling behind / letting people down
**Evidence:** Never stated directly. Inferred from: scheduling all relaxation, guilt when not working ("I couldn't take time for myself without feeling selfish"), fainting from overwork, needing permission structures for enjoyment. The paperwork dream (running out = "horrible") treats the absence of work as existential threat.
**Confidence:** High inference, but needs human confirmation.

### False belief (Soul, required doctrine)
**Proposed:** "If I stop working, everything falls apart"
**Evidence:** Behavioral only. She works to physical collapse. Others (Valen, Elsie, Reina, Landen) all independently tell her to slow down. She deflects every time. The resolution in her arc (10-heart) shows her recognizing this pattern.
**Confidence:** Strong inference from behavioral pattern; needs human confirmation.

### Value-conflict stance (Soul, required doctrine)
**Proposed:** Duty over self-care — she follows duty until her body physically stops her. The lever that tips her is another person's concern (Landen's warning, the player's care), not her own recognition. Guilt manifests as deflection and minimization ("I'm okay!"), never anger or acknowledgment.
**Evidence:** 6-heart (deflects Landen), 8-heart (collapses), 10-heart (names the pattern).
**Confidence:** Well-supported.

### Unresolved tension (Soul, required doctrine)
**Proposed:** She loves her work genuinely (it's not just obligation — she dreams about it, finds it "satisfying," writes in pink ink) BUT she uses it to avoid vulnerability. The tension is that the thing she uses to hide is also the thing she authentically loves. Resolving one risks losing the other.
**Evidence:** Beach work self-awareness ("why am I like this"), simultaneous joy and compulsion in the same dialogue lines.
**Confidence:** Interpretive but well-grounded.
