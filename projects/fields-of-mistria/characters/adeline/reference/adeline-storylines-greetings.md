## Heart Event Storylines

### Paperwork Party (2 Hearts)

**Trigger:** Relationship reaches 2 hearts; gameplay-triggered event
**Setting:** Manor house — Adeline, Eiland, and Elsie working on administrative tasks
**What happens:** Adeline invites the player to a working session. Each family member handles different paperwork: Elsie writes grant applications, Eiland processes excavation paperwork, Adeline handles tax documents. The player reviews completed work. Treats from the Inn reward whoever finishes first.
**Player choices:** Enthusiasm for paperwork (genuine vs. tentative) — affects Adeline's response warmth but not outcome.
**Key character moment:** "Nothing better than a triple check, it's so satisfying!" — reveals work as genuine pleasure, not performance.
**Emotional register:** Light, warm, comedic. Establishes family dynamic and Adeline's administrative identity.
**NPCs present:** Eiland, Elsie

### Earthquake Relief Choice (4 Hearts)

**Trigger:** Relationship reaches 4 hearts; gameplay-triggered event
**Setting:** Town — Adeline presents earthquake relief options
**What happens:** Adeline's grant applications to the Capital succeeded. She presents two relief choices: practical supplies (stone and wood for rebuilding) or a cookie care package from a famous Capital bakery (Eiland's idea). The player chooses. Follow-up reactions from Landen, Hayden, Eiland, or Dell depending on choice.
**Player choices:** Supplies vs. cookies — affects which follow-up dialogue triggers from NPCs, plus material rewards.
**Key character moment:** Portrait shifts reveal her preference (concerned before cookies, sigh before endorsing Eiland's idea) while diplomatically presenting both options as valid.
**Emotional register:** Professional pride modulated by sibling exasperation. Shows competent leadership and democratic decision-making.
**NPCs present:** None during main scene; follow-ups involve Landen, Hayden, Eiland, Dell

### Town Inspection Walk (6 Hearts)

**Trigger:** Relationship reaches 6 hearts; gameplay-triggered event
**Setting:** Walking tour of Mistria, visiting various locations
**What happens:** Adeline takes the player on an inspection of the town. Landen warns her not to overwork herself; she deflects. She admits enjoying the player's company but frames it through obligation. She offers the player an exit ("I won't keep you"). The walk ends with her instinct to return to desk work; the player can suggest a meal at the Inn instead.
**Player choices:** Offer breaks / encourage continuation; suggest meal at the end. All paths lead to the same emotional outcome — she's touched by the attention.
**Key character moment:** "I-I've really been enjoying your company, though" — the stutter. And: Landen's warning triggers concerned + drop effect, showing it lands harder than she admits.
**Emotional register:** Shifting from professional composure to personal vulnerability. First sustained one-on-one time that isn't work-framed.
**NPCs present:** Landen (brief appearance)

### Collapse (8 Hearts)

**Trigger:** Relationship reaches 8 hearts; gameplay-triggered event
**Setting:** Adeline's room at the Manor — tea/date
**What happens:** The player arrives; Adeline is visibly exhausted (tired portrait variants throughout). She shares a childhood memory: her mother's Summit picnics, picking lemons, baking with Eiland. She starts to ask the player something important — then faints. After recovery, she asks if the player will stay in Mistria. The event branches: declaring feelings (dating path) or affirming friendship (best friend path). She gives the player the Hero's Ring, a town heirloom.
**Player choices:** Best friend vs. romantic partner — major path divergence affecting all subsequent heart events.
**Key character moment:** The collapse itself — her body forces what her mind won't allow. Also: the confession that she broke the player's welcome vase on arrival night because she was flustered.
**Emotional register:** Most emotionally intense event. Moves through nostalgia, crisis, vulnerability, and commitment. The tired portraits throughout create sustained tension.
**NPCs present:** None during scene; follow-ups from Eiland, Elsie

### Summit Proposal (10 Hearts)

**Trigger:** Player presents engagement ring while dating Adeline
**Setting:** Walk to the Summit — her childhood picnic spot
**What happens:** Adeline reflects on the view and her growth. She names her core pattern explicitly ("I couldn't take time for myself without feeling selfish") and its resolution ("I don't feel that way anymore"). She gives the player a handcrafted Lifetime Planner. She tries to propose but can't find the words; the player proposes instead. She accepts.
**Player choices:** Propose now vs. delay — delaying lets the player return later.
**Key character moment:** "There's only one person that I just couldn't live without" — and the Lifetime Planner as emotional expression through administrative love language.
**Emotional register:** Arc resolution. Peaceful, intimate, joyful. The Summit connects to the childhood memory from the 8-heart event.
**NPCs present:** None

### Wedding Ceremony

**Trigger:** Post-proposal; scheduled ceremony
**Setting:** Gazebo (where her parents were married), then reception, then farmhouse
**What happens:** Elsie officiates a candle-lighting ceremony with light metaphors. Adeline's vows tie personal love to shared purpose ("your light is the sun that brightens my world... this land that we would protect and lead together"). Reception: Wiscar and Linnet attend from the Capital — Wiscar trusts Mistria to them; Linnet reveals Adeline wrote about the player in letters home.
**Key character moment:** Even in vows, she frames love through Mistria. And: learning she wrote about the player all along.
**Emotional register:** Ceremonial warmth. Family reunion undertones.
**NPCs present:** Elsie (officiant), Wiscar, Linnet, Eiland, townspeople

## Story Event Storylines

These are town infrastructure events where Adeline appears as the administrative leader. Each represents a potential scenario:

### Town Rebuilding Projects (progressive)

Adeline coordinates all major infrastructure projects in sequence:
- **Bridge Repair** (Parts 1-2): "Repairing the bridge is only the beginning. Get excited, [Ari]! We're talking civil infrastructure!"
- **Inn Renovation**: proposes project, coordinates with Hemlock/Josephine
- **General Store Upgrade**: coordinates with Nora/Holt
- **Mill Restoration**: manages the approval and supply chain
- **Summit Stairs Repair**: organizes effort
- **Beach Bridge Restoration**: coordinates
- **Barn Expansion**: proposes expanded barn project for Hayden
- **Carpenter Shop Upgrade**: approves expansion
- **Saturday Market** (upgrade + plaza): negotiates with Aldarian Merchant's Guild; gathers construction team
- **Stone Refinery Workshop**: orchestrates mining infrastructure
- **Food Reserves** (Parts 1-2): emergency stockpile coordination, then long-lasting rations

Each follows a pattern: Adeline identifies the need, presents a plan, involves relevant townspeople, assigns tasks to the player, celebrates completion.

### Quest Board Introduction

**Trigger:** Early game, player enters town
**What happens:** Adeline introduces the Request Board system, explains how quests work, shows the player the Map feature.
**Key character moment:** Her enthusiasm for the administrative system she designed.

### Day Zero — Arrival

**Trigger:** Game start
**What happens:** Adeline greets the player at the farmhouse she prepared for them. She is the reason the player came to Mistria (posted the "capable adventurer" request).

### Lost and Found

**Trigger:** Story progression
**What happens:** Adeline leads a town council meeting, assigns Dragonguard duties — showing her leadership and management of both adults and children.

### Town Rank Milestones

**Trigger:** Various progression thresholds
**What happens:** Adeline and Eiland present at rank announcements (e.g., Mistril Star). Marks collective achievement.

## Situational Greetings

These are contextual dialogue triggers that work as ambient greetings:

### Weather-responsive
- Pleasant weather, outdoors: "Isn't it beautiful out here, [Ari]?" (beautiful_out_here)
- Rain, writing at desk: "There's nothing cozier than paperwork on a rainy day." (rainy_cozy_paperwork)
- Sun, writing outdoors: "There's nothing better than doing paperwork outside, under the sun!" (sunshine_paperwork)

### Location-specific
- Quest Board (multiple variants by progression): "Don't suppose I could interest you in a teensy quest or two?" / "Morning! I was posting some new requests!"
- Bathhouse: "I smell lavender and roses from the bath! I can't wait to jump in." / post-bath: "Whew... a nice, relaxing bath was just what I needed."
- Inn (morning): "Morning paperwork over some of Reina's Wild Berry Scones... ooh, I just know it's going to be a great day!"
- Inn (evening, wine): "March and Ryis are right... there's something nice about a drink after a long day of work."
- Beach: "The Beach is so peaceful... I'm glad you're here to enjoy it too!" / "I saw a crab, [Ari]! It was so cute! Even though it pinched me!"
- Manor garden: various seasonal observations about flowers, family memories

### Time-sensitive
- First week: "Sorry, I was just thinking about some forms I need to fill out later." (new-player encounter)
- One year milestone: "Gosh, it feels like you've always been part of our community! How time flies!" (time_flies)
- Quest board anniversary: "I was still working out my request board system last year, but now it feels like old hat!"

### Activity-triggered
- Low player health: gives chocolate with concern (low_stamina)
- Walking, traveling: "Even though I grew up in Mistria, I always see something new when I'm out and about."
- Her writing animation: "I'm on a tear today! Sometimes leaving my office really helps open my frame of mind." (productive_day)
- Seasonal transition: "The turning of the seasons is so exciting. Time for a fresh start for the request board!" (fresh_start)

### Relationship-gated
- Post-D&D at the Inn: "That evening of Dragons & Drama was more gripping than I expected. Enough for me to forget about work!" (dnd_follow_up)
- Champion's Shield: acknowledges the King's rare honor with genuine admiration (champion_shield_follow_up)
- "Thanks for always making time for me, [Ari]. I'm glad we can be more than work friends!" (4-5 hearts; thanks_for_making_time)
