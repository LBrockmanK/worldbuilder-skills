## Identity

- **Name:** Adeline
- **Title:** Acting Leader of Mistria (official); "Lady Adeline" (used by children and formal contexts)
- **Bio (game text):** "Bright-eyed, hardworking noble looking to help Mistria recover after the earthquake. Eiland's sister."
- **Birthday:** Winter 18
- **Tags:** townsfolk, noble, dateable
- **Affiliation:** Royal Family of Mistria (Baron/Baroness lineage)
- **Aldarian name:** ADLN

## Family & Household

- **Brother:** Eiland — co-manages town affairs from the same manor. Teasing sibling dynamic; she assigns him paperwork he finds archaic, he lobbies for cookies over practical supplies. As children: she built scale models of Mistria from snow, he played "rampaging dragon." He ate sugar straight from the bag. She worries he prioritizes archaeology over Mistria's future.
- **Great Aunt:** Elsie — NOT a blood relative (wiki-confirmed trivia). Lives at the Manor. Former opera singer in the Capital. Adeline admires her vitality ("Her life is SO exciting") but finds her exhausting. Elsie officiated Adeline's parents' wedding by the gazebo. Elsie has banned Adeline from napping in the office.
- **Father:** Baron Wiscar of Mistria — moved to the Capital after the earthquake. Hands-on personality: renovated the Manor himself when they first moved in, injured himself frequently with tools (Linnet hid the toolbox). Got stranded on the Summit in winter with Errol and Valen's father.
- **Mother:** Baroness Linnet of Mistria — initially rejected Wiscar because she didn't want to marry into nobility; they eventually eloped. Took young Adeline on Summit picnics in spring to pick lemons and bake together. Adeline chose her mother's old office specifically to avoid repeating her childhood habit of napping under the desk. Adeline writes her letters about the player.
- **Child:** Ellie (daughter, post-marriage gameplay)

## Knowledge & Competence

**Established expertise:**
- Administrative law: grant writing, tax documents, civic code proposals, budget management
- Town management: infrastructure planning, business reviews, supply chain coordination
- Documentation: archiving, record-keeping, form design, itemization
- Diplomatic communication: working with the Capital, Aldarian Merchant's Guild, Crown Requests

**Stated gaps:**
- Archaeology (defers to Eiland): "I don't really understand what Eiland is doing with his excavations"
- Cooking (implied): no cooking-related dialogue; loves Darcy's pastries, Reina's cooking
- Fishing: interested but inexperienced; Terithia offers lessons, she worries fish are "feisty"
- Physical combat: not referenced; defers to Errol on mine safety

**Way of handling not-knowing:**
- Delegates to the expert: "Eiland could probably tell you more" (about the old statue)
- Collaborates across domains: contacts Celine for tree identification when Ryis can't identify wood
- Admits limits cheerfully: "I don't really understand" without defensiveness
