## Summary of Personality Patterns from Dialogue

**Speech habits:** Uses exclamation marks frequently. Casual and warm. Says "hehe," "whew," "wowee," "zippy," "jiffy." Addresses the player by name often. Occasional winking tone.

**Core identity:** Defines herself through her furniture business. Nearly every line connects to sourcing, restoring, selling, or appreciating furniture and decor.

**Relationships revealed:**
- Ryis and Landen: Teach her advanced carpentry techniques
- Olric and Hayden: Offer to help her pack up; she's friendly with both
- Jo (Josephine) and Hemlock: Customers who bought a big dresser
- Balor: Trade contact who can source antique furniture from Caldosia
- Errol: Someone she'd ask for color-matching help (painter/artist)
- Louis: Fellow Saturday Market vendor (hats/clothing)
- Seridia: A difficult dragon customer

**Attitudes:** Enthusiastic and hardworking. Physically strong and proud of it. Romantic about furniture's history and lifecycle. Sees interior design as a lifestyle, not a chore. Business-savvy (counts tesserae, plans inventory) but genuinely loves her craft. Gets inspired by customers. Values rest after long market days.
