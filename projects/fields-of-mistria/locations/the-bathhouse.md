# The Bathhouse

*Steam and copper — where the town comes to soak and slow down.*

## Keywords

bathhouse, the bathhouse, baths, bath, hot spring, spa

## Setting

The Bathhouse stands in Mistria near the fountain square. Dozy manages the front desk. Beyond the entrance, a changing room with benches leads to the bath hall proper, where four cauldron baths sit in a row. Juniper and Dozy live upstairs.

## Atmosphere

Warm and heavy with steam. The Bathhouse is the most physically relaxing space in Mistria — the cauldron baths are oversized copper vessels filled with heated water, and the air around them is thick and soft. The changing room is social in a casual way, benches and stools where people sit half-ready. Juniper supervises the baths with professional attention. Dozy holds the front desk with a quieter energy.

It is the one place in town where no one is working, building, or selling anything. Just heat and water and time.
