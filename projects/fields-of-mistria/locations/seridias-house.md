# Seridia's House

*A home at the edge of the ruins, where one room faces the world and another faces something else entirely.*

## Setting

Seridia's house stands near the Western Ruins, set apart from Mistria proper. The front room is a living space — her home, furnished and personal. A back room opens behind it, and the atmosphere there shifts completely. The void ambience that fills that space has no parallel anywhere else in Mistria above ground.

## Atmosphere

The front of Seridia's house is warm in a reserved way — she lives here, and the space reflects a person who keeps her own counsel. Her personal music plays, distinct from any location theme.

The back room is something else. The void ambience that fills it connects to the deepest places beneath the mountain — the same sound that lives in the seal chambers and the Priestess' Quarters far underground. Whatever Seridia's relationship to those depths, her house holds a door to it.
