# Sweetwater Farm

*Hayden's place — where the animals know you before you reach the gate.*

## Setting

Sweetwater Farm lies east of Mistria, a working livestock operation run by Hayden. A barn houses cows and horses. The farmhouse doubles as a shop for animal supplies. A statue stands on the grounds, and the land extends into open pasture and foraging ground.

## Atmosphere

Sweetwater has the comfortable, unselfconscious atmosphere of a place built around daily work rather than appearances. The animals set the rhythm. Visitors come to talk farming, buy supplies, or just sit at the bench inside Hayden's house. The air smells like hay and earth and the particular warmth of a barn in the afternoon.
