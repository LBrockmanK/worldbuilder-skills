# The Deep Woods

*Dense forest where the canopy closes overhead and the air smells of old growth and strange flowers.*

## Keywords

deep woods, the deep woods, forest, woods, dragonsworn glade

## Setting

North of Mistria, the woods thicken into something older. Caldarus' house sits deep within, accessible by winding paths through heavy tree cover. Ponds — one to the west, one to the east — break the forest floor. Garden plots grow in clearings where enough light reaches down. A bench near Caldarus' home overlooks the water.

Beyond the main woods lies the Dragonsworn Glade, a quieter clearing with sparse undergrowth and an air of deliberate stillness.

## Atmosphere

The Deep Woods feel enclosed and alive. Birdsong is constant — more birds here than anywhere else in Mistria. The flora is different too: bell berries, temple flowers, spirit mushrooms, things that don't grow in tended gardens. Light filters green through the canopy. The ponds sit dark and still.

It is a place where someone chose to live away from everyone, and the forest closed in around the choice.
