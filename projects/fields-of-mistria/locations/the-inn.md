# The Inn

*The social heart of Mistria — where the town gathers, stories are told, and Friday nights come alive.*

## Keywords

inn, the inn, sleeping dragon, tavern, bar, pub

## Setting

The Inn sits in southwestern Mistria, just west of the main road into town. A two-story building with a ground-floor bar and dining area, a kitchen in the back, and a balcony overlooking the main room. Private rooms upstairs house the family and at least one long-term lodger.

It's run by Hemlock and Josephine as a live-in family business — they and their children live above the establishment. The Inn serves food, drinks, and offers lodging.

## Atmosphere

Warm, lived-in, lively on busy nights and quietly comfortable on slow ones. The bar is the centerpiece of the ground floor. Tables seat groups for meals and conversation. A small performance area hosts music and storytelling — Josephine sings, Hemlock plays.
