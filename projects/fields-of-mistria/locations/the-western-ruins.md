# The Western Ruins

*An archaeological dig where the old world surfaces in broken stone and buried artifacts.*

## Keywords

western ruins, the western ruins, ruins, dig site, excavation

## Setting

West of Mistria, past the edge of settled land, the Western Ruins spread across uneven ground. An excavation pit anchors the site, surrounded by tarps covering sorted artifacts. Dig site tours run when someone is available to lead them. Seridia's house stands nearby, a short walk from the work.

## Atmosphere

The Western Ruins feel purposeful and solitary. The work here is slow and careful — brushing dirt from stone, cataloguing what comes up. The ruins themselves are older than anyone in Mistria can date with certainty. Wind moves across the open ground without the trees or buildings to break it.

There is a sense of listening here — as though the earth has something to say and the dig is the act of leaning closer.
