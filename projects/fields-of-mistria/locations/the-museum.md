# The Museum

*Four wings, four collections, and the slow work of cataloguing what Mistria has lost and found.*

## Setting

The Museum stands in the Narrows, built into the rocky landscape between town and the mines. The entry hall leads to four collection wings: Archaeology, Insect, Fish, and Flora. A desk near the entrance serves visitors. Display cases and wall space fill as collections grow.

## Atmosphere

Quiet and institutional in the best sense — a place that takes the act of looking seriously. Each wing has its own character shaped by what it holds. The Archaeology wing connects to the dig work at the Western Ruins. The natural history wings — insect, fish, flora — turn the valley's living things into objects of study and appreciation.

The Museum is one of the few places in Mistria that feels permanent by design rather than by survival. It was built to remember.
