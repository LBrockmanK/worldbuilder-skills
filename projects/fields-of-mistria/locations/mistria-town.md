# Mistria

*The heart of everything — a small town rebuilding itself around a fountain, a manor on the hill, and the people who stayed.*

## Setting

Mistria spreads along a main road that runs north to south, with a fountain square near its center. The Manor House sits on a hill at the northern end, overlooking the town through its gardens and gazebo. South of the fountain, the Inn and General Store face each other across the road. The Blacksmith's forge works in the open air near the fountain, and the Bathhouse and Clinic sit to the east. A mill stands at the town's edge, and Celine's cottage garden grows quietly to the south.

The town connects outward in every direction — south to the farm, east to the Eastern Road and Sweetwater Farm, north to the Deep Woods, west to the Narrows and the Western Ruins, and south again to the Beach.

## Atmosphere

Mistria feels like a place that was bigger once and is learning to be enough. Buildings stand in various states of repair — some restored, some still waiting. On calm days, the fountain square fills with conversation and the sound of the forge. Saturday brings the travelling market: Louis, Vera, Darcy, Merri, and the other vendors set up their stalls. Balor's wagon sits parked to one side, always.

Children play by the big tree. Celine tends her garden. The smell of the forge mixes with whatever Hemlock is cooking at the Inn. In the evenings, light spills from windows onto the road.
