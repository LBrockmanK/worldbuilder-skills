# The Summit

*The peak above Mistria, where the sky opens wide and the town is a scatter of rooftops below.*

## Setting

The Summit sits at the top of the mountain above town, reached by climbing through the Narrows. The terrain is sparse — rock and scrub, little shade, the wind stronger than anywhere else in the valley.

## Atmosphere

Exposed and quiet. The Summit strips away Mistria's warmth and noise, leaving sky and stone. On clear nights, the stars are visible in a way they aren't from town. During the Shooting Star Festival, the town gathers here to watch the sky — one of the few times this lonely place fills with people.
