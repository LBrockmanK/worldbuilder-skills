# The Beach

*Salt air and warm sand, where Mistria meets the sea.*

## Setting

The Beach stretches along the coast south of Mistria. A dock extends into the water. Campfire benches cluster near the shore, and a picnic area offers seating with a view. Towels mark where people settle in the sand. Terithia's Tackle Shop sits at the edge of the beach, part storefront, part home.

A hidden cave opens somewhere along the shoreline for those who find it.

## Atmosphere

The Beach is the brightest, most open space in Mistria's world. The ambient sound shifts here — waves replace birdsong, and the air carries salt instead of forest damp. Sand castles appear and collapse. The campfire draws people in the evening. The dock is a place for quiet conversations with water underneath.

It feels like a rest from the weight of the inland places — the ruins, the mines, the woods. Nothing buried here, just surf and light.
