# The Mines

*The mountain's interior — layer after layer descending toward something ancient and sealed.*

## Keywords

mines, the mines, dungeon, underground, caverns, lava caves, seal chambers

## Setting

The Mines open from the Narrows, beginning with an entrance chamber that serves as the threshold between the surface world and what lies below. From there, the depths unfold in distinct layers: the Upper Mines give way to the Tide Caverns, where water seeps through stone. Deeper still, the Deep Earth presses close. At the lowest levels, the Lava Caves burn with subterranean heat.

Beyond the mined floors, sealed chambers wait — the Water Seal, Earth Seal, Fire Seal, Ruins Seal, and the Void Seal, each with its own elemental presence. Deeper than all of these, the Priestess' Quarters sit in silence, wrapped in void ambience, with soil that somehow still grows things.

## Atmosphere

Each layer has its own weight. The Upper Mines feel like work — stone and ore and the echo of tools. The Tide Caverns are wetter, stranger, the sound of unseen water constant. The Deep Earth is close and heavy. The Lava Caves are hot and bright in a way that feels wrong underground.

The seal chambers are something apart from mining entirely. Their music shifts to something ceremonial and final. The cave ambience persists, but it is no longer the sound of a place people work — it is the sound of a place people built to hold something shut.

## Story role

The Mines are where Mistria's deeper story unfolds. The seals are not natural formations — they were placed deliberately, and what they contain drives the central narrative forward. The Priestess' Quarters beneath everything suggest that someone lived down here once, tending whatever the seals hold.
