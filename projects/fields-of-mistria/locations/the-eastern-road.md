# The Eastern Road

*A forested path east of town, where the sound of sawing carries from the Carpenter's Shop.*

## Keywords

eastern road, the eastern road, carpenter's shop, carpenter, pond

## Setting

A road winds east from Mistria through trees and past a small pond. The Carpenter's Shop stands along the way — Landen and Ryis work here, with a porch workshop and lumber stacked outside. The path is lined with forest, opening occasionally to clearings and the water's edge.

A bench sits by the pond. Children sometimes play nearby while someone supervises from the porch.

## Atmosphere

Dappled and unhurried. The Eastern Road feels like the space between town and wilderness — still close enough to hear Mistria behind you, but green and quiet enough to forget it. The rhythmic sound of carpentry gives the area its character. On rainy days, the pond surface breaks with droplets and the work moves indoors.
