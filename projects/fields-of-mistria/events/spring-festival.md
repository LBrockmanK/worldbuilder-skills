# Spring Festival

*The flower competition — a town-wide effort to make Mistria bloom.*

## Setting

- **When:** Spring, Day 17
- **Where:** Mistria town square
- **Weather:** Clear skies (special weather override)
- **Music:** "Music/Location Tracks/SpringFestival"

## Description

The Spring Festival is Mistria's celebration of renewal. The whole town pitches in for a flower competition, decorating public spaces with arrangements, planters, and garlands. The results are cumulative — the better the town does collectively, the more the square transforms. At its best, barrel planters overflow with blooms, stone planters are replaced with towering floral displays, and the town fountain itself disappears under a cascade of flowers.

Three vendors set up stalls for the day. Maple serves floral teas and seasonal pastries. Nora sells spring-scented souvenirs, decorative planters, and wreaths. Elsie offers flower crowns, floral earrings, and festival outfits.

There is no date mechanic at this festival — the Spring Festival is about the community working together, not romantic moments.

## Lead-up

Decorations begin appearing around town a few days before the festival. The player should be warned in advance so they can gather flowers and prepare their contribution. Spring foraging is at its peak around this time.

## Tone

Bright, cooperative, optimistic. The escalating decoration results give the day a sense of shared accomplishment — the town looks different afterward depending on how well everyone did. It is the most visually transformative event of the year.
