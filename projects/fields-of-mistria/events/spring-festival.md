# Spring Festival

*The flower competition — a town-wide effort to make Mistria bloom.*

## Keywords

spring festival, spring, flower competition, flower festival, spring celebration

## Setting

- **When:** Spring, Day 17
- **Where:** Mistria town square
- **Weather:** Clear skies (special weather override)
- **Music:** Spring Festival

## Description

The Spring Festival is Mistria's celebration of renewal. The whole town pitches in, decorating public spaces with flower arrangements, planters, and garlands. The more everyone contributes, the more the square transforms — at its best, barrel planters overflow with blooms, stone planters give way to towering floral displays, and the town fountain disappears under a cascade of flowers.

Three vendors set up stalls for the day. Maple serves floral teas and seasonal pastries. Nora sells spring-scented souvenirs, decorative planters, and wreaths. Elsie offers flower crowns, floral earrings, and festival outfits.

The Spring Festival is about the community working together — a collective effort, not a personal occasion.

## Lead-up

Decorations begin appearing around town a few days before the festival. Spring foraging is at its peak, and the hills and meadows are full of wildflowers.

## Tone

Bright, cooperative, optimistic. The escalating decorations give the day a sense of shared accomplishment — the town looks different afterward depending on how well everyone pitched in. It is the most visually transformative event of the year.
