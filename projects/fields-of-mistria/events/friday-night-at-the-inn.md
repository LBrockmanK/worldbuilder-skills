# Friday Night at the Inn

*The weekly gathering — the whole town under one roof.*

## Setting

- **When:** Every Friday evening
- **Where:** The Sleeping Dragon Inn
- **Weather:** No override (normal weather)
- **Music:** Inn evening ambiance

## Description

Every Friday night, the town gathers at the Sleeping Dragon Inn. It is the social heartbeat of Mistria — the one night when everyone shows up, regardless of what else is happening.

Josephine sings. Hemlock plays. Stories get told. Drinks flow. People who barely cross paths during the week sit at the same tables and catch up. The bar is crowded, the food is communal, and the mood is warm. This is where gossip spreads, friendships deepen, and the player learns what is really going on in town.

## Lead-up

Friday Night is routine — no advance preparation needed. However, early in the story, a special introductory event welcomes the player to their first Friday Night. Reina invites them, and it serves as a proper introduction to the Inn's social role. After that, it becomes a weekly rhythm.

## Tone

Warm, social, grounded. Friday Night is not a spectacle — it is a ritual. The comfort is in the repetition: the same faces, the same music, the same place. For the player, it is the easiest way to feel like part of the community. For the town, it is the thread that holds everyone together.
