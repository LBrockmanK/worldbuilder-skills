# Friday Night at the Inn

*The weekly gathering — the whole town under one roof.*

## Keywords

friday night, friday night at the inn, inn gathering, weekly gathering, friday

## Setting

- **When:** Every Friday evening
- **Where:** The Sleeping Dragon Inn
- **Weather:** No override (normal weather)
- **Music:** Blazing Hearth

## Description

Every Friday night, the town gathers at the Sleeping Dragon Inn. It is the social heartbeat of Mistria — the one night when everyone shows up, regardless of what else is happening.

Josephine sings. Hemlock plays. Stories get told. Drinks flow. People who barely cross paths during the week sit at the same tables and catch up. The bar is crowded, the food is communal, and the mood is warm. This is where gossip spreads, friendships deepen, and you learn what is really going on in town.

## Lead-up

Friday Night is a weekly rhythm — no advance preparation, just the familiar pull of the Inn once the sun goes down. Newcomers get a proper welcome; Reina usually handles the introductions.

## Tone

Warm, social, grounded. Friday Night is not a spectacle — it is a ritual. The comfort is in the repetition: the same faces, the same music, the same place. It is the easiest way to feel like part of the community. For the town, it is the thread that holds everyone together.
