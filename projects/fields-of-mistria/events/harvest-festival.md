# Harvest Festival

*The fall celebration — a harvest competition followed by a dance under the autumn sky.*

## Setting

- **When:** Fall, Day 10
- **Where:** Mistria town square
- **Weather:** Clear skies (special weather override)
- **Music:** "Music/Location Tracks/HarvestFestivalTheme"

## Description

The Harvest Festival is Mistria's autumn celebration. The town is decorated for the occasion, and the day is split between a harvest competition and a community dance.

The competition is a harvest showcase — participants present what they've grown, and the town judges the results. Placement earns recognition, from honorable mention to first place.

The dance follows. Anyone can invite someone to dance with them, and whether they accept depends on the relationship — a casual acquaintance will politely decline, while a close friend or romantic interest will say yes. A few people are harder to win over than others. The dance is a single shared moment, not individual performances — everyone dances together.

Nora runs a souvenir stall with seasonal treats (candied queen berries, queen berry pie), recipe scrolls, autumn decorations (scarecrow, cornucopia), and harvest-themed outfits.

## Lead-up

A few days before the festival, the town starts preparing — decorations go up, and word gets around. The player should be notified in advance so they can prepare too. Queen berry season peaks around the festival, and the advance notice is a natural prompt to start gathering them.

## Tone

Warm, communal, autumnal. The competition gives the day structure, but the dance is the emotional heart of it. For someone with a romantic interest, the festival is the moment to make a move. For everyone else, it's a good meal and a good time.
