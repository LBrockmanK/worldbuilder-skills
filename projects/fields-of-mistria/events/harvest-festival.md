# Harvest Festival

*The fall celebration — a harvest competition followed by a dance under the autumn sky.*

## Keywords

harvest festival, harvest, fall festival, autumn dance, harvest competition, autumn celebration

## Setting

- **When:** Fall, Day 10
- **Where:** Mistria town square
- **Weather:** Clear skies (special weather override)
- **Music:** "Music/Location Tracks/HarvestFestivalTheme"

## Description

The Harvest Festival is Mistria's autumn celebration. The town square is dressed in fall colors, and the day splits between a harvest competition and a community dance.

The competition is a harvest showcase — participants present their best crops, preserves, and crafted goods, and the town judges the results. Placement earns recognition, from honorable mention to first place.

The dance follows. Anyone can invite someone to dance, and most people are happy to accept from someone they know well — a close friend, a sweetheart, someone who has spent real time with them. A stranger asking out of the blue gets a polite no. A few people are harder to win over than others. Everyone dances together; it is a shared moment, not individual performances.

Nora runs a souvenir stall with seasonal treats (candied queen berries, queen berry pie), recipe scrolls, autumn decorations (scarecrow, cornucopia), and harvest-themed outfits.

## Lead-up

A few days before the festival, decorations go up around the square and people start talking about it. Queen berry season peaks around this time, and the orchards are busy.

## Tone

Warm, communal, autumnal. The competition gives the day structure, but the dance is the emotional heart of it. For someone with a romantic interest, the festival is the moment to make a move. For everyone else, it's a good meal and a good time.
