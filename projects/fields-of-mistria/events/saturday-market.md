# Saturday Market

*The weekly market — traveling vendors and town commerce in the square.*

## Keywords

saturday market, market, weekly market, town market, vendors, market day

## Setting

- **When:** Every Saturday
- **Where:** Mistria town square
- **Weather:** No override (normal weather)
- **Music:** Market Day

## Description

Every Saturday, vendors set up stalls around the town square. The market brings goods and people that are not available the rest of the week — traveling merchants with specialty wares, seasonal ingredients, and crafting supplies that cannot be found in the general store.

The market grows with the town. Early on, a handful of familiar faces run the stalls — Merri, Darcy, Vera, and Louis. As Mistria recovers, new vendors arrive: Stillwell, Taliferro, Wheedle, and Zorel. The stalls look better, the variety expands, and the square feels busier.

## Lead-up

The market is a weekly fixture. Vendors appear every Saturday morning and pack up at the end of the day. Some townspeople save their shopping lists for market day, and a few only come into the square on Saturdays.

## Tone

Practical, lively, evolving. The Saturday Market is less about atmosphere and more about the town feeling alive and growing. Each new vendor is proof that Mistria is recovering. It is the weekly shopping trip for the town, and evidence of progress for anyone watching.
