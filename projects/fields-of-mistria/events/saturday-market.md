# Saturday Market

*The weekly market — traveling vendors and town commerce in the square.*

## Keywords

saturday market, market, weekly market, town market, vendors, market day

## Setting

- **When:** Every Saturday
- **Where:** Mistria town square
- **Weather:** No override (normal weather)
- **Music:** Town daytime music

## Description

Every Saturday, eight vendors set up stalls around the town square. The market brings goods and people that are not available the rest of the week — traveling merchants with specialty wares, seasonal ingredients, and crafting supplies that cannot be found in the general store.

The market starts modest. Early on, a handful of familiar faces run the stalls. As the town is restored and the market is formally upgraded, new vendors arrive and the selection expands. The upgrade is a visible milestone — the stalls look better, the variety grows, and the square feels busier.

A story quest early on introduces the player to the original four vendors: Merri, Darcy, Vera, and Louis. Later, Stillwell, Taliferro, Wheedle, and Zorel join after additional restoration work.

## Lead-up

The market is a weekly fixture — no advance warning needed. Vendors appear every Saturday morning and pack up at the end of the day. Some fetch requests from townsfolk are only available on market days.

## Tone

Practical, lively, evolving. The Saturday Market is less about atmosphere and more about the town feeling alive and growing. Each new vendor is proof that Mistria is recovering. For the player, it is the weekly shopping trip; for the world, it is evidence of progress.
