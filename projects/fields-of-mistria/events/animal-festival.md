# Animal Festival

*The winter showcase — a day to celebrate the animals that make the farm feel alive.*

## Setting

- **When:** Winter, Day 10
- **Where:** Mistria town square
- **Weather:** Clear skies (forced calm)
- **Music:** "Music/Location Tracks/AnimalFestival"

## Description

The Animal Festival is Mistria's lightest celebration. There are no high-stakes competitions and no romantic undercurrents — just townsfolk bringing their animals to show off and enjoy the winter air.

Small animals and large animals are exhibited separately. People walk around admiring each other's livestock, comparing coats and temperaments, and generally making a fuss. Josephine judges the entries, but the atmosphere is more county fair than serious contest.

Nora's stall sells animal-themed treats — chicky hot chocolate, cow donuts — and cosmetic accessories for the animals themselves: hats, collars, monocles, and wings. What is available depends on which animals the player has raised.

## Lead-up

The festival is announced a few days in advance, giving the player time to groom and prepare their animals. Winter is quiet on the farm, and the festival is a welcome break from the cold.

## Tone

Lighthearted, cozy, playful. No pressure, no stakes — just a warm day in a cold season, spent with animals and neighbors. The most relaxed event on the calendar.
