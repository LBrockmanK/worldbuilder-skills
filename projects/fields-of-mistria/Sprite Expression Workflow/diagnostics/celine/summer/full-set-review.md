# Celine Summer full-set review

## Result

The Celine Summer Seiyo-26 sprite set is complete. All expression portraits were generated independently from the approved Summer neutral using the built-in image-generation path and the same fixed soft-muted-sage-to-warm-pale-cream gradient.

## Accepted production candidates

- Neutral: candidate v1, user approved.
- Personality pilot: confident v1, laughing v1, emotional v1, angry v1, and annoyed v2, user approved.
- Remaining set: amused v1, beaming v1, blushing v1, comedic-shock v1, crying v1, embarrassed v1, emotional-shock v1, flirty v1, happy v1, intimate v1, nervous v1, sad v1, shy v1, sleepy-tired v1, smiling v1, stoic v1, surprised v1, upset v1, wary v1, and worried-concerned v1.

## Rejected candidates

- Annoyed v1 was retained in diagnostics. It read as worried or confused and used an unclear single-finger temple gesture. Annoyed v2 corrected both issues and was promoted after user approval.

## Visual QA

- Identity and style: all production portraits preserve Celine's apparent age, face, fair skin, peach-blonde hair, blunt bangs, crown detail, green-grey eyes, delicate warm linework, large-eye construction, soft cel-shading, and luminous Seiyo rendering.
- Outfit: all preserve the sleeveless rose-pink dress, pale interlaced neckline trim, gathered skirt, deep-plum belt, round gold buckle, and hanging belt end. No Spring capelet, high collar, brooch, cuffs, jewelry, or gold ornaments leaked into the set.
- Background and cleanliness: every portrait uses the fixed sage-to-cream gradient through all four edges. No borders, frames, margins, text, logos, signatures, watermarks, props, scenery, or extra characters are present.
- Expression semantics: all 26 labels remain visually distinguishable through gaze, brows, mouth, posture, and Celine-specific contained acting.
- Anatomy: each portrait contains one head, two shoulders, and exactly two arms. Every visible arm traces continuously shoulder-to-elbow-to-wrist-to-hand. Gesture portraits were inspected for natural wrists, plausible five-finger construction, and absence of extra, fused, repeated, or ambiguous limbs. Hands intentionally outside a crop are supported by continuous visible arms.
