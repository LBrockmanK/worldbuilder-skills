# Mara

Mara is an adult lighthouse keeper: practical, dryly funny, fiercely protective, and reluctant to accept help. She speaks plainly and rarely raises her voice. When confident she is grounded rather than showy; when amused, one corner of her mouth lifts before she suppresses it. Anger makes her quieter and more exact. Concern brings her physically closer, while fear makes her completely still.

She has medium copper-brown skin, short thick wavy navy-blue hair with one prominent silver streak at the front, and large amber eyes. Her Spring outfit is a cream sailor-collar blouse with a rust-red neckerchief under a weathered cropped navy coat with brass buttons. She wears a narrow brown utility belt and one small brass compass pendant.
