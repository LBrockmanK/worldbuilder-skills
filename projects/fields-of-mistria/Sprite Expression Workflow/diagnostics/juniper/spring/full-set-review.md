# Juniper Spring full-set review

Juniper's Spring set contains all 26 Seiyo expressions. The approved neutral base was first calibrated against Celine's rendering style, then every expression variant was generated independently using only `sprites/juniper/spring/juniper_spring_neutral.png` as its visual reference.

## Production decisions

- Fixed background: soft muted lavender-violet fading to warm pale gold.
- Corrected identity colors: muted light golden-beige skin and dark desaturated plum-violet hair.
- Shared Seiyo prefix and suffix applied to every prompt.
- Bespoke physicality emphasizes expansive theatrical gestures, hair movement, practiced superiority, and conspicuous stillness when vulnerability breaks through.
- The five-expression pilot (`confident`, `laughing`, `annoyed`, `emotional`, and `angry`) was visually approved before the remaining set was generated.
- Nineteen of the 20 remaining expressions passed first-pass visual review. `embarrassed` was regenerated after a duplicated-arm anatomy defect was identified; candidate v2 uses a simpler separated-arm pose and replaced the defective production file.

## Continuity review

- Adult identity, facial construction, hair mass and color, circlet, jewelry, bodice, sash, nail color, and gradient remain coherent across the set.
- Low-intensity expressions remain distinct: `neutral`, `smiling`, `happy`, and `stoic` differ through gaze, mouth, and posture rather than exaggerated facial changes.
- Vulnerable expressions remove Juniper's performed superiority instead of turning her into a different physical archetype.
- High-energy expressions expand hair and gesture while retaining the same early-2000s visual-novel rendering language.

All approved files are promoted under semantic filenames in `sprites/juniper/spring/` and mapped in its manifest. First-pass candidates remain here for provenance.
