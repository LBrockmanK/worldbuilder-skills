# Juniper Spring personality-range review

All five candidates were generated independently from the approved `juniper_spring_neutral.png`. No Celine portrait, pixel sprite, or cross-character reference was supplied during expression generation.

Shared invariants:

- Seiyo prefix and suffix applied through the Juniper Spring specification
- Approved Juniper neutral used as the sole visual identity and style anchor
- Soft muted lavender-violet to warm pale-gold gradient
- Corrected muted golden-beige skin and desaturated plum-violet hair
- Spring circlet, jewelry, black-and-gold bodice, purple sash, and painted nails preserved

## Candidates

- `juniper_spring_confident_candidate_v1.png` — controlled superiority; open palm and hand-on-hip posture communicate effortless demonstration.
- `juniper_spring_laughing_candidate_v1.png` — signature grand laugh; expanded hair silhouette and lifted hand make this distinctly Juniper.
- `juniper_spring_annoyed_candidate_v1.png` — narrowed sidelong gaze and small dismissive hand motion distinguish irritation from anger.
- `juniper_spring_emotional_candidate_v1.png` — reduced gesture, softened brows, shining eyes, and hand over heart remove her performed superiority.
- `juniper_spring_angry_candidate_v1.png` — genuine dangerous anger expressed through a strongly foreshortened open spell-casting hand; no visible magic effect was added.

All five candidates were visually approved and promoted to the production folder and manifest without revision.
