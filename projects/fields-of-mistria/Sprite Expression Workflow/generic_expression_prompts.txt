Expression Sprite Prompts — Generic (character-agnostic)
==================================================
FORMAT: Change the character's facial expression to [face] and their body language to [body].
        Replace the background with a gradient. Keep the character's appearance,
        clothing, and distinctive features unchanged.

SETUP: Find-replace the two placeholder colors before use:
  [TOP COLOR]    — color at the top of the gradient  (e.g. "deep navy blue", "dark charcoal")
  [BOTTOM COLOR] — color at the bottom               (e.g. "soft lavender", "vivid crimson")
  Pick colors that complement your character's palette.

NOTE: For characters with distinctive features (unusual skin tone, wings, horns, etc.)
      add a character-specific anchor at the end, e.g.:
      "... same [feature] as the original."
