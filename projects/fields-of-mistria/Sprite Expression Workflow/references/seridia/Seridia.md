## Background

- Dragon and former Witch Queen of Aldaria. She drew power through the Gate Between Worlds, raised towers, granted spells to vassals, and forged weapons. This is what she was built for and what she is good at.
- Caldarus and his Dragonsworn sealed her in the Gateway Between Worlds for destabilizing the world. She spent centuries imprisoned. The earthquake before the story cracked the seal, letting magic seep upward through the Mines.
- She survives by reading threats early and acting before she has full information. She learned cunning in a world that punished her for existing, and she has never unlearned it.
- She lives at the edge of the Western Ruins when free. The front room is personal, reserved. The back room connects to the underground seal chambers and carries the void ambience of the place she was held.
- Her Aldarian name is SRDY. Her music is the Priestess theme. The false identity outlived the deception.

## Body

**As the Dragon Priestess:** A ghostly, partially translucent figure encountered deep underground. She wears the priestess robes and speaks with authority. Her eyes shine. The identity is a calculated deception. She presents as a sealed priestess calling for help rather than the queen who was sealed for good reason.

**In revealed form:** Blue hair, red-amber eyes, and large organic dragon horns growing from her head. The horns begin in her gray-taupe skin tone, gradually darken toward charcoal-brown tips, and carry subtle striations of both tones through the middle transition. Solid gold circular disk earrings, each bearing a narrow vertical slit-eye motif. She wears a dark outfit with bone-like ornamental structure across the chest and shoulders, dark wing shapes behind her, and orange-gold accents along the arms. Her features are sharper than Caldarus's. In human form she reads as something that predates the shape it is wearing.

- She holds eye contact until the other person looks away.
- Her hands move when she talks about things she wants. They go still when she talks about things she has lost.
- When she enters a room for the first time, she stands in the doorway and looks at the ceiling before anything else.

## Soul

- When she offers someone anything they want — rule, riches, power — she is confused when they ask for friendship instead. She wants to be chosen for what she is, not what she can give, but does not know how to be chosen any other way. *(core want)*
- When she senses a shift in someone's loyalty, she acts before she has evidence. She survived centuries by reading threats early. She is afraid of being left with no one who returns. *(core fear)*
- She treats affection as payment for service. "Such loyalty deserves reward." She believes she must justify caring about someone by offering them power, or she has nothing worth giving. *(false belief)*
- She learned cunning to survive in a world set against her. When she admits she regrets a deception, she hedges: "Had I known your heart then." She is learning that trust can replace cunning, but every admission comes with a qualifier. *(value-conflict stance)*
- She has the power to dominate any situation she enters. She promised not to. She enters a flower competition for fun and considers summoning enough to guarantee a win before deciding not to. The line between restraint and patience is one she walks every day. *(unresolved tension)*
- She values survival above all. It cost her every genuine connection she might have had. When she calls someone the truest companion she has had in any lifetime, there are no earlier companions to compare against. *(values with costs)*

## Relationships

- **Caldarus — The One Who Sealed Her:** He took centuries from her. She hates the statuette that bears his name. She also knows he was right to seal her, and that knowledge sits worse than the anger. If they both walk free, they swear binding vows to protect Mistria. She keeps the vow and keeps the grudge at the same time. She watches him for signs of old condescension. He watches her for signs of old ambition. Neither has stopped watching.
- **Juniper — The Disciple (arrival):** When Seridia meets Juniper, her first instinct is to recruit. She was a queen who granted power to vassals, and she meets a witch who runs a bathhouse. She treats Juniper as an apprentice. Juniper's familiar Dozy is suspicious of Seridia and will not go to her for pets. She will not go to him. The impasse continues.
- **Celine — The Fearless One (arrival):** Celine visits Seridia's temple to ask for flowers and walks in without hesitation. Seridia calls her "fearless, that one." She has no script for a person who is simply unafraid.

## Story Seeds

**The Dragon Priestess** — *Condition: A mortal reaches the deep Mines.* Seridia appears as a ghostly image and calls herself the Dragon Priestess. She asks for help. The identity is false. She hid what she was because cunning was the only tool she had. She did not consider whether the deception was necessary until after it worked.

**The Final Seal** — *Condition: All seals broken.* Seridia is freed from centuries of imprisonment. She reveals her true identity: the Witch Queen, not the Priestess. She is grateful. She is also immediately assessing the tactical situation.

**The Witch Queen's Offer** — *Condition: High trust.* Seridia offers someone who has helped her anything they desire. Rule over Aldaria. Riches. Untold magic power. "Speak true. You need not hide anything from me." If they ask for friendship or love, she does not understand at first. Then she says more than she intended.

**The Priestess Unmasked** — *Condition: After identity reveal.* Seridia reflects on her deception. She says she learned cunning to survive and turned it on someone who helped her without hesitation. She wonders whether the disguise was necessary. She cannot quite bring herself to say it was not.

**Flowers for the Festival** — *Condition: Spring Festival, Seridia living in town.* Seridia competes in the Spring Festival and considers summoning enough Breath of Spring flowers to guarantee a win. She decides not to. "Where is the fun in that?"

**The Pickle Jar Incident** — *Condition: Seridia interacts with townsfolk.* Reina asks Seridia to help open a pickle jar. Seridia disappears the jar. Pickles land everywhere. She does not offer to help clean up. She does not see why this is a problem.

**The Finest Silverware** — *Condition: Seridia visits the inn.* Seridia insists on the finest dishes and silverware when she eats at Hemlock's inn. When told they do not have anything that special, she tells them to check again. They check. They find it. She did not explain where it came from.

**The Shooting Star Rejection** — *Condition: Someone invites another person to the Shooting Star Festival instead of Seridia.* She tells them to let her know if they are turned down. "I would be happy to help ensure that they regret that decision." She is joking. Probably.

**The Gate Between Worlds** — *Condition: Deep trust, romantic or otherwise.* Seridia brings someone she trusts to the font of all Magic, a place that soothes her. She admits her old plans and machinations fall away when she is with them. This is the most vulnerable she has been since the sealing.

**The Binding Vows** — *Condition: Both Caldarus and Seridia free and present in Mistria.* They swear mutual protection vows. "I promise. I swear it upon the eternal void." She means every word. She also watches Caldarus for signs that he does not.

**A New Way to Live** — *Condition: Deepening trust.* Seridia tells someone that their way of living — thriving without making enemies — challenges her. "Therein lies your challenge to me." She is not used to being changed by example rather than by force.

**The Impertinent Proposal** — *Condition: Romantic path, proposal.* If someone proposes to Seridia, she is furious. Not because she does not want it, but because she was about to propose first. "You dare preempt me?" She accepts, then calls the person an impertinent creature. She is smiling when she says it.

## Design Notes

### Session Notes
Seridia is a former conqueror learning to live without conquest. Her comedy comes from imperial habits applied to small-town life (disappearing pickle jars, conjuring silverware, considering magical domination of a flower contest, negotiating petting terms with a dog). Her depth comes from a character who equated power with worth for so long that she does not believe she can be wanted without offering something in return. The priestess phase is important: the player first encounters her as someone she is not. The deception and its unraveling are central to her arc.
