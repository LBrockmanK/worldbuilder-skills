# Economy — Ranching and Fishing

## Ranching

Animals are kept at Hayden's Farm. The player (Ari) can pet, feed, and breed animals. Animals gain heart levels through daily care (petting, feeding, letting them outside). At higher heart levels, animals produce golden-quality products. Breeding requires heart-shaped treats specific to each species and a minimum heart level of 2.

Animals have seasonal variants (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter) born only in their respective season, plus color tiers ranging from common (tier 1, default unlocked) through rare colors (tiers 2-3), Silver (tier 4), Gold (tier 5), and seasonal (tier 6). Each animal can wear cosmetic accessories.

### Chicken

- **Size:** Small (picked up for petting). Eats seeds.
- **Products:** Hens produce Eggs daily; roosters produce Feathers every 3 days. Golden variants: Golden Egg, Golden Feather.
- **Breeding:** Uses eggs (incubation). Baby chicks mature in 3 days.
- **Variants:** White, Brown, Spotted (default). Red, Yellow, Gray (tier 2). Blue, Purple, Pink (tier 3). Silver, Gold (unique sprites). Seasonal variants.
- **Available from start** (no unlock requirements).

### Duck

- **Size:** Small (picked up). Eats seeds. Can swim.
- **Products:** Females produce Duck Eggs daily; males produce Duck Feathers every 3 days. Golden variants: Golden Duck Egg, Golden Duck Feather.
- **Breeding:** Uses eggs. Ducklings mature in 3 days.
- **Variants:** Mallard, Muscovy, White (default). Mandarin, Ruddy Shelduck, Cayuga (tier 2). Blue, Purple, Pink (tier 3). Silver, Gold, seasonal. Males and females of some variants have distinct colorations.
- **Requires:** Repaired Hayden's Farm.

### Rabbit

- **Size:** Small (picked up). Eats seeds. Faster movement speed than other small animals.
- **Products:** Rabbit Wool every 3 days (both sexes). Golden Rabbit Wool.
- **Breeding:** Live birth. Babies mature in 3 days.
- **Variants:** White, Brown, Black and White (default). Brown Sable, Orange Sable, Black (tier 2). Blue, Purple, Pink (tier 3). Silver, Gold, seasonal.
- **Requires:** Repaired Hayden's Farm.

### Capybara

- **Size:** Small (picked up). Eats seeds. Can swim.
- **Products:** Bristle every 3 days (both sexes). Golden Bristle.
- **Breeding:** Live birth. Babies mature in 3 days.
- **Variants:** Light Brown, Dark Brown, Pale (default). Gray, White, Red (tier 2). Blue, Purple, Pink (tier 3). Gold, Silver, seasonal.
- **Requires:** Repaired Hayden's Farm + Winter Year 1.

### Cow

- **Size:** Large (petted in place). Eats hay. Mountable.
- **Products:** Females produce Cow Milk daily; males produce Bull Horns every 3 days. Golden variants: Golden Cow Milk, Golden Bull Horn.
- **Breeding:** Live birth.
- **Variants:** Brown, Spotted, Black (default). Chocolate, Red, White (tier 2). Blue, Purple, Pink (tier 3). Silver, Gold, seasonal.
- **Available from start.**

### Sheep

- **Size:** Large (petted). Eats hay. Mountable.
- **Products:** Sheep Wool every 3 days (both sexes). Golden Sheep Wool.
- **Breeding:** Live birth.
- **Variants:** White, Black, Brown (default). Black Nosed, Beige, White Nosed (tier 2). Blue, Purple, Pink (tier 3). Silver, Gold, seasonal.
- **Requires:** Repaired Hayden's Farm.

### Horse

- **Size:** Large (petted). Eats hay. Mountable.
- **Products:** Horse Hair every 3 days (both sexes). Golden Horse Hair.
- **Breeding:** Live birth.
- **Variants:** Spotted, Sandy Bay, Champagne (default). Chestnut, Black, White (tier 2). Blue, Purple, Pink (tier 3). Silver, Gold, seasonal.
- **Special variants:** Mistmare and Giant Chicken (white/gold) use the horse slot but are not acquirable through normal breeding.
- **Requires:** Repaired Hayden's Farm.

### Alpaca

- **Size:** Large (petted). Eats hay. Mountable.
- **Products:** Alpaca Wool every 3 days (both sexes). Golden Alpaca Wool.
- **Breeding:** Live birth.
- **Variants:** White, Black, Brown (default). Snowy, Desert, Indigo (tier 2). Blue, Purple, Pink (tier 3). Silver, Gold, seasonal.
- **Requires:** Repaired Hayden's Farm + Winter Year 1.

### Hayden's Farm NPAs (Non-Player Animals)

Hayden's farm has its own resident animals: a Chestnut Horse (male), White Cow (male), Brown Cow (female), and — once the player unlocks them — a Black and White Rabbit, Mallard Duck, White Sheep, Light Brown Capybara, and Black Alpaca.

## Fishing

Fish are caught by rod across four habitats. Each species has a rarity (common, uncommon, rare, legendary) and a size class (small, medium, large, giant). Legendary fish appear only under specific weather conditions and are soulbound until donated.

### Ocean

**All seasons:** Sardine (small), Squid (medium), Pollock (large), Tuna (large), Cod (large, summer/winter), Jellyfish (medium, spring/fall), Octopus (large, summer/winter), Puffer Fish (medium, summer/winter), Red Snapper (large, spring/fall), Sea Bream (medium, spring/fall), Swordfish (large, spring/fall), Bonito (medium, summer/winter).

**Spring:** Anchovy (small), Shrimp (small), Lobster (medium), Mackerel (large, rainy days), Ocean Sunfish (giant, rare).

**Summer:** Crab (small), Dart (small), Stingray (medium), Char (large), Grouper (giant, rare).

**Fall:** Butterfish (small), Mullet (medium), Saury (large), Halibut (large, rainy days), Shark (giant, rare).

**Winter:** Horse Mackerel (small), Sand Lance (small), Sea Bass (medium), King Crab (large, snowy days), Oarfish (giant, rare), Snow Fish (medium, legendary — blizzard days only).

### Pond

**All seasons:** Tetra (small), Koi (medium), Roach (medium), Catfish (medium, uncommon), Turtle (large), Snapping Turtle (large, summer/winter), Snakehead (large, spring/fall), Frog (small, spring/fall), Bullfrog (small, rainy/snowy, summer/winter).

**Spring:** Angel Fish (small), Barb (small), Crucian Carp (medium), Brown Trout (large), Goldfish (small, rare), Cherry Fish (small, legendary — breezy cherry blossom days).

**Summer:** Golden Shiner (small), Lake Chub (small), Brown Bullhead (medium), Sauger (large), Giant Koi (giant, rare).

**Fall:** Killifish (small), Rainbow Trout (medium), White Perch (medium, rare), Bluefish (large, rainy days), Striped Bass (large).

**Winter:** Tilapia (small), Flathead Catfish (medium), Freshwater Eel (medium), Burbot (large, snowy days), Giant Tilapia (giant, uncommon), Alligator Gar (giant, rare).

### River

**All seasons:** Smallmouth Bass (small), Trout (medium), Rock Bass (medium, spring/fall), Goby (medium, uncommon), Pike (large), Salmon (large, spring/fall), Sturgeon (large, uncommon, spring/fall), Chum (large, uncommon, summer/winter), Gar (large, summer/winter).

**Spring:** Blue Gill (small), Chub (small), Carp (medium), Walleye (large, rainy days), Paddlefish (giant, rare).

**Summer:** Loach (small), Minnow (small), Sweetfish (medium), Bream (large), Tarpon (giant, rare), Lightning Fish (medium, legendary — stormy days).

**Fall:** Grayling (small, rainy days), Lamprey (small), Shad (medium), Razorback (medium, rare), Perch (large, rainy days), Leaf Fish (small, legendary — breezy falling leaf days).

**Winter:** Herring (small), Dace (small, snowy days), Bowfish (large), Shadow Bass (medium, rare).

### Deep Woods

**All seasons:** Sunny (small, rainy/snowy days), Forest Perch (medium, rainy/snowy days), Silver Redhorse (medium, uncommon), Lake Trout (large), Muskie (large, uncommon).

### Dive Spots

Forageable underwater items by habitat: Seaweed, Clam, Pearl Clam (ocean); Paper Pondshell, Newt (pond); Freshwater Oyster, Crayfish (river). Beach shells include Spirula Shell, Sand Dollar, Pink Scallop Shell, and Blue Conch Shell.

### Bait Fish

Caught only with specific bait tiers: Massive Minnow, Copper Rockfish (Common Bait); Iron Fish, Silver Squid (Uncommon Bait); Golden Eel (Rare Bait).

### Fish Trap

Passive catches from Fish Traps: Blue Crab (common), Sea Urchin (uncommon), Bait Thief (common), Hake (uncommon), Amberjack (rare).

### Treasure Boxes

Wooden, Copper, Silver, and Golden Treasure Boxes can be fished up. Each is soulbound and contains random loot.
