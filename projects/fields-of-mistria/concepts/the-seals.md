# The Seals

*Ancient chambers beneath the earth, holding something that should not have been disturbed.*

## Premise

Five elemental seal chambers lie buried beneath the Narrows. The earthquake that damaged Mistria also cracked something deeper — old magic is leaking through, and strange things stir underground. The seals are the core of the story's mystery arc, running parallel to the town restoration above.

## The chambers

Each seal is tied to an element and guarded by offerings that reflect the world around it. The progression runs Water, Earth, Fire, Ruins, and finally Void — though in freeform the order can bend to the story's needs. Breaking a seal requires gathering items native to that domain: creatures, minerals, plants, and fish from the surrounding environment, as if the seal demands proof you understand its territory.

Dragon tablets found in the mines tell the history of whoever built these chambers and why. Eiland and Juniper are the primary guides through this mystery — Eiland through scholarship, Juniper through intuition and alchemy.

## What connects

Seridia and Caldarus are tied to the seals' deeper history. The Dragonsworn Tablet on the lowest floor is the final piece — reaching it requires not just breaking seals but building trust with the people who understand what lies below.

## Narrative significance

The seals are the underground counterpart to town restoration. Where restoration rebuilds daily life above ground, the seals uncover what the earthquake woke beneath it. Together they form the two main story thrusts: one communal and practical, one solitary and strange. Progress in each feeds the other — the town gives you the relationships and resources to go deeper, and what you find below gives the town's recovery a larger meaning.
