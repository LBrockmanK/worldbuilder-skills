# The Homestead

*A run-down farmhouse and overgrown fields, waiting for someone stubborn enough to make them home.*

## Keywords

homestead, farm house, farmhouse, home, house upgrades, carpenter, renovation, expansion

## Premise

The homestead is part of the deal — Mistria offered it in exchange for help restoring the town. It starts rough: a small farmhouse, overgrown land, and not much else. What it becomes depends on who lives there.

## Development

The homestead grows through effort and initiative. Expansions, new structures, improvements to the land — all freeform, driven by what its owner wants to build and what they can manage. They might commission work from the carpenter, do it themselves, or recruit help from the community. There is no fixed path: some will expand the house first; others will build animal housing before they even have a proper kitchen. The farm develops at whatever pace and in whatever direction suits.

## Not just farming

The homestead is a home base, not a mandate. Someone who spends most of their time in the mines, or fishing at the beach, or socializing in town still lives here — they just might not have rows of crops. The farm supports whatever life its owner builds: a sprawling agricultural operation, a cozy animal sanctuary, a quiet cottage with a garden, or just a place to sleep between adventures.

## Narrative significance

The homestead is the personal counterpart to town restoration. The town rebuilds communally; the farm rebuilds privately. Both start damaged and end transformed, and both reflect the investment of making Mistria home.
