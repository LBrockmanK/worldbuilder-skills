# Weather

*Rain changes the mood before it changes anything else.*

## Keywords

weather, rain, snow, storm, blizzard, sunny, cloudy, forecast

## Premise

Most days in Mistria are calm — clear skies, open air, people going about their routines. But several days each season bring inclement weather: rain in spring, summer, and fall; snow in winter. Heavy storms roll in less often, darkening the sky and driving people indoors. Rare special weather — cherry blossom breezes, falling leaf winds, blizzards — marks moments that feel like the world itself is putting on a show.

## What weather does

Rain and snow reshape the atmosphere of a scene. Some people stay inside. Outdoor activities shift. The soundscape changes — rain on rooftops, wind through trees, the muffled quiet of snowfall. Certain fish only bite in the rain. Certain creatures only appear in storms. The world feels different on a wet day, and that difference matters as much for mood as for practicality.

Winter replaces rain with snow entirely, giving the cold months their own character.

## Narrative significance

Weather is atmosphere first and condition second. A rainy day at the Inn feels different from a sunny one. A blizzard turns a walk to the mines into something worth describing. Weather gives scenes texture — it is the difference between "they went fishing" and "they went fishing in the rain, and the river was high."
