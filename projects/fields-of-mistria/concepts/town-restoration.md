# Town Restoration

*An earthquake-damaged village rebuilt through labor, community, and stubbornness.*

## Premise

Mistria was hit by an earthquake before the player arrives. Buildings are damaged, infrastructure is broken, and the town has lost its momentum. The player is offered a homestead in exchange for helping restore the community — that exchange is the reason they're here.

Restoration is the backbone of the story. The player isn't just farming for personal gain — materials gathered, money earned, and skills developed feed back into community projects that make Mistria whole again.

## What needs restoring

The following are known restoration targets. In freeform roleplay, the order and approach are flexible — the community works on what it can, when it can.

- **The Bridge** — reconnects town to the eastern road and the Carpenter's Shop beyond. Without it, the eastern part of the region is cut off.
- **The Inn** — restores the town's gathering place. Until the Inn reopens, there's nowhere for the community to come together.
- **Hayden's Barn** — gets Sweetwater Farm fully operational again.
- **The General Store** — expands what Nora can stock. The store functions in its damaged state, but a full restoration unlocks the inventory the town actually needs.
- **The Mill** — opens goods processing. Raw products can be refined into higher-value items (milk into cheese, grain into flour).
- **The Saturday Market** — upgrades the weekly market stalls, bringing in more variety from the traveling vendors.
- **The Bell Tower** — a late-stage project that signals the town's full recovery.

## What changes

Each restoration shifts the world. A repaired building isn't just accessible — it changes daily life. The Inn's restoration creates Friday Night. The bridge lets people move freely. The General Store repair means farmers can actually get the seeds and tools they need.

People's routines change too. NPCs who had nowhere to go start spending time in restored spaces. The town feels more alive with each project completed.

## Narrative significance

Town restoration gives the farming-sim loop a purpose beyond personal wealth. The player is building something communal, and the community responds — showing up to help with repairs, using the restored spaces, shifting their daily patterns. The town at the start of the story and the town at the end are recognizably different places, and the player made that happen.

Town restoration and the breaking of the magic seals beneath the Narrows are the two main story thrusts for the world. Restoration is the communal, above-ground arc — rebuilding daily life. The seals are the mysterious, underground arc — uncovering what the earthquake woke up. They interleave: progress in one feeds motivation for the other.
