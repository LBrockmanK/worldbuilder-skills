# Town Restoration

*An earthquake-damaged village rebuilt through labor, community, and stubbornness.*

## Keywords

restoration, repair, rebuild, earthquake, town repair, community projects, construction

## Premise

Mistria was hit by an earthquake before the newcomer arrives. Buildings are damaged, infrastructure is broken, and the town has lost its momentum. A homestead is offered in exchange for helping restore the community — that exchange is the reason anyone new is here at all.

Restoration is the backbone of the story. The work of the farm — materials gathered, money earned, skills developed — feeds back into community projects that make Mistria whole again.

## What needs restoring

The following are known restoration targets. The order and approach are flexible — the community works on what it can, when it can.

- **The Bridge** — reconnects town to the eastern road and the Carpenter's Shop beyond. Without it, the eastern part of the region is cut off.
- **The Inn** — restores the town's gathering place. Until the Inn reopens, there's nowhere for the community to come together.
- **Hayden's Barn** — gets Sweetwater Farm fully operational again.
- **The General Store** — expands what Nora can stock. The store functions in its damaged state, but a full restoration means the town can finally get the seeds and supplies it actually needs.
- **The Mill** — opens goods processing. Milk becomes cheese, grain becomes flour, fruit becomes preserves.
- **The Saturday Market** — improves the weekly market stalls, bringing in more variety from traveling vendors.
- **The Bell Tower** — a late project that signals the town's full recovery.

## What changes

Each restoration shifts the world. A repaired building changes daily life. The Inn's restoration creates Friday Night. The bridge lets people move freely. Townspeople who had nowhere to go start spending time in restored spaces. The town feels more alive with each project completed.

## Narrative significance

Town restoration gives the daily work of the farm a purpose beyond personal wealth. The community responds — showing up to help with repairs, using the restored spaces, shifting their daily patterns. The town at the start of the story and the town at the end are recognizably different places.

Town restoration and the breaking of the magic seals beneath the Narrows are the two main story arcs. Restoration is the communal, above-ground arc — rebuilding daily life. The seals are the mysterious, underground arc — uncovering what the earthquake woke up. They interleave: what happens in one feeds the other.
