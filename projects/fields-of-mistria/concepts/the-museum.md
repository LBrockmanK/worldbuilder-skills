# The Museum

*A record of everything the region contains — its living world and its buried past.*

## Keywords

museum, collection, donate, donations, exhibits, specimens, wings

## Premise

The museum sits in the Narrows, curated by Errol, and houses four wings: archaeology, insects, fish, and flora. It starts sparse and fills as specimens and artifacts arrive from across the region. Each addition builds the growing record of Mistria's natural and historical wealth.

## The four wings

- **Archaeology** — Artifacts from the mines and dig sites around the region. Aldarian swords, Caldosian busts, prehistoric fossils, dragon relics, ritual objects, and the inexplicable OOPArts that resist all classification. The archaeology wing tells the story of the civilizations that came before.
- **Fish** — Specimens from every habitat: ocean, river, pond, deep woods, and the underground waterways beneath the Narrows. Seasonal catches and legendary fish fill out the displays.
- **Flora** — Crops, wildflowers, and foraged plants from every season, plus the unique species from each mine environment — sweetroot, crystal roses, flame peppers, ethereal grass from the ruins.
- **Insects** — Bugs from every biome and season. Surface species, beach dwellers, deep woods specimens, and the strange insects adapted to mine environments — fire wasps, crystal wing moths, void snails.

## Narrative significance

The museum transforms scattered discoveries into shared knowledge. Every fish caught, plant gathered, bug netted, and artifact unearthed contributes to a growing picture of the region. It gives exploration a cumulative purpose — not just personal gain but a public record. A full museum means understanding Mistria more thoroughly than anyone has in a very long time.
