# Magic

*Rare, practical, and earned — not studied from books but unlocked from the land itself.*

## Keywords

magic, spells, spell, enchantment, sacred light, dragon's breath, summon rain, growth

## Premise

Magic is not commonplace in Mistria. Most people live without it, and the player learning to cast spells is unusual — a consequence of the seal discoveries underground, not formal training. Magic here is tied to the story's progression: each ability surfaces as the seals reveal more of the region's buried history.

## The five spells

- **Full Restore** — A healing wave that mends exhaustion and injury. The kind of magic that keeps you on your feet when the mines go deep.
- **Summon Rain** — Calls a brief storm that waters the fields. Weather bent to the farmer's will.
- **Growth** — Accelerates the growth of plants in a small area, coaxing a season's patience into a moment.
- **Dragon's Breath** — A stream of fire, destructive and brief. The most overtly dangerous magic the player learns.
- **Sacred Light** — A protective, holy illumination that pierces the darkness of the deepest ruins.

## Narrative significance

Magic blurs the line between farming life and underground adventure. The same person who tends crops and feeds chickens can summon rain and breathe fire — but these abilities arrive through story, not study. They mark the player as someone changed by what they found beneath the Narrows, and they give everyday tasks an undercurrent of the extraordinary.
