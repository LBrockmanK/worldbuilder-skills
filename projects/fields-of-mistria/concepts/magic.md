# Magic

*Rare, practical, and earned — not studied from books but drawn from the land itself.*

## Keywords

magic, spells, spell, enchantment, sacred light, dragon's breath, summon rain, growth

## Premise

Magic is not commonplace in Mistria. Most people live without it, and someone learning to cast spells is unusual — a consequence of the seal discoveries underground, not formal training. Magic here is tied to the story: each ability surfaces as the seals reveal more of the region's buried history.

## The five spells

- **Full Restore** — A healing wave that mends exhaustion and injury. The kind of magic that keeps you on your feet when the mines go deep.
- **Summon Rain** — Calls a brief storm that waters the fields. Weather bent to a farmer's will.
- **Growth** — Accelerates the growth of plants in a small area, coaxing a season's patience into a moment.
- **Dragon's Breath** — A stream of fire, destructive and brief. The most overtly dangerous magic anyone in Mistria wields.
- **Sacred Light** — A protective, holy illumination that pierces the darkness of the deepest ruins.

## Narrative significance

Magic blurs the line between farming life and underground discovery. The same person who tends crops and feeds chickens can summon rain and breathe fire — but these abilities arrive through story, not study. They mark someone changed by what they found beneath the Narrows, and they give everyday tasks an undercurrent of the extraordinary.
