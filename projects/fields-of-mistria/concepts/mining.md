# Mining

*Five worlds stacked beneath one entrance, each deeper and stranger than the last.*

## Premise

The mines beneath the Narrows descend through five distinct environments, each with its own ecology, atmosphere, and dangers. What begins as practical resource extraction — ores and building materials — becomes the gateway to the seal mystery and the buried history of the region.

## The five levels

- **Upper Mines** — Rocky tunnels left by previous miners. Rockclods hop between stone piles, saplings lurk in corners, and mushrooms glow faintly in the dark. Copper and iron are found here, alongside old mining equipment and sweetroot growing in the walls.
- **Tide Caverns** — Underground waterways where the air tastes of salt. Enchanterns flicker like lanterns, and the walls are crusted with starlight coral. Seaweed and sea grapes grow in the shallows; strange fish swim in pools no sunlight reaches.
- **Deep Earth** — Crystal formations and compressed stone. Stalagmite creatures erupt from the floor. The flora here is mineral as much as organic — crystal roses, earthshrooms, rockroot. Silver and gold veins thread through the walls.
- **Lava Caves** — Volcanic heat and molten light. Lava cats prowl the ledges, flame spirits drift through the air, and the ground itself is warm. Flame peppers and breath-of-fire flowers grow where nothing should survive. Obsidian and rare gems form in the cooling rock.
- **The Ruins** — Ancient constructed spaces at the deepest level. Griffin statues animate, flying tomes circle overhead, and the architecture speaks of a civilization that understood the seals. The artifacts here are the most significant — and the most unsettling.

## Narrative significance

Mining serves two purposes that interleave. Practically, it supplies the ores, gems, and materials that fuel the town's restoration and the player's livelihood. Narratively, it is the path to the seals and the buried past. Each level descended is another step into a world that existed long before Mistria, and the creatures, plants, and artifacts found there are pieces of a story the surface has forgotten.
