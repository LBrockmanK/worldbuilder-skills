# Farming

*Seasonal labor, patient growth, and the economic backbone of a homestead.*

## Premise

The player came to Mistria for a homestead, and the farm is what makes that homestead work. Crops follow the seasons — each one has its own roster of plants that will grow, and planting the wrong thing at the wrong time means nothing comes up. Fruit trees take longer but bear harvests across multiple seasons once established.

## How it works

The farm's rhythm follows the calendar. Spring brings root vegetables and early fruits. Summer favors heat-loving crops. Fall is harvest season for hardier plants. Winter has its own cold-weather varieties. Greenhouses bypass seasonal constraints, letting a determined farmer grow anything year-round.

The mill processes raw goods into refined products — grain into flour, milk into cheese, fruit into preserves. What comes off the farm feeds the household, supplies the community, and funds restoration projects.

## Narrative significance

Farming is the player's economic foundation and daily anchor. It gives every morning a purpose and every season a shape. The crops shipped and materials gathered feed directly into town restoration — the farm is not separate from community life but central to it. What grows on that land is how Mistria gets rebuilt.
