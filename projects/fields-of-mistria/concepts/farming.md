# Farming

*Seasonal labor, patient growth, and the work that holds a homestead together.*

## Keywords

farming, crops, harvest, planting, seeds, greenhouse, farm, agriculture

## Premise

The farm is what makes the homestead work. Crops follow the seasons — each one has its own roster of plants that will grow, and planting the wrong thing at the wrong time means nothing comes up. Fruit trees take longer but bear harvests across multiple seasons once established.

## The rhythm of the land

Spring brings root vegetables and early fruits. Summer favors heat-loving crops. Fall is harvest season for hardier plants. Winter has its own cold-weather varieties. Greenhouses shelter plants from the seasons, letting a determined farmer grow anything year-round.

The mill processes raw goods into refined products — grain into flour, milk into cheese, fruit into preserves. What comes off the farm feeds the household, supplies the community, and funds restoration projects.

## Narrative significance

Farming is the daily anchor of life in Mistria. It gives every morning a purpose and every season a shape. The crops harvested and materials gathered feed directly into town restoration — the farm is not separate from community life but central to it. What grows on that land is how Mistria gets rebuilt.
