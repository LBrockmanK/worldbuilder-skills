# Fishing

*Every body of water holds something different, and what bites depends on when you cast.*

## Premise

Mistria's waterways are rich and varied. Rivers, ponds, the ocean, deep woods pools, and underground caverns each hold their own populations of fish. What you can catch shifts with the seasons, the weather, and the time of day. Fishing is part livelihood, part exploration, part patience.

## Where and how

The beach and coastal waters offer ocean species — shrimp and anchovies in spring, crabs in summer, sharks in fall, king crab in winter storms. Rivers and ponds rotate their own seasonal populations. The Deep Woods holds reclusive freshwater species that only appear in rain or snow. Underground waterways in the mines shelter strange cave-adapted fish found nowhere else.

Rod fishing is the most common method. Diving lets you forage underwater — clams, crayfish, seaweed. Fish traps work passively, catching whatever wanders in overnight. Legendary fish appear only under specific rare conditions: a blizzard, a thunderstorm, cherry blossoms on the wind.

## Narrative significance

Fishing gives every body of water in Mistria a reason to visit. The seasonal rotation means familiar spots yield different catches throughout the year. Weather-dependent fish reward paying attention to the world's rhythms. And the underground fish — cave sharks, sapphire bettas, firesail fish from the lava caves — tie fishing into the mine exploration arc, making even a quiet hobby part of the larger story.
