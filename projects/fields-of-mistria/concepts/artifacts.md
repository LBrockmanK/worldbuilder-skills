# Artifacts

*Curiosities alone, but together they tell the story of what happened underground.*

## Keywords

artifacts, relics, archaeology, dig sites, ancient objects, ooparts, donations

## Premise

The mines beneath the Narrows are layered with history. Ancient objects surface at every depth — mining picks left by forgotten workers, coral formations from underground tides, crystals shaped by pressure and time, fire-forged relics from volcanic depths, and strange items from the deepest ruins that defy easy explanation. Each artifact belongs to the environment that produced it.

## What they reveal

Individually, an artifact is a curiosity — a tidestone, a warm rock, a miner's old helmet. Collectively, they sketch the outline of civilizations and events that preceded Mistria. The archaeology wing of the museum organizes them into sets: Aldarian, Caldosian, Alda, Ancient, Dragon, Prehistoric, and stranger collections — ritual objects, mist-touched relics, out-of-place items that nobody can fully explain.

Different sites across the region yield their own finds. The Narrows produces Aldarian relics. The Eastern Road turns up Caldosian remnants. The Deep Woods holds petrified wood and crystal apples. The beach gives up fossils from before recorded history.

## Narrative significance

Artifacts give the mines a purpose beyond raw materials. Every depth descended is another layer of the past uncovered. Bringing them to the museum builds a visible record of the region's buried history — and the deeper you go, the stranger and more significant the objects become. The OOPArts at the bottom resist all categories, hinting that the full story may be older and stranger than anyone in Mistria suspects.
