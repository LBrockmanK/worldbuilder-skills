# Artifacts

*Curiosities alone, but together they tell the story of what happened underground.*

## Keywords

artifacts, relics, archaeology, dig sites, ancient objects, ooparts, aldarian, caldosian, witch queen

## Premise

The mines beneath the Narrows are layered with history. Ancient objects surface at every depth — and each belongs to the era and environment that produced it. Individually, an artifact is a curiosity. Collectively, they sketch the outline of civilizations and ages that preceded Mistria.

## The ages of the land

The archaeology wing of the museum organizes artifacts into eras, and the eras tell a story:

**Prehistoric** — from before recorded memory. Fossils, trilobites, dinosaur bones, amber with insects trapped inside, and a meteorite. The land itself remembers things no living person could.

**Alda (the Dark Ages)** — the earliest known civilization. Bronze swords, clay pots, feather pendants, mural tablets. Bronze-age technology and craftsmanship — the foundations that everything after was built on.

**Ancient (the Witch Queen's reign)** — a period of magic and rule. Crystal goblets, gold coins, horn circlets, a royal scepter. Whoever the Witch Queen was, she left behind objects of power and refinement. This era connects to the seals and whatever was bound beneath the Narrows.

**Caldosian Empire** — an imperial power that rose after the Ancient period. An emperor's bust, breastplates, drinking horns, ceremonial statuettes. A military and political force that shaped the region.

**Aldarian Kingdom** — the kingdom that gives its name to the wider land. Swords, war banners, gauntlets, and the crown of Aldaria itself. The relationship between Aldaria and present-day Mistria is part of the region's unresolved history.

**Dragon** — artifacts from the deepest ruins. Scales, claws, hardened essence, a forged bracelet, and a pact tablet. The pact tablet suggests a treaty between the dragon-kind and one of the civilizations above — a bargain struck and possibly broken. These connect directly to Caldarus, Seridia, and the seal mystery.

## Beyond the timeline

Some artifacts resist any era:

- **Ritual objects** — found in underground chambers, from ceremonies whose purpose is unclear
- **Mist relics** — found in the strange mist spots that appear across the region, touched by something liminal
- **OOPArts** — "out of place artifacts" that defy all categories: a muttering cube, a weightless stone, a completely wrong map, a black tablet, an unknown dragon statuette. They hint that the full story is older and stranger than the museum's timeline can contain

## Where they surface

Different depths and sites yield different eras. The upper mines produce tools and everyday objects. The tide caverns hold coral and water-touched relics. The deep earth gives up crystals and geological oddities. The lava caves yield fire-forged objects. And the ruins at the bottom — the oldest and strangest — hold the dragon artifacts and the things that don't fit anywhere else.

The farm, the deep woods, the beach, and the waterways all have their own finds too. History is buried everywhere in this region, not just underground.
