# Pipeline Quality Trial — Condition A Run 2

## PART 1: Synthesized Soul Entries

1. She lets sentences dissolve before they reach their point, creating gaps that other people rush to fill. She absorbs their disclosures without reaction and deploys them later — a detail returned at the exact moment it carries weight, proving the drifting attention was intake disguised as absence. The only genuine part of the act is her refusal to stand up when sitting will do.

2. She attributes her continued presence to the tree's shade and its proximity to the river. Neither feature is unique to this location. She has been through seasons where the tree offered nothing and the river froze, and she stayed without altering the explanation. Vesper stopped asking years ago. The question that remains is not why she stays — it is why she cannot say why.

3. She consumes individuals she has known by name and surveys the village's functioning within the same hour, checking whether the loss created a gap that needs filling. The survey is habitual and precise — she knows which roles are occupied, which supply chains are running, which absences would ripple. The juxtaposition of eating a person and auditing their replacement pipeline does not register as contradictory to her because she has built a model in which the pipeline is the entity and the person was raw material.

4. A slow rearrangement of coils narrows the path beside her to a corridor that ends near her jaw. She begins describing something she saw that morning, or asks about someone's family — anything that requires the listener to stay close and lean in. The topic varies. The geometry does not. Each encounter is unique enough that no one spots the template, and the people who might have compared notes are no longer available to compare.

5. When the audience disappears, the stalling speech and loose posture vanish in the same instant. What replaces them is direct, physical, and efficient: a tail that immobilizes before the target can reorient, followed by silence and cleanup. She returns to her public configuration — slouched, blinking slowly, mid-yawn — before anyone walks through. The resurrection erases the encounter from the only witness who saw both versions.

6. Years ago she stood before a community she had threatened and discovered that her body had already resolved the question her voice had posed. The muscles did not engage. The stance softened into something that felt like exhaling after holding breath she did not know she was holding. What she learned was not that she was incapable — she was and is capable — but that destroying a community she knew was something she did not want, and that the wanting mattered more than the capability. She has spent every year since arranging her life to avoid re-encountering that lesson.

7. Her intake avoids people whose removal would disrupt something downstream: the person who maintains the well mechanism, the one who distributes grain, the one whose workshop three other trades depend on. She takes from positions the village backfills quickly and without visible strain. She describes this as path-of-least-resistance behavior. The path she follows requires a working knowledge of the village's dependency structure that exceeds what any other resident holds, including the people who officially manage it.

8. A threat breaches the village perimeter and she unfolds into motion that seems borrowed from a different creature — fast, enormous, conclusive. The threat is neutralized in the time it takes bystanders to process what is happening. She returns to rest. Someone approaches with gratitude forming on their face. She talks over them — a complaint, an irrelevant observation, a request to keep the noise down next time. The block is instant and total. No acknowledgment lands.

9. She carries a registry of permanent deaths she would deny maintaining. When one occurs, she vacates her preferred location and settles somewhere less comfortable — a colder spot, a louder corner, a place with worse sightlines. She stays until she does not. She never explains the move. She never speaks the name. If someone else speaks it, she waits for the syllables to end with the stillness of someone letting a wave pass over them.

10. Rado Stary was there, accessible, and she chose not to act. She told herself that impaired prey lacks value. Her record contradicts this — she has taken humans who were sleeping, distracted, or otherwise uncomprehending. The distinction with Rado is not his awareness but his suffering. She tracked it, categorized it, and exempted him on the basis of a judgment about one person's inner experience — an act of individual recognition that her operating model says she does not perform.

## PART 2: Variant Spreads

### Entry 1
**Variant A:** She drops a sentence before its conclusion and waits. People fill the gap, talking past their own boundaries. She takes it all in without changing expression. Days later, she inserts one of their unguarded details into a different conversation — perfectly timed, impossible to have guessed.
**Variant B:** The performed inattention gives her total social intelligence at the cost of being excluded from every decision. The village treats her as an unthinking presence, which means no one filters around her. She hears everything. She is consulted on nothing. The asymmetry suits her.
**Variant C:** An observer sees drowsiness and scattered thoughts. The actual process: real-time cataloguing of disclosures, cross-referencing against prior interactions, and storage for deployment at useful moments. The laziness that keeps her horizontal is real. The vacancy that makes her seem absent is manufactured.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 2
**Variant A:** "The shade," she says, gesturing upward. The tree is currently leafless and the shade nonexistent. She does not correct the discrepancy. She has never browsed alternatives.
**Variant B:** The excuse erodes with every season that disproves it, but abandoning it would mean producing a replacement, and every honest replacement sounds like attachment. She pays the cost of a thinning cover story rather than the cost of an honest one.
**Variant C:** Newcomers accept the tree explanation at face value. Long-term residents — Vesper especially — hear it as a reflex rather than a reason. The gap between the spoken justification and the observable behavior has widened to the point where the excuse functions as deflection rather than explanation, and everyone present knows it except, nominally, her.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 3
**Variant A:** She eats someone whose name she used yesterday and checks the grain distribution that afternoon. The check is routine — volumes, staffing, flow. The person she consumed existed inside a function; the function is what she tracks. Whether the person filling it is the same one as last week does not register in the model.
**Variant B:** Treating the village as a system makes predation frictionless, but it creates a blindspot: the system she maintains with such care is composed entirely of individuals whose individuality she denies. The care is visible in her actions — the precision of her monitoring, the consistency of her maintenance. The denial is visible in her framing. Both are genuine. Neither can survive scrutiny of the other.
**Variant C:** What the village sees: intermittent, purposeless predation. What she is doing: running population management with the sophistication of a trained administrator. What neither she nor the village sees clearly: the management behavior implies a stake in the outcome that is personal, not practical, and the predation behavior requires the denial of exactly that stake.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 4
**Variant A:** Her coils redirect foot traffic toward her mouth as she opens a conversation about something immediate — a local event, a shared acquaintance. The human approaches to hear better. The topic is always specific enough to seem spontaneous, general enough to suit any target.
**Variant B:** The method succeeds because each instance is socially unremarkable — a creature chatting, a person walking closer. The aggregation is remarkable: a pattern of encounters that reliably route humans to the same physical endpoint. The pattern is invisible at the individual level and only visible across time, which is a time scale no individual target survives to accumulate.
**Variant C:** At ground level: a sociable creature and a willing listener. At structural level: a repeating spatial configuration where conversation serves as a guidance system and the endpoint is predetermined. The human participates voluntarily in every step, which is the point. The voluntariness is what makes the mechanism invisible and what makes it work.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 5
**Variant A:** Alone, she moves without preamble. The idle chatter and drifting eye contact switch off. Her tail reaches the person before they process the change. The entire encounter — from first movement to reset — fits inside a window shorter than most of her public sentences.
**Variant B:** The gap between the two modes is the clearest evidence that the public mode is performed rather than real. Performing scattered and slow requires continuous effort; the private mode is what remains when that effort is withdrawn. Predation is her rest state. Sociability is her work state.
**Variant C:** A hypothetical witness to both versions would see two incompatible creatures: one rambling, drowsy, and physically passive; one silent, precise, and explosive. The incompatibility is the design. The public version exists to make the private version unimaginable, and the private version exists only when no public version is needed.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 6
**Variant A:** She threatened a community and watched them refuse her. Standing there with the ability to make good on every word, she felt her body release the strike readiness like someone putting down a weight they did not know they were holding. She departed before dawn. The relief was the problem — it was her body confirming that she did not want what she had just demanded the right to do.
**Variant B:** The bluff cost her the narrative that she passed through communities without accruing attachment. The involuntary physical decompression and the relief that followed it were data she could not explain away. She responded by never testing the boundary again, which preserved the uncertainty about whether she could cross it — an uncertainty she prefers to the certainty the bluff provided.
**Variant C:** The community experienced a threat that failed. She experienced an involuntary disclosure: her body voted before her mind decided, and it voted for preservation. The relief was the endorsement — not reluctant, not delayed, but immediate, as though her nervous system had been waiting for the permission to stand down. She treats this as an incident rather than evidence because the evidence implies a connection to communities she insists does not exist.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 7
**Variant A:** She avoids people who anchor critical functions — the ones whose absence would halt something downstream. Her targets are drawn from roles the village fills again within days, and the selection is consistent enough across years to constitute a policy she has never written down.
**Variant B:** The policy requires her to map the village's internal dependencies in real time — tracking who feeds into what, which roles have backup, which ones are singular. This mapping is the work of someone invested in the village's operational continuity, which is the work of a stakeholder. She produces the work and refuses the title.
**Variant C:** Random predation would occasionally hit a critical node. Hers never does. The statistical improbability of this record is invisible to the village because no one is running the analysis, but it implies an active selection process that weighs each potential target against the community's structural resilience. She does not describe it this way. She says she picks whoever is easiest.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 8
**Variant A:** The village faces a threat and she responds with force that snaps structures and shakes the ground. It ends. She resettles and begins a sentence about something unrelated — the temperature, a noise that annoyed her earlier — before the nearest person has finished processing the intervention.
**Variant B:** By deflecting every acknowledgment, she has built a social position where her defensive capacity is known but never formalized. This protects her self-narrative as a freeloading guest, at the cost of never being seen as what she functionally is: the village's most decisive protector.
**Variant C:** The deflection pattern is legible as policy rather than personality once you see it across multiple incidents. She prepares the complaint or the redirect during the engagement itself. The post-combat social management is as tactical as the combat. She is fighting two engagements: one physical, one reputational. She wins both.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 9
**Variant A:** A permanent death sends her away from her preferred spot. She finds a different location — never one she has used before — and occupies it without sleeping, without eating, without conversation. The absence of the deceased is not mentioned. If their name arises, she holds still and lets it pass without response.
**Variant B:** She processes permanent loss through displacement — giving up comfort for a duration that correlates with connection. The correlation is the tell: she claims not to differentiate individuals, but the length of her withdrawal differentiates precisely. A person she barely knew moves her for a day. A person she saw often moves her for a week. She would deny measuring.
**Variant C:** The pattern is: someone dies permanently, she moves, she stays, she returns. The village reads these as restless episodes. The timing tells a different story — each episode begins within hours of a specific death and ends on no visible external trigger. She is mourning in a register the village does not recognize as mourning because it contains no words, no tears, and no names.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 10
**Variant A:** She had Rado available and did not act. Her reason — impaired prey lacks sport — is inconsistent with her record of taking unaware or incapacitated humans. The real variable is his suffering: she assessed it, recognized it as a specific and ongoing condition, and decided against adding to it.
**Variant B:** The exemption is small but structurally significant: it means she evaluated one human's particular experience and made a decision based on that evaluation. Her framework holds that individual experience is irrelevant to her calculus. Rado's exemption proves the framework has a hole she is not repairing.
**Variant C:** The village might assume proximity or timing explain why Rado has not been targeted. In reality, the decision was deliberate and singular. She watches him with attention calibrated to him specifically — his state, his distress, his patterns — and this watching is qualitatively different from the systemic scanning she gives the village. She is tracking a person, not a function. The distinction is the one her worldview cannot accommodate.

> Fail-check: PASS.
