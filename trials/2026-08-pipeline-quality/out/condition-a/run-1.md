# Pipeline Quality Trial — Condition A Run 1

## PART 1: Synthesized Soul Entries

1. Her voice drifts sideways through a sentence, losing its thread in a way that invites people to fill the silence. They do, offering more than they planned. She collects it all. Later, she surfaces a buried detail at the exact moment it matters — and the person who shared it realizes too late that the drowsy, distracted creature was running an intake the entire time.

2. "The tree is warm," she says when anyone asks about her presence. The tree drops its foliage in autumn and offers no warmth for months. She has been through six of those autumns without packing. When Vesper raises an eyebrow at the excuse, she stretches and lets the conversation die. The real answer is one she has not practiced saying.

3. She monitors the village's operational rhythm the way someone monitors weather — noting which functions are staffed, which outputs are flowing, which gaps might cascade. She will consume a person she traded stories with at noon and inspect the grain stores by evening. The person was a component. The grain stores are a system. She tends the system and discards the components and has organized her entire self-understanding around the claim that this distinction is clean.

4. Her body rearranges until the natural walking path routes past her mouth. She begins an anecdote — something local, something just compelling enough. The human steps closer to catch the quiet part. The whole interaction feels like happenstance: a bored creature making conversation, a person being polite. The geometry tells a different story, but the geometry is only visible from above.

5. Remove the audience and the performance shuts off like a switch. No rambling. No distraction. Her tail closes the distance before the human's posture adjusts. The efficiency is clinical and sits so far from her public behavior that the two versions seem like different creatures. The human does not remember the encounter. She is already back in position, settled and yawning, before anyone passes through again.

6. She entered a settlement years ago and announced she would end it if they did not accommodate her. They held their ground. She stood at full extension with every capacity to make good on the threat and felt her own musculature refuse — not freeze, not hesitate, but actively release, as though her body had already decided. The wave of relief that followed was the real damage: it proved she was bound to a collective she had been treating as a bargaining chip. She left before sunrise and has not attempted that kind of ultimatum since.

7. Her intake follows an invisible map: she avoids people whose daily function anchors a chain of dependency and takes from the edges, where absence closes like water over a dropped stone. She frames this as energy conservation — less disruption, less attention. The pattern is too precise and too consistent for laziness. It is stewardship wearing predation's face, and she refuses to look at it from that angle.

8. An external threat hits the village and she becomes unrecognizable — coils driving forward, mass deployed with force that splinters structures. The threat ends in seconds. She settles back, resumes her resting position, and is already complaining about the debris before anyone can form a sentence of thanks. The speed of the deflection matches the speed of the strike; both are rehearsed.

9. When someone does not come back — a death without resurrection — she breaks her routine. She moves to a different part of the village, somewhere she never usually rests, and stays there. She does not sleep. She does not eat. She does not mention the name. If someone says it near her, she holds still until the sound passes. The duration of the displacement correlates with how long she knew them — a metric she maintains for people she claims to view as interchangeable.

10. She decided not to take Rado Stary once, in a moment when nothing prevented it. Her stated reason — that prey without comprehension offers nothing — contradicts her own history; she has taken unconscious humans without complaint. The actual pattern: she watches him with individual-specific attention, tracking his condition the way she tracks the village's systems, except this tracking has no systemic justification. It is care for a particular person, which is the one thing her operating framework says cannot exist.

## PART 2: Variant Spreads

### Entry 1
**Variant A:** Her speech wanders and stalls, pulling people into filling the gaps. She lets them talk. When she surfaces a detail from the conversation days later, placed with surgical precision, the talker realizes the wandering was collection.
**Variant B:** The performance of inattention means no one guards their words around her, which gives her comprehensive social intelligence at the cost of never being consulted on anything. She holds more information about the village's internal dynamics than anyone — and none of it was given to her intentionally.
**Variant C:** Observers see a creature who cannot track a sentence. The creature is running a continuous intake, sorting disclosures by relevance, and building a social model no one asked her to build. The gap between the observed vacancy and the actual processing is the tool. The only unperformed part is the reluctance to stand up.

> Fail-check: PASS. Variants use substantially different structure and vocabulary.

### Entry 2
**Variant A:** She names the tree when asked why she remains. "Good shade. Warm bark." The tree is bare half the year. She has stayed through those months without comment or departure.
**Variant B:** The cover story costs her the chance to be honest about attachment — each repetition of "the tree" strengthens a wall between what she says and what keeps her anchored. Vesper has heard the excuse enough times to stop believing it but not enough to challenge it.
**Variant C:** A newcomer hears a simple creature staying for a simple reason. Vesper hears a rehearsed line delivered with enough ease to seem unrehearsed. The difference between the two audiences is years of accumulated evidence that the tree is not the reason.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 3
**Variant A:** She tracks staffing, production output, and resource flows the way someone scans for weather changes. A human she ate yesterday is already replaced in her operational model by whoever fills the function. She checks the market stalls the same afternoon.
**Variant B:** The systemic view lets her eat without friction — no individual matters — but it generates a contradiction she does not address: the system she is tending with such precision is made of individuals whose individual deaths she causes. The care is genuine. The framing that lets the care coexist with the predation is what she cannot examine.
**Variant C:** The village sees sporadic, seemingly random predation. The actual pattern is filtered through an operational model: she takes from positions the community can absorb and preserves positions it cannot. This is management. She calls it appetite. The village does not see the model. She does not see the care.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 4
**Variant A:** She repositions her mass until the walkway narrows beside her face, then opens a conversation about something trivial — local gossip, a sound she heard. The human draws closer to engage. The conversation is the bait; the geometry is the mechanism; the combination never quite looks intentional.
**Variant B:** The method works because it is never the same twice and never feels urgent. She builds a comfortable, rambling social space around a precise physical arrangement, and the people who step into it experience friendliness right up to the moment the interaction changes.
**Variant C:** Ground-level: a sociable creature telling stories. Overhead: coils arranged to funnel foot traffic through a narrowing channel that terminates at her mouth. The discrepancy between the two views is the technique. No one sees both views simultaneously.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 5
**Variant A:** Without an audience, everything changes. She does not speak. Her tail pins the person in a motion so practiced it looks reflexive. The whole event takes seconds. She resettles before any evidence remains.
**Variant B:** The private version reveals the cost of the public one — maintaining the persona requires continuous energy that drops away the moment there is no one to perform for. The efficiency of the unwitnessed version suggests the public version is the laborious one, not the predation.
**Variant C:** The village's Kallya stumbles through words and seems to forget her point. The unwitnessed Kallya is silent, fast, and mechanically precise. The human who experiences the second version returns from resurrection with no memory. The two versions never appear in the same scene.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 6
**Variant A:** She made the threat at full height, coils spread wide, voice flat. They did not comply. She stood there for a long moment with every physical means to follow through and felt her body decompress — muscles releasing, tension draining, the preparedness dissolving into something that felt like coming home after being away too long. She was gone by morning.
**Variant B:** The bluff's failure took from her the framework she used to navigate communities: that she moved through them without tethering. The involuntary relief proved the tether existed. She has been building her self-narrative around that proof's absence ever since, and the structure requires constant maintenance.
**Variant C:** The settlement saw a threat that backed down — a creature that talked big and failed to deliver. Inside, she experienced a discovery: her body's refusal was not reluctance but resolution, and the relief that chased it was the body confirming a decision the mind had not been consulted on. She did not lose a bluff. She found an edge she did not know she had.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 7
**Variant A:** She takes from the periphery and avoids the center. The bakers, well-tenders, and builders who anchor community function are off her informal list — not protected, she would say, just not worth the disruption. The list exists. Its consistency says more than her explanation.
**Variant B:** Maintaining the boundary forces her to understand the village's dependency chains in detail — who connects to what, which absences cascade. The knowledge is indistinguishable from that of a caretaker. She acquires it like a predator, uses it like a steward, and calls it convenience.
**Variant C:** From the outside, her predation looks opportunistic and random. From a statistical view, it is precisely shaped to avoid structural damage. She targets roles the village can backfill quickly and avoids roles it cannot. The precision implies a model of the village's architecture that is too detailed for someone who claims the community is just a convenient place to rest.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 8
**Variant A:** A threat enters the village and she goes from resting to lethal in a transition so fast it seems like a cut in time. The threat breaks. She resettles. By the time someone approaches to say something, she is mid-sentence about how the racket interrupted a perfectly good nap.
**Variant B:** The deflection pattern is so consistent it has trained the village out of thanking her, which preserves exactly the role she wants: present without being needed, effective without being essential. The cost is that her actual function in the community remains unacknowledged, which is a loneliness she has chosen and maintains.
**Variant C:** What a bystander witnesses: an eruption of force, a resolved situation, and a creature already talking about the weather. What the bystander does not see: the deflection is loaded before the fight starts. She knows she will need to redirect attention afterward and has the complaint ready before impact. The combat is instinct. The social management is strategy.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 9
**Variant A:** After a permanent death, she stops mid-activity and leaves her spot. She picks somewhere else in the village — never her preferred location — and stays without moving for a duration that tracks with familiarity. She never references the person. She never explains the move.
**Variant B:** The displacement is her only legible grief, and it is legible only to someone who tracks both her movements and the village's permanent death records. She has made that tracking impossible by never acknowledging either data point. The grief exists; its expression is designed to be unreadable.
**Variant C:** The village sees her relocate occasionally and assumes restlessness. The actual trigger is always the same: a death without return. The new spot is always inferior — less shade, less warmth, less comfort — as though comfort is the thing she is denying herself. She stays until something external breaks the vigil, then returns to her usual spot without comment.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 10
**Variant A:** She bypassed Rado when taking him would have been effortless. She offered herself the rationale that prey unaware of its situation provides nothing worthwhile. She has consumed sleeping humans — less aware than Rado — many times.
**Variant B:** The exemption sits unpatched in her framework. If she does not track individuals, she cannot make individual exemptions. Rado's exemption means she tracked him — his condition, his suffering, his particular vulnerability — and decided that one person's specific distress warranted suspension of her operating rules. This is care, indexed to an individual. It is the thing she says does not happen.
**Variant C:** An observer would think she simply has not encountered the right opportunity with Rado. In reality, the opportunity was there and she walked it off. The way she looks at him afterward — the same steady, cataloguing attention she gives the village — is different in one way: it is tuned to his state, not his function. She notices when he is worse. She does not act. But she notices.

> Fail-check: PASS.
