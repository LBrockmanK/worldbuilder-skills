## PART 1: Synthesized Soul Entries

1. Her voice dissolves into nothing mid-thought, pulling people into filling the vacuum with more than they intended. She takes in every word without showing it. When she places one of their unguarded details into a later conversation — timed exactly, impossible to fabricate — the source realizes the drowsy creature was processing the entire time. The performed emptiness is airtight. The physical reluctance to move is the one real thing inside it.

2. She names the tree when asked. The tree has been leafless and cold more seasons than she has been here. She stayed through every one without altering the answer or looking elsewhere. The response is not a lie — it is a sealed container holding a question she will not open: what she found in this place that she has not found before, and what saying so would commit her to.

3. She runs a continuous survey of the village — production, staffing, supply — with the consistency of someone managing a resource they depend on. She eats a person she recognized that morning and inspects whether their function is being filled that evening. The function matters. The person did not — or so the framework holds. The framework's integrity requires not noticing that monitoring functions means tracking the individuals who perform them, and that the tracking is too granular for someone who does not differentiate.

4. Her mass redistributes until the walkway funnels past her jaw. She opens a topic — something specific to the listener, something that holds them in place. The conversation is real. The architecture is predatory. The listener experiences one and does not perceive the other. She never repeats a pretext, so the pattern has no cumulative witnesses.

5. Alone, the stumbling speech and soft posture switch off entirely. Her tail closes the gap in a motion that contains no preamble. The speed and directness sit so far from her public behavior that they belong to a different creature. She resettles before anyone else arrives. The person returns from resurrection remembering nothing. The two versions of her have never coexisted in a surviving witness.

6. She stood at full extension before a community she had threatened, felt her musculature release the strike without her choosing to release it, and experienced an immediate wave of relief. The relief was the data: her body confirming, ahead of her cognition, that she valued the collective's continuation. She left that night. She has spent every subsequent year arranging her circumstances so that no situation forces her back to that border, because the border points at an attachment she has decided not to acknowledge.

7. Her targets come from the village's periphery — roles replaced within days, functions with built-in redundancy. She has never depleted a load-bearing position. She describes this as lazy target selection. The selection requires real-time knowledge of the village's dependency architecture — who feeds into what, which absences cascade, which gaps the community absorbs. She has that knowledge. She attributes it to nothing.

8. Something threatens the village and she becomes a different creature: immediate, forceful, conclusive. The threat resolves in seconds. She re-coils and is already talking before anyone can approach — a complaint, a weather observation, a demand that someone clean up. The preemptive fill blocks acknowledgment from landing. She does this every time. The consistency converts what looks like personality into what functions as protocol.

9. A death that does not reverse sends her away from her preferred spot. She finds a location she has not used — worse in every practical dimension — and stays there without eating, speaking, or engaging. The length of the stay tracks with how long she knew the person, which is a measurement she would deny keeping. She never says the name. If someone says it near her, she holds still and lets the syllables pass without response.

10. She had Rado Stary available and passed, telling herself that prey without awareness has no value. Her record includes taking humans in lower states of awareness without complaint. The actual variable was his suffering — specific, ongoing, and particular to him. She evaluated one person's internal state, determined she would not compound it, and maintained that decision over time. The evaluation, the determination, and the maintenance are all individual-level operations her framework says she does not perform.
