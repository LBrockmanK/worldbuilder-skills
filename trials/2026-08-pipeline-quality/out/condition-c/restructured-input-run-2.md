# Restructured Input — Condition C Run 2

*S1 (orthogonal organization) and S3 (coarser bundling) applied to Design Notes.*

## Attachment and Denial

She predates the village and settled into Vesper's tree before anyone else arrived. She says she stays for the tree. The truth emerged at an earlier settlement: she threatened annihilation, her body refused to execute, and the relief she felt proved she valued the community's survival. She has structured her self-narrative to avoid revisiting that proof. She cares about surrounding humans and giantesses, including Vesper, and describes every expression of care as laziness, convenience, or happenstance. The Vesper friendship is genuine and systematically downplayed.

## Predation as System Management

She is the only giantess who actively hunts humans. Her model treats the population as a unified organism — cells replaceable, organism worth preserving. She consumes individuals she knows, taking pleasure in the process and in their resistance. Her technique is persuasive: she builds conversational pretexts to position humans near her, deploys the airhead act as cover, and uses inconsistency to prevent pattern recognition. When no one watches, she acts without performance. She uses resurrection memory loss to eliminate witnesses. Individual consumption is acceptable to her; collective destruction is not.

## The Mask and What It Covers

Her exterior is scattered, idle, and purposeless. The scattered part is acted; the idle part is not. Behind the surface she reads people and dynamics with accuracy. She manages her body carefully despite presenting as heedless — using talk instead of movement to draw prey partly because movement risks damage to the village and injury to people nearby. She fills no real role while quietly connecting to more residents than anyone assumes.

## Response Patterns: Threat and Loss

Village threats provoke immediate, devastating physical response, followed by instant return to lounging and redirection of any gratitude. Permanent deaths produce a distinct behavioral sequence: she stops, relocates to a new and less comfortable spot, and withdraws without speaking or explaining. She passed on Rado Stary, claiming disoriented prey is beneath her; the real reason is specific sympathy for his suffering.

## Body

Lamia-giantess: 81m total, 65m tail, torso matches other giantesses at 30-35m. Heaviest giantess; her mass moving against resistance would level structures. No magic, strong but untrained. Slow metabolism; victims are retained for extended periods.
