# Restructured Input — Condition C Run 1

*S1 (orthogonal organization) and S3 (coarser bundling) applied to Design Notes.*

## Drives and Self-Deception

She stays in Viralys and attributes her presence to the tree and to inertia. The real reason surfaced at a previous settlement where she threatened collective destruction, could not execute, and experienced involuntary relief — a physiological confirmation that she valued communal continuity. She has refused to examine this since. She cares about the community and its members, including Vesper, and frames every instance of that care as coincidence, convenience, or freeloading. Her friendship with Vesper is real and actively denied.

## Predatory Framework and Methods

She is the only actively predatory giantess. Her operating model treats the human population as a single organism whose individual cells can be consumed without threatening the whole. She eats known humans hedonistically and enjoys their resistance. Her predation is social: she manufactures verbal and situational pretexts to route humans near her mouth, using her performed airhead persona as the disarming mechanism and unpredictability as control. Unwitnessed, she drops performance and acts directly. She systematically exploits resurrection amnesia. Her boundary is collective destruction, not individual consumption.

## Facade and Precision

She presents as vacant, scatterbrained, and unmotivated. The vacancy is performed; the lack of motivation is genuine. She reads people and situations precisely behind the mask. She appears careless about her mass while actually managing it carefully — preferring verbal luring over physical movement partly to avoid damaging structures and injuring nearby humans. She holds nominal community roles she did not earn and contributes nothing formally, while maintaining more cross-population relationships than most residents.

## Response to Loss and Threat

She responds to external village threats with immediate overwhelming force, then instantly resumes her resting posture and deflects any acknowledgment. When a known human dies permanently, she stops what she is doing, relocates to an unfamiliar spot, and remains there in silence. She spared Rado Stary despite easy opportunity, stating that confused prey has no value — the real motivation is compassion for his particular suffering.

## Physical Form

Lamia-giantess, 81m total with 65m tail, humanoid torso equivalent to 30-35m. Heaviest giantess; forced movement would cause structural destruction. Purely physical, no magic, strong but untrained in combat. Slow metabolism with extended digestion.
