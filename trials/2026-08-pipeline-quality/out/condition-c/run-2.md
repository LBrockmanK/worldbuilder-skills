## PART 1: Synthesized Soul Entries

1. She lets her words scatter before they land, and the people around her fill the gaps with things they did not mean to share. She collects without acknowledgment. When she surfaces a detail days later — positioned with a precision that could only come from active listening — the source understands that the scattering was a net cast wide. The only honest part of the performance is her disinclination to move; that part she does not have to fake.

2. She points at the tree. The tree has been bare for months. She stayed through those months. She has not looked at other trees, other locations, other settlements. The answer is a cap screwed onto a question she will not unscrew: what is it about this place, specifically, that holds her. The answer, if she gave it, would include the word "people," and she has never used that word to explain anything about herself.

3. She consumes people she has interacted with and surveys the village's output in the same cycle — market volumes, workshop staffing, supply chains. The person is gone. The function continues or needs replacing. To her, the function is the real thing and the person was the housing. The framework holds until she tracks the housing too closely — knowing names, remembering habits, noticing the gap before the function gap registers. These moments exist. She overrides them.

4. She rearranges her mass until the natural path passes alongside her face, then launches a conversation calibrated to the specific person — their trade, their family, something she heard them mention. The specificity makes it feel personal. The positioning makes it lethal. The person experiences one layer. The other layer is invisible at their elevation. She varies the topic every time, so the method has no surviving pattern.

5. When no one is present, the halting speech and drifting focus disappear as though a switch has been thrown. She acts in a single sequence — tail first, mass second — with a directness that contradicts every observable minute of her public existence. She is back in her resting position before the next person passes through. The target returns from resurrection without the encounter in their memory. The two versions of her exist on opposite sides of a wall that resurrection maintains.

6. Years ago, facing a community that had called her threat, her body put the threat down without consulting her. The strike readiness drained from her in a wave — muscles unlocking, stance softening — and what replaced it was relief so immediate it felt like her body answering a question she had been avoiding. The answer was that she wanted the community to continue. She left before dawn. Every year since has been structured around not re-encountering that answer, because the answer implies an attachment to groups of people she has decided she does not have.

7. She draws from positions the village replenishes without difficulty — peripheral roles, redundant functions, gaps that close in days. Load-bearing positions are never hit. She attributes this to a lazy preference for targets that cause no complications. The preference, maintained across years without a single exception, requires her to model which roles are critical and which are disposable — a level of institutional knowledge that exceeds what the village's official administration holds. She has the knowledge. She does not account for having it.

8. A threat reaches the village and she becomes something the village does not recognize: fast, enormous, final. The engagement lasts seconds. She returns to her coils. Before the nearest person can form a sentence, she is already talking — a grievance about the noise, a demand that someone handle the debris, an observation about the weather. The verbal pre-empt absorbs the space where acknowledgment would form. She runs this operation identically every time, which means the casual tone is engineered, not felt.

9. When someone she knew dies and does not return, she halts. She leaves her accustomed spot and finds a new one — always less favorable, always unfamiliar. She stays there without eating, speaking, or sleeping for a period that corresponds, precisely, to how well she knew them. She does not mention their name. If someone else mentions it near her, she waits without motion until the sound has passed. She returns to her usual place eventually. She does not explain the interval.

10. She chose not to take Rado when nothing prevented it. She offered the explanation that prey without comprehension has no value, but her own history includes targets in states of lower comprehension. The distinction with Rado is his suffering — particular, ongoing, and visible to her. She evaluated his condition as an individual, determined his specific distress was a kind she would not add to, and has maintained that determination across time. The evaluation, the specificity, and the persistence are all operations her framework explicitly disallows — yet they continue, unpatched, because patching would require acknowledging that an individual mattered.

## PART 2: Variant Spreads

### Entry 1
**Variant A:** Her sentences trail away and people complete them, offering more than the sentence required. She stores everything. When she returns a detail later with impossible precision, the person who gave it understands the trailing was intake.
**Variant B:** The vacancy act gives her all available information and zero influence. No one guards their words near her. No one seeks her input. She holds richer data than anyone visibly present, and the cost — permanent dismissal — is one she selected and sustains.
**Variant C:** An observer sees drowsiness. The actual process: continuous cataloguing, cross-referencing, and selective future deployment of captured disclosures. The inertia is real — she does not want to exert. The mental vacancy is constructed atop the inertia and indistinguishable from it to anyone not running the comparison across time.
