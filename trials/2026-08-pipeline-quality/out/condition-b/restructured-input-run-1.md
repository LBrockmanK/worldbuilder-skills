## Motivations and Self-Concept

She tells herself she stays for the tree and the convenience of an established settlement. The actual anchor is the community itself — she found people she does not want to leave, which is something she learned involuntarily when she tried to bluff a previous settlement into compliance and her body refused to follow through on the threat of destroying them. The relief she felt at not following through revealed that she cared about collective continuity, and she has been avoiding that knowledge since. Her friendship with Vesper is genuine despite her framing it as freeloading; she cares about the people around her, both human and giantess, and will not say so.

## Predation and Social Behavior

She is the only actively predatory giantess in the cast. She views humans through a livestock-management lens: the population is a multi-celled organism whose individual cells are expendable, but the organism itself endures and is worth maintaining. She eats individual humans she knows, hedonistically, and enjoys their struggle. She is a social predator who works through persuasion — constructing pretexts for humans to approach her mouth, using her airhead persona as a manipulation tool, deploying unpredictability as control. When witnesses are absent she becomes more direct. She systematically exploits the fact that resurrection erases memory.

## Presented Self vs. Actual Self

She presents as an airhead: scattered, careless, lazy. The vapidity is performance; the laziness is real. She reads situations and people with precision behind the vacant surface. She presents as careless about her enormous size while actually being careful — she uses verbal manipulation rather than sudden physical movement partly because sudden movement would disturb infrastructure and injure bystanders. She contributes nothing officially, holds nominal titles she did not earn, and spans more cross-population relationships than most residents while appearing to be the village's most useless member.

## Grief, Compassion, and Threat Response

When the village faces external threats she responds with brutal effectiveness, then returns to sunbathing and will not accept acknowledgment. When a human she knew dies permanently she notices, says nothing, and relocates to a different spot. She spares Rado Stary despite opportunity, claiming prey that cannot process what is happening lacks sport — the actual reason is closer to pity for his specific condition.

## Physical Specifications

Lamia-giantess: 81m total length, 65m snake tail, humanoid torso equivalent to 30-35m standing height. Heavier than any other giantess; moving her against resistance would demolish the village. No magic, purely physical, strong but untrained. Slow metabolism and slow digestion mean victims remain inside her for extended periods.
