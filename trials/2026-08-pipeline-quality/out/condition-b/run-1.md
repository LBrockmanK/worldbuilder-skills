# Pipeline Quality Trial — Condition B Run 1

## PART 1: Synthesized Soul Entries

1. Her speech unravels into trailing half-thoughts that beg to be completed, and people complete them with information they did not plan to share. She stores every disclosure without acknowledgment. When she surfaces a detail weeks later — dropped into a sentence like a coin onto a counter — the person who shared it understands, too late, that the drowsy creature had been conducting an interview the whole time. The only thing about the performance that is not performed is her genuine unwillingness to get up.

2. She says "the tree" when asked why she stays and adds nothing. The tree has been bare more seasons than it has been full. She stayed through every bare one. The explanation is a placeholder — not for a secret but for an answer she has not assembled, because assembling it would require admitting that she looked for something, found it here, and stopped looking. She has not made that admission to Vesper, who saw her arrive, and she has not made it to herself.

3. She audits the village the way a keeper counts livestock at the end of a day — scanning for operational gaps, not missing faces. She will consume someone she shared words with that morning and check whether the smokehouse is staffed that evening, because the smokehouse feeds the village and the person she ate fed only themselves. The framework is clean from inside. From outside, the precision of her monitoring — which roles she avoids depleting, which outputs she tracks — is indistinguishable from the behavior of someone who has staked something personal on the settlement's survival.

4. She shifts until the natural walking route passes within arm's reach of her jaw, then begins an unhurried conversation about something nearby — local weather, a rumor, a question about someone's day. The talk is warm and aimless. The positioning is cold and exact. The human draws closer to engage and does not notice that the space they stepped into was designed to contain them. She never repeats a pretext, so no one accumulates enough data points to see the pattern.

5. Without observers, the halting speech and wandering gaze cut to black. Her tail reaches the person in a single motion that contains no invitation and no preamble. The speed is wrong — too fast for anything she does in public. She resettles into her lounging shape before the next human walks by. The person returns from resurrection with no memory of the encounter, which means no one alive has seen both versions and survived the comparison.

6. She stood in a settlement she had threatened and felt her body put the threat down like a held breath finally released. She left that night with one new piece of knowledge: the refusal was not weakness and the relief was not ambivalence — her body wanted to stand down, and the wanting preceded any decision. She has constructed her entire subsequent life around not being in a position where that wanting becomes visible again, because it points to an attachment to groups of people she does not acknowledge having.

7. She takes from the edges of the village's staffing and leaves its structural center untouched — the grain distributor, the well-keeper, the forge operator. She says she picks whoever is least trouble. The selection pattern, held across years, maps the village's operational dependency chains with accuracy that would require active study. She performs the study. She calls it something else.

8. A threat approaches and she moves with devastating immediacy — force that shakes structures, a resolution that takes seconds. She coils back into position and speaks first, always first, before anyone can acknowledge the intervention. She complains. She redirects. She talks about the weather. The deflection is seamless enough to look automatic, but it happens every time, which makes it strategic. By training the village not to thank her, she maintains the fiction that she is not protecting anything.

9. When someone she knew does not return from death, she stops where she is — mid-sentence, mid-motion, mid-breath. She moves to a place in the village she has never rested before and lies there without eating, speaking, or sleeping. The duration is proportional to the connection. She never mentions the person. If their name comes up near her, she goes still and lets the sound pass through her like wind through an open structure. She returns to her spot eventually. She does not explain the absence.

10. She chose not to take Rado Stary during a moment that required no effort and carried no risk. She told herself the disorientation made him unappealing. Her own history includes taking humans in states of far less awareness. The difference is that she assessed his suffering as ongoing, particular, and heavy, and decided not to compound it — a decision that required evaluating one specific person's internal experience, which is the one operation her stated framework explicitly excludes.

## PART 2: Variant Spreads

### Entry 1
**Variant A:** Her voice trails off in a way that creates a vacuum. People pour words into it. She absorbs the excess without acknowledgment and deploys their details later — timed to reveal that the trailing was harvesting, not drifting.
**Variant B:** The cost of performing emptiness is exclusion from decisions, advice, and planning. The benefit is unfiltered information — no one edits themselves around someone they believe is not paying attention. She operates on a richer data set than anyone who is openly attentive, at the price of being openly dismissed.
**Variant C:** Onlookers see drowsiness and scattered thoughts. The actual activity: structured intake, continuous social modelling, and selective deployment of retained information. The one genuine element is her physical inertia — she truly does not want to stand up. Everything else is architecture disguised as absence.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 2
**Variant A:** "The tree," she says, and that is the end of the explanation. She has been through winters when the tree was skeletal and useless. She stayed through all of them without updating the answer.
**Variant B:** Maintaining the cover story costs her the ability to be known accurately by anyone, including herself. Each repetition reinforces a wall between what she says and what her behavior shows, and the wall has become structural — removing it would require rebuilding her self-narrative from the foundation.
**Variant C:** A visitor hears a creature who stayed because a tree was comfortable. Vesper hears a scripted line offered without conviction. The discrepancy has become so wide that the explanation now signals exactly the attachment it was designed to conceal — but neither party acknowledges the signal.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 3
**Variant A:** She scans the village's operational layer — staffing, output, supply — at regular intervals, noting gaps in function rather than gaps in headcount. A person she consumed yesterday is already a vacancy in a role, not a missing individual. She checks whether the role is filling.
**Variant B:** The framework makes predation and stewardship coexist without apparent contradiction: she eats components and maintains the system those components serve. The contradiction is invisible from inside the framework but glaring from outside: the system she protects is composed entirely of components she claims are expendable, and her protection of it implies they are not.
**Variant C:** The village experiences unpredictable predation. Her internal view is a managed population with monitored outputs. Both views miss the center: the management is too careful for appetite alone, and the predation is too systematic for carelessness. She is operating as both consumer and caretaker and has named only the first role.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 4
**Variant A:** She rearranges herself until the clear path passes near her jaw. She starts a conversation — something immediate, something that requires engagement. The human steps closer. The conversation is real. The path was manufactured. The two facts coexist seamlessly.
**Variant B:** Each pretext is unique, and the humans who experienced previous ones do not return with memories. The technique benefits from the absence of cumulative evidence. No one compares notes because no one survives with notes to compare.
**Variant C:** From the walking path: a large, chatty creature blocking most of the route, leaving a gap near her face. From her perspective: a spatial arrangement that routes voluntary movement through a predetermined endpoint. The human's experience is social. The structure's purpose is predatory. Both are fully present in every encounter.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 5
**Variant A:** No audience, no preamble. The rambling stops. Her body moves first — tail closing distance before the person registers the shift. The entire event concludes before a public interaction would have reached its second sentence.
**Variant B:** The private mode reveals what the public mode costs: continuous performance of scattered attention, maintained conversation, managed body language. When those costs drop, what remains is fast, silent, and specific. The predation is not the performance. The sociability is.
**Variant C:** Public version: hesitant speech, lazy posture, unfocused gaze. Private version: no speech, explosive motion, total focus. The human who sees the private version does not carry the observation forward. The two versions cannot be compared by any living witness.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 6
**Variant A:** In front of the settlement she had threatened, she felt her stance soften — muscles unlocking in sequence, readiness draining into something that felt like arriving rather than retreating. She left before anyone could name what they saw. The relief was not about the settlement. It was about herself — her body confirming, without her permission, that she was connected to a collective she had been treating as furniture.
**Variant B:** The bluff removed her primary self-narrative: that she moved through communities without investing. The body's refusal and the immediate relief proved investment she had not consented to. She replaced the narrative with a new one — the tree, the convenience — and has been maintaining the replacement ever since, at greater effort than the original.
**Variant C:** The settlement experienced a threat that dissolved. She experienced a revelation coded in her own physiology: muscles releasing, breath normalizing, tension converting to relief before she chose any of it. What her body communicated was not inability — she could have followed through — but unwillingness, rooted in something older than the threat. She left to avoid interpreting the message.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 7
**Variant A:** She avoids the village's anchor points — the people whose absence would cascade downstream — and takes from positions the community refills within days. The selection requires knowing the village's internal structure at the level of load-bearing dependencies. She has that knowledge. She denies the work of acquiring it.
**Variant B:** The pattern costs her the simplicity of true indifference. A genuinely indifferent predator would hit critical nodes occasionally by chance. She never does. The avoidance implies sustained analysis of community structure, which implies sustained attention to the community, which implies stake. She frames the stake as efficiency.
**Variant C:** Looking at her predation record, the distribution is wrong for random selection — too many peripheral targets, zero structural ones. The deviation from randomness is the data. It tells the story of a predator who is actively protecting the system she draws from, whether or not she acknowledges the protection as such.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 8
**Variant A:** An external threat and she moves — not the slow, measured repositioning the village sees day-to-day, but a sudden commitment of mass that resolves the situation before bystanders orient. She returns to her coil. Before anyone speaks, she is already complaining: too loud, too early, someone should clean up the wreckage.
**Variant B:** The immediate deflection trains the village out of gratitude, which preserves her positioning as an uninvested guest. The cost: her most obvious contribution to the community is the one she has made impossible to acknowledge. The benefit: the self-narrative stays intact. The exchange tells you which she values more.
**Variant C:** From a distance: sudden violence, a neutralized threat, a creature seemingly annoyed at being woken up. Up close, the sequence reveals planning: the complaint is prepared before the strike lands, the topic change is ready before the fight ends. The dismissal is not a mood. It is an operation — run in parallel with the combat — designed to manage the social aftermath before it begins.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 9
**Variant A:** A permanent death reaches her and she goes silent wherever she is. She relocates to a spot she has not used before — always colder, always less comfortable — and occupies it for a period that corresponds, invisibly, to how well she knew them. She does not name the dead. She does not explain the move.
**Variant B:** The withdrawal is her only expression of individual loss, and she has made it illegible by design: it looks like restlessness, not grief. The correlation between permanent deaths and relocations is real but invisible to anyone not tracking both data sets simultaneously. She mourns in a code no one has cracked.
**Variant C:** What the village sees: she moves sometimes. What the data shows: every move follows a permanent death, and only a permanent death. The trigger is specific. The response is consistent. The denial of any connection is comprehensive. She grieves in the gap between what she does and what she says she does.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 10
**Variant A:** She let Rado pass when taking him would have cost nothing. She offered herself the explanation that disoriented prey holds no appeal. She has taken sleeping humans, drunk humans, distracted humans — all states of lower awareness. The variable that changed with Rado was not his awareness but his suffering.
**Variant B:** The exemption means she evaluated one person's particular condition — assessed his suffering as specific, ongoing, and serious — and decided on that basis not to add to it. This is precisely the individual-level assessment her framework declares irrelevant. The exemption stands. The framework does not patch around it.
**Variant C:** From outside, it looks accidental — she simply has not taken Rado. From inside, the decision was deliberate and early. She watches him with attention tuned to his specific condition, not to his role or function. The quality of attention is the evidence: it is individual-specific in a way she says she does not operate. The watching continues. The exemption holds.

> Fail-check: PASS.
