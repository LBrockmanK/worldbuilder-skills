# Pipeline Quality Trial — Condition B Run 2

## PART 1: Synthesized Soul Entries

1. She speaks in dissolving half-sentences that invite completion, and people supply more than the sentence needed. She catalogs the surplus without reaction. Days later she returns a detail so precisely placed that the person who offered it realizes the sleepy voice and wandering gaze were collection instruments. The only authentic piece is the inertia — she would genuinely rather not move.

2. She cites the tree as her reason for staying. The tree provides neither warmth nor shade for much of the year. She has remained through those empty seasons without revising the explanation or scouting alternatives. The line functions less as a reason and more as a seal over a question she has decided not to open: what she found here that she has not found elsewhere, and what it would mean to name it.

3. She tracks village operations — food production, craft output, population flow — with the regularity and granularity of someone managing a resource. She eats a person she greeted that morning and checks whether the resulting vacancy affects downstream functions. The vacant role concerns her. The vacant life does not. At least, that is the framework, and the framework's integrity depends on never noticing that the role-monitoring requires knowing individual humans well enough to assess their systemic weight.

4. She arranges her body so the open route passes alongside her mouth, then opens a conversation that draws the listener a step closer — a question about their family, a half-remembered observation about the weather near their home. The conversation is genuine; the geometry is predatory. The fusion is so seamless the human experiences friendliness. The humans who experienced it before them are not available to compare.

5. Alone with a target, the loose posture and halting speech shut off in a single transition. Her tail reaches them before they register the change. The entire encounter, from switch to cleanup, fits inside the time her public persona would spend on a greeting. She resumes the persona before the next passerby arrives. The victim returns from resurrection remembering nothing, which keeps the two versions permanently separate.

6. At an earlier settlement, she threatened collective destruction and stood ready to deliver. Her body released the readiness before her mind chose to — a decompression that traveled through her like heat leaving a surface. The relief was immediate, and it was the relief that changed her: it was her own response telling her she valued the collective's survival, a verdict she had not requested and could not appeal. She left. She has structured every subsequent year to avoid being in a position where that verdict reasserts itself.

7. Her targets occupy roles the village replaces without strain — peripheral positions, redundant functions, gaps that close within days. She has not taken a load-bearing community member in the entire time she has been here. She attributes this to a preference for easy targets. The preference would need to be informed by a comprehensive model of the village's dependency architecture to produce the pattern it produces. She has the model. She says she has a preference.

8. An external threat and her lounging body becomes a weapon. The response is immediate, massive, and final. She returns to her coiled resting shape before the dust settles. When someone approaches with acknowledgment forming, she talks first — a gripe, a non-sequitur, an observation about something trivial. She fills the conversational space before gratitude can enter it. The fill is instant and consistent across every incident, which marks it as policy, not personality.

9. A death without return produces a specific sequence: she stops, she leaves her spot, she finds somewhere she has not rested before. The new location is always inferior — less comfortable, less sheltered, less suited to her body. She stays without eating, talking, or engaging for a duration that maps, silently, to how long she knew the person. She never acknowledges the death. She never explains the relocation. If the name is spoken nearby, she holds herself still until it passes.

10. She assessed Rado Stary's condition — his persistent confusion, his particular suffering — and exempted him from predation she would otherwise have pursued without hesitation. Her stated rationale does not hold against her own record. The actual decision required her to evaluate one specific human's internal state, determine that his suffering was a kind she would not add to, and maintain that determination over time. This is care indexed to an individual, exercised by someone whose operating principle is that individuals do not register.

## PART 2: Variant Spreads

### Entry 1
**Variant A:** Her sentences lose their endings and people supply replacements, bringing their own thoughts into a gap she manufactured. She holds the information without responding. Later, she returns it — one detail, perfectly timed — and the source understands that the sleepiness was operational.
**Variant B:** Performing absence costs her all influence and earns her all information. No one censors themselves near a creature they believe is half-asleep. She runs on richer social data than anyone who is visibly engaged, and the trade-off — knowing everything, being asked nothing — suits the role she has designed for herself.
**Variant C:** Surface: a creature drifting in and out of a conversation. Subsurface: active intake, sorting, and storage of disclosures that will be recalled with precision. The lethargy is the one true thing — she does not want to exert herself. The apparent mental absence is constructed to look like a consequence of the lethargy. It is not. It is a separate operation.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 2
**Variant A:** She says "the tree" and gestures vaguely overhead. The tree is leafless. She has been through leafless seasons without leaving or adjusting the story. She has not scouted other locations.
**Variant B:** The excuse has thinned to transparency, but replacing it would require constructing a truthful alternative, and every truthful alternative contains the word "stay" in a sense that implies choosing this place for reasons that exist inside it, not above it. She keeps the excuse because the replacement costs more than the embarrassment.
**Variant C:** Visitors accept the tree as sufficient explanation. Vesper hears it as ritual — a response offered by reflex, stripped of informational content, serving only to close the question. The tree is not the reason. The refusal to provide the reason is the tell. Both parties know this; neither says so.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 3
**Variant A:** She monitors production volumes, staffing levels, and supply chains at intervals. A person she ate that morning becomes a gap in a function she tracks. She checks whether the gap is filling. Whether the person had a name she liked is not part of the assessment.
**Variant B:** The operational view lets predation proceed without resistance — she consumes parts and maintains the whole. The cost appears when the framework cracks: she sometimes pauses on a detail about someone she consumed — a habit, a phrase they used — before overwriting it with the systemic view. These moments are brief. They should not exist if the framework is airtight.
**Variant C:** The village experiences her as a sporadic predator. She experiences herself as a systems manager. The truth visible from neither position: her management behavior implies she is investing in an outcome, and the investment implies she values the community as more than a food source. The predation says otherwise. She lives in the gap between the two and does not look down.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 4
**Variant A:** Her body redistributes until the walkable route channels toward her face. She opens a question — specific, warm, requiring the listener to stay and engage. The listener steps closer. The specificity makes it personal. The geometry makes it functional. The two layers operate simultaneously and neither interrupts the other.
**Variant B:** The pretext method benefits from non-repetition and non-survival. She never uses the same conversational opener twice. The people who fell for previous ones do not return with the memory, so no one assembles the sequence into a recognizable technique.
**Variant C:** What the human experiences: an engaging creature asking about their day. What a structural analysis reveals: a spatial funnel built from repositioned mass, guided by a conversation calibrated to maintain proximity. The human's experience is genuine — she is genuinely interested. The interest and the predation are not separate. They are the same event observed from different layers.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 5
**Variant A:** No audience present, the wandering talk and soft posture switch off. Her tail moves first. The target is restrained before they process the transition. The speed reveals a physicality that her public behavior systematically conceals.
**Variant B:** The private mode is what remains when performance costs drop to zero — fast, silent, directed. The contrast with her public mode is the strongest evidence that public behavior requires continuous effort. The predation is not effortful. The sociability is.
**Variant C:** Two versions exist. The public one speaks slowly, drifts, seems to lose track. The private one does not speak at all and has never lost track of anything. No living person has seen both and retained the memory. The versions are designed to be irreconcilable, and the design works because the only overlap — the target — cannot report.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 6
**Variant A:** Standing at full scale before the settlement that had refused her, she felt her readiness drain — not collapse but release, like unclenching something she had been holding without noticing. The relief arrived before she chose to feel it. She left overnight. She now knows her body will vote against community destruction, and she has arranged her life to never call that vote again.
**Variant B:** The bluff stripped her of a framework: that she existed alongside communities without being part of them. The physical refusal and the relief that endorsed it were proof of membership she had not applied for. She replaced the framework with a simpler one — tree, convenience, no attachment — that requires more active maintenance than the one it replaced.
**Variant C:** The settlement saw a hollow threat. She saw a self-disclosure: her physiology answering a question about attachment before her cognition processed it. The relief was the signature — instant, deep, unambiguous. It was the body confirming not just that she would not destroy them but that she did not want to. Want was the word she could not accommodate. She has been reorganizing around its absence ever since.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 7
**Variant A:** She targets positions the village replenishes within days — redundant roles, peripheral functions, gaps that close quickly. She has never taken someone whose disappearance would cascade into dependent systems. She credits instinct for path of least resistance. The path is too precise for instinct.
**Variant B:** Sustaining the pattern means she actively models the village's internal dependency structure — who feeds what, which roles have fallback, which removals cascade. This knowledge is acquired through sustained observation of a community she claims she is not invested in. The knowledge itself is the investment; she cannot have one without the other.
**Variant C:** If her predation were random, critical nodes would occasionally be hit. They have not been. The zero-hit record on load-bearing roles, across years, is the statistical fingerprint of intentional avoidance. The intention requires a map of what is load-bearing. The map requires attention. The attention implies stake. She has the map. She denies the stake.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 8
**Variant A:** External threat: she unfurls with force that cracks structural supports and shakes the ground. The situation resolves. She coils back, and before anyone can approach, she is already talking — a complaint about the disruption, a question about something mundane. The conversational pre-empt lands before gratitude can form.
**Variant B:** Consistent deflection has built a social norm: the village does not thank her. This norm keeps her contribution unofficial, which is the category she needs it in. The cost is that her most concrete expression of care for the community — lethal defense — is the one she has made socially invisible.
**Variant C:** What bystanders observe: an eruption, a resolution, an annoyed creature. What a pattern analysis shows: the deflection is prepared before impact; the complaint and the redirect are queued during the engagement. She fights two battles per incident — the physical threat and the social aftermath — and wins both with the same planning horizon.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 9
**Variant A:** Someone dies permanently. She goes quiet. She leaves her usual position and finds a spot she has not used — always worse, always exposed or cold. She stays for days. She does not eat or talk. She does not speak the name.
**Variant B:** The duration of the withdrawal tracks with depth of familiarity, which is a metric she keeps for individuals she claims not to differentiate. The tracking is the breach in her framework: it means she measured connection, which means connection existed, which means individuals were not interchangeable after all.
**Variant C:** The village reads her relocations as restlessness or mood. The actual pattern: every relocation follows a non-resurrecting death, and the timing is immediate. She moves within hours. She returns without explanation. The grief is expressed in movement and silence and deliberate discomfort — a register no one around her has decoded, which may be the point.

> Fail-check: PASS.

### Entry 10
**Variant A:** Rado was available and she passed. She said impaired prey was not worth the effort. She has expended less effort on more impaired targets. The distinction was not about effort — it was about his particular suffering, which she assessed and decided not to deepen.
**Variant B:** By exempting Rado she created a data point her framework cannot explain: an individual-level mercy decision based on evaluation of one person's subjective state. The framework holds that subjective states of individual humans are not inputs to her decision-making. Rado's exemption is an input from exactly that category. She has not reconciled the two.
**Variant C:** From the outside, Rado's safety looks like coincidence or oversight. From her vantage, it was a single decision made early and never revisited. She assessed his suffering, judged it to be a kind she would not compound, and locked the judgment in. The assessment required attending to one individual as an individual. The lock required maintaining that attention over time. Both are acts her stated framework says she does not perform.

> Fail-check: PASS.
