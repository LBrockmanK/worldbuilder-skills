# Restructured Input — Condition B Run 2

*S1 (orthogonal organization) and S3 (coarser bundling) applied to Design Notes.*

## Internal Drives and Attachments

She arrived before the village existed and settled into Vesper's tree. Her stated reason for remaining is the tree itself. The actual reason surfaced years earlier at a different settlement, where she threatened collective destruction, found herself physically unable to follow through, and felt relief at the refusal — proving she cared about communal continuity, a discovery she has been avoiding since. She cares about the people around her, including Vesper, and frames every attachment as convenience or coincidence. The friendship with Vesper is real; the denial of it is equally real.

## Predatory Operation and Social Manipulation

She is the cast's only actively predatory giantess. Her framework treats the human population as an organism whose individual cells are expendable provided the organism continues. She consumes known individuals hedonistically and enjoys the process. Her method is social rather than physical: she constructs pretexts that lead humans close to her mouth, uses her performed vacancy as a disarming tool, and relies on unpredictability. Without witnesses she becomes direct and efficient. She exploits resurrection amnesia to erase evidence. She draws a line at collective annihilation but not at individual consumption.

## Performance and Concealment

Her surface reads as scattered, careless, and idle. The scatteredness is acted; the idleness is genuine. She reads people and situations with precision the vacant performance conceals. She appears careless about her massive body while exercising careful control — using verbal manipulation rather than sudden movement partly because movement risks structural damage and bystander harm. She holds nominal community roles she did not earn and contributes nothing formally while functioning as one of the village's most socially connected members.

## Protective and Grief Responses

External threats draw immediate, overwhelming physical response followed by instant return to resting posture and active deflection of any recognition. Permanent deaths — those without resurrection — produce silent acknowledgment: she stops speaking, relocates to an unfamiliar spot, and remains there without engaging. She spared Rado Stary when the opportunity was easy, citing lack of sport in confused prey while the actual motivation was specific compassion for his condition.

## Physiology

Lamia-giantess form, 81m total with 65m tail. Torso scale equivalent to other giantesses at 30-35m. Heaviest resident; displacement against resistance would destroy structures. No magical abilities, physically powerful but combat-untrained. Slow metabolism produces extended digestion periods.
