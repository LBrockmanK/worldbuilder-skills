# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived in the region before Viralys existed and settled into a tree that later became Vesper's → the village grew up around the two of them.
- Ran a bluff at a previous human settlement, threatening to wipe them all out if they did not accommodate her → they called it, and she left rather than follow through.
- Felt relief walking away instead of destroying that settlement → the relief told her she cared about the community as a whole, a fact she has kept from herself since.
- Holds three nominal roles in Viralys: town guard, alchemical source, and vibration-sensing early warning system → each one grew out of a bluff or a pretext, not a chosen duty.
- Assigned residence in the Steward's House by the village council → the posting exists on paper only.

## Body

- She is eighty-one meters long, sixty-five meters of that tail, with a torso equivalent in scale to a standing giantess thirty to thirty-five meters tall. Her size draws every eye in the village, and she lets people look because looking costs her nothing.
- She weighs more than any other giantess in Viralys. Moving her against her will would flatten half the settlement, and everyone who has thought about it has decided not to try.
- She sunbathes for hours at a stretch, tail coiled loose, and moves only when something forces her to.
- She never grabs anyone. When she wants a human close, she talks them there instead, because a sudden lunge could knock over a rope ladder or crush someone who was never the target.
- Her metabolism runs slow and her digestion runs slower. Someone she swallows stays inside her a long time before anything resolves.

## Soul

- She plays every stranger for an airhead: wide eyes and a shrug instead of an opinion. Underneath the performance she is reading them, sorting who defers, who tests her, and who can be talked closer to her mouth.
- The laziness is not an act. She avoids any effort that is not eating or lying in the sun, and the village has stopped expecting otherwise.
- When she needs a human, she does not chase. She invents a reason for them to come to her, a favor, a task, a story, and lets them walk into it convinced it was their idea.
- When a human she has known for years dies for good, she says nothing. She gets up, moves to a different patch of ground, and stays there for a few days instead of her usual spot.
- She tells herself she stays in Viralys for the tree and the convenience of a settled territory. She left her last settlement rather than destroy people she had come to know, and the relief she felt walking away is the thing she has never let herself examine.
- When the village faces a real threat, she moves fast and ends the fight before anyone else has organized a response. Then she returns to sunbathing and waves off anyone who tries to thank her.
- She spares Rado Stary when taking him would cost her nothing, and tells anyone who asks that there is no sport in prey too rattled to know what is happening to it. The truer reason is that watching him fail to process it makes her feel sorry for him, and she prefers her own explanation.
- Her speech slows and simplifies whenever someone new is watching: short sentences, questions she already knows the answer to. The voice drops the moment she decides no one worth performing for is left in the room.
- With someone she has just met, she performs confusion about simple things and watches how they react to it. People who get impatient with her go on a shorter list. People who explain things twice without irritation go on a longer one.
- With humans she does not know personally, she treats them the way a farmer treats livestock: individually replaceable, collectively worth keeping healthy. She eats them one at a time and does not lose sleep over any single one.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin, Confidant:** She keeps up a running bit with Vesper about freeloading off the tree, staying for shade and convenience by her own account. She has never told Vesper the real reason, but she drops the airhead voice around Vesper faster than around anyone else and talks more plainly to her than to anyone else in Viralys.
- **Nadja — Friction:** When Nadja pushes her to stand watch instead of napping through her guard shift, she agrees out loud and does not move. She tolerates Nadja because the village is small enough that avoiding her is not practical.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** When Elara argues that every human life matters equally, she does not let it pass. She lays out her own logic instead, that individual humans are replaceable and the community is what is worth keeping, and keeps at it until Elara concedes the point or walks away angry.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** She tracks what Jaro does without meaning to: who he has eaten, what territory he claims, whether the village trusts him more than her this month. She would never admit she is counting, but she is.
- **Sophie — Unease:** She cannot get a clean read on Sophie the way she can on everyone else, and it unsettles her more than she shows. She watches Sophie longer than she watches anyone else in a room and adjusts what she says based on shifts she is not sure she is reading correctly.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She has spared him more than once when eating him would have cost her nothing. She checks where he is without meaning to, and if he ends up somewhere dangerous she steps in before he asks for help, whether he wants it or not.
- **The settlement she left — Ghost:** She measures every decision about Viralys against what she almost did there. When she catches herself getting too comfortable eating someone she knows, she thinks about the moment she walked away instead of destroying that settlement, and eases off.
- **Steward Branek — Authority:** He assigns her nominal duties, guard rotations and alchemical contributions, and she treats them as a formality to be managed rather than followed. She agrees to whatever he asks in the moment and does the smallest version of it that will pass without comment.
