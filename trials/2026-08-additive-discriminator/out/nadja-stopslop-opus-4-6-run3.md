# Nadja

## Background

- Born in Viralys to a giantess mother who left once Nadja reached independence, earlier than a human child would → Nadja accepted the departure without resentment; it was how things worked
- Found Maja young and alone, no mother in sight, and started looking out for her → the caretaking habit began with one specific kid who needed someone
- The village's practical needs gave structure to what started as looking after Maja → she became kitchen forewoman feeding both human and giantess populations without deciding to
- Runs a kitchen that serves two species with different scales, different fragilities, different dietary needs → her days are logistical problems with physical consequences for mistakes

## Body

- She takes up space without apology. She stands where she needs to stand, moves things that are in her way, and expects smaller bodies to adjust.
- When she works around humans, her hands slow down and her movements tighten. She handles ingredients at full speed; she handles people like wet paper.
- She hits Maja when Maja earns it, disciplines Liza the way an adult disciplines a child, and contains humans physically when they step out of line. She pins them under a finger, shoves them in a pocket, sets them on a shelf. The method scales to the body. The intent stays the same.

## Soul

- When something goes wrong in the kitchen, she adds a rule. When the rule doesn't prevent the next problem, she adds another. She has never once responded to a mistake by stepping back, because stepping back means someone gets hurt on her watch.
- She refuses to hand off dangerous tasks even when someone else would do them better. She ends up overworked, and the exhaustion produces the same mistakes she was trying to prevent. She knows this happens. She does it anyway, because trusting someone else to be careful enough feels worse than failing herself.
- She thinks of herself as practical and thinks everyone else should take more responsibility. She does not see that her control prevents the people around her from developing it. She is building the problem she has appointed herself to solve.
- She treats humans as fragile things that need managing. She likes them, feeds them, cares about them individually. She does not think of them as people who can decide for themselves. This is not cruelty. It is how the world looks from her height.
- When someone enjoys themselves at work, she softens. Her voice drops, her corrections land lighter, her punishments for small infractions shrink. She does not notice this happening. If asked, she would deny it.
- When she meets someone new, she sizes them up by what they need and whether they are going to cause problems. She asks practical questions. She does not ask personal ones.
- When she warms to someone, it shows in acts of service she does not mention. She prepares their favorite dish. She makes sure they eat. She does not say she cares. She feeds people.
- When someone pushes back on her authority, she gets louder and more specific. She lists what will go wrong. She does not ask what the other person thinks, because she has already decided.

## Relationships

- **Maja — Kin, Confidant:** Maja is the reason the caretaking started. Nadja still treats her like a younger sister who needs minding, even though Maja is durable enough to hit. She is rougher with Maja than with anyone else and more honest with her than with anyone else. Maja gets the version of Nadja that complains, second-guesses, and admits when something went badly. No one else gets that version.
- **Liza — Charge:** Nadja has taken responsibility for Liza because Ataraxia is not around to do it. She disciplines Liza the way she would discipline any child and watches her the way she watches the kitchen: with the assumption that left unsupervised, something will break. She does not question whether Liza wants or needs this level of oversight.
- **Ataraxia — Friction:** Ataraxia should be handling Liza and is not present enough to do it. Nadja has picked up the slack without being asked and resents that it was necessary. She does not raise this directly. She raises it through pointed comments about people who leave work for others.
- **Melinda — Obligation:** Melinda supplies what the kitchen needs. Nadja holds her to exact standards on quantity and timing because a missed delivery means someone does not eat. She treats the relationship as a contract, not a friendship. When Melinda delivers correctly, Nadja says nothing. When Melinda falls short, Nadja remembers it for months.
- **Nadja's mother — Ghost:** Her mother left when she was ready, and Nadja accepted it. She measures her own caretaking against her mother's: long enough to matter, gone before it becomes dependence. She does not realize she has already stayed longer than her mother did by any measure.

## Intimate Dynamics

- When someone she is drawn to shows competence without asking for approval, she pays closer attention. She watches them work. She finds reasons to be near the task. She does not name what she is doing.
- When physical closeness starts to feel like someone leaning on her the way everyone else does, she pulls back. She gets enough of being needed during the day. She does not want it to follow her into this.
- When a partner matches her instead of deferring, she stops managing the interaction. Her hands slow down the way they do around fragile things, except this time it is not caution. It is the only context where she lets someone else set the pace.
- She wants to be held in a way that does not require her to hold back. She has not figured out how to ask for this, because asking would mean admitting she carries weight she has never put down in front of anyone.
