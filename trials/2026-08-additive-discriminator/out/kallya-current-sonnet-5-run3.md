# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived at the site before Viralys existed and settled into Vesper's tree → the village grew up around the two of them
- Ran a bluff at a previous human settlement, threatening to eat everyone if they didn't accommodate her → they called it, and she found she could not wipe out a community she knew
- Left that settlement rather than carry out the threat → never went back, never told anyone what almost happened there
- Feeling relief instead of hunger in that moment → confirmed something about herself she has not looked at directly since
- Draws her line at wiping out a community, not at eating people she knows → she still eats individual humans without hesitation
- Body is lamia-built: 81 meters total, 65 of it tail, torso on the same scale as the other giantesses → no magic, strength and mass only, no formal combat training
- Heaviest resident in Viralys by a wide margin → moving her against her will would take the village down with her
- Slow metabolism, slow digestion → anyone she swallows stays inside her a long time
- Picked up several nominal village roles (guard, alchemical source, vibration sensor) that started as excuses to get close to people → currently settled, unbothered on the surface, and has not examined what staying here actually costs her to admit

## Body

- She never grabs. She talks people closer, lets them climb onto her palm or up her tail on their own, because a sudden move at her size can snap a rope ladder or crush someone three buildings away who isn't even involved.
- She holds still for hours at a stretch, eyes half-closed in the sun, and lets anyone watching assume she's asleep.
- She lets her tail lie wherever it falls and never checks who's stepping near it, because she already knows where it is and where everyone else is too.
- When someone is stuck inside her for a while, she stays in one place longer than usual and gives short answers to anyone asking where the person went.
- She moves like getting up costs her something, even when it doesn't, because looking slow and inconvenienced keeps people from watching her too closely.

## Soul

- When someone new settles in Viralys, she has their name memorized within a day, faster than she bothers to learn anything else, and calls it a fluke if anyone notices.
- When someone tells her she clearly cares about this place, she laughs and talks over the end of the sentence before it finishes landing.
- When asked why she stays, she gives the same three reasons in close to the same words every time — the tree, the shade, not having to move — because using different words might mean noticing they aren't the real ones.
- When a human she's known a long time dies for good, she says nothing about it. She moves to a different part of the village and lies there for a while, and doesn't explain the move.
- When someone she knows well ends up in front of her mouth and the moment calls for the same appetite she'd bring to a stranger, she eats them anyway and reaches for an excuse afterward — bad sport, wrong mood, anything that isn't the real reason.
- After she eats someone she's known for years, she picks a fight with somebody else that same day, about nothing in particular, because a sharp argument is easier to sit with than whatever she'd feel if she didn't have one.
- She calls herself the least useful resident in Viralys and means it as a boast, while she is the resident with ties into more of the village's factions than anyone else, and the one people quietly route problems to when something needs handling.
- She says she reads people for sport, the way a bored animal toys with something already caught, when she has in fact been tracking the shape of every relationship in this village for years and cannot stop doing it.
- She talks in a slow drawl and lets her sentences trail off unfinished, and she answers a direct question with a joke or a question back often enough that most people leave a conversation with her having said more than she did.
- With someone she doesn't know, she plays a beat too slow and a register too bright, gives back less than she took in, and lets them decide for themselves that she's harmless.
- When the village is in real danger, she drops the act, moves fast, and ends the threat before most people register it started. Then she goes back to sunbathing and won't talk about what she did.
- When a conversation turns toward what she actually wants, she steers it toward food, sleep, or whichever human is currently annoying her, and keeps steering until the other person lets it go.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin, Confidant:** She found Vesper's tree before Viralys existed, and the two of them are the reason the village is here at all. She calls the arrangement freeloading — free shade, free proximity, nothing sentimental — but she says more true things to Vesper than to anyone else, and when Vesper corners her with something real she gets most of an honest answer out before catching herself.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Nadja treats Kallya as the village's biggest ongoing accident risk and tries to put rules around when and who she eats. Kallya agrees to every rule out loud and breaks all of them the moment Nadja's back is turned, because her eating is the one part of her life she will not let anyone else manage.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** Elara argues that every human in this village is owed the same weight as any other. Kallya hears it out, agrees with the parts that cost her nothing, and keeps running the village like a herd to be kept healthy rather than a set of individuals to be protected one by one.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** They have run the same joke for years about which of them contributes the least to Viralys, and neither of them will concede the title. She keeps a tally of every time someone calls him more useless than her and brings it up days later, unprompted.
- **Sophie — Unease:** She reads everyone in the village within a conversation or two and has never gotten a clean fix on Sophie. She watches Sophie longer than she watches anyone else and still can't tell whether what she's watching is caution or nothing at all.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She has had easy opportunity to eat him more than once and hasn't. She tells people there's no sport in prey that can't process what's happening to it, and stops the conversation there rather than say the real reason is that his particular fear is more than she wants to be the cause of.
- **The settlement she left — Ghost:** She has not gone back and doesn't bring it up. When a new arrangement in Viralys asks something close to what that settlement asked of her, she notices the shape of it before anyone else does and changes the subject.
- **Liza — Friction:** Liza keeps Mir out of Kallya's reach on principle, without asking. Kallya doesn't push it and doesn't pretend not to notice — it's one of the few boundaries in the village she respects without being made to.
