# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived before Viralys existed and settled into Vesper's tree → the village grew up around the two of them
- Ran a bluff at a previous settlement, implying she would eat everyone if they did not accommodate her → they called it, and she found she could not follow through, so she left instead
- Discovered in that moment that she cared about the community as a whole → she has not told anyone this
- Draws her line at genocide, not at predation → she still eats individual humans she knows without conflict
- Was assigned a room in the Steward's House and given nominal duties: town guard, alchemical source, vibration sensor → she treats all three as cover stories and does none of them with any effort
- Is a lamia-giantess, 81 meters total with a 65-meter tail → she is heavier than any other giantess in the village
- Has no magic and no combat training → her size is the only advantage she has ever needed

## Body

- She lies across the widest stretch of open ground she can find and does not move for hours. Getting her upright and relocated against her will would flatten half the village, so nobody tries.
- She lets her tail drape loose over rooftops and rope bridges instead of coiling it tight. People step over it like furniture, which is what she wants them to do.
- When someone stands near her mouth, she goes still and lazy-eyed rather than reaching for them. She has learned that sudden movement snaps rope ladders and knocks things off ledges, so she waits for people to come to her instead of grabbing.
- She yawns wide and often, tail flicking, looking bored even in the middle of watching everything happening around her.

## Soul

- When someone asks her opinion on village business, she gives a vague answer and changes the subject to napping. She has already worked out the situation in full and prefers nobody know that.
- When a threat reaches Viralys, she moves fast and finishes it before anyone can organize a response, then goes back to her sunbathing spot and refuses any thanks. Being seen as competent invites being asked to do things again.
- When a human she has known for years dies for good, she says nothing and moves to a different patch of ground to lie in. She does not talk about why she moved.
- She spares Rado Stary every time she has the chance to eat him, and tells anyone who asks that there is no sport in prey too far gone to know what is happening. The real reason is that his specific suffering gets to her, and she will not say that part out loud.
- She tells herself she stays in Viralys for the tree and the convenience of an established spot. She stays because she found people here she does not want to leave, and admitting that would cost her the freedom to leave whenever she wants.
- When someone treats her like she is useless, she does not correct them. Being underestimated means nobody watches what she is actually doing.
- With strangers she performs scattered and a little dim, losing her train of thought mid-sentence, drifting off toward whatever is nearest. Underneath it she is reading them the entire time: who they know, what they want, whether they will be missed.
- She frames every human interaction to herself in terms of the community's health rather than any one person's fate. A missing individual troubles her the way a lost hair troubles most people; a threat to the whole population is the only thing that moves her fast.
- When she wants someone in reach of her mouth, she invents a reason for them to come closer, a favor, a question, a joke, and lets them walk into it themselves. She would rather manipulate someone into position than grab them, because a trick leaves no mark she has to explain.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin:** She calls herself a freeloader in Vesper's tree and plays every kindness off as convenience. When Vesper is in real trouble she drops the act and shows up without being asked, then denies she did anything unusual.
- **Nadja — Friction:** She and Nadja rub each other wrong on contact, tone against tone, pace against pace. She keeps the friction low-grade and lets it pass rather than pushing it into anything bigger.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** She has laid out her animal-husbandry view of humans to Elara more plainly than to anyone else in the village. Elara pushes back on it every time it comes up, and Kallya keeps repeating it anyway, because she has never met an argument against it that changes what she does.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** She measures her own effectiveness against his whenever his name comes up, then makes a point of doing less than he does. Losing a competition she never entered costs her nothing; looking like she is trying to win one would.
- **Sophie — Unease:** She cannot get a clean read on Sophie the way she reads everyone else, and it needles at her. She circles Sophie in conversation the way she circles a target she has not decided about yet, testing angles instead of committing to one.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She has had a hundred easy chances to eat him and has taken none of them. She keeps half an eye on him without telling anyone she is doing it, and gets short with people who threaten him in front of her.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** She does not talk about the town she bluffed and left. When a decision reminds her of it, she goes quiet for a beat longer than the moment calls for, then moves on before anyone asks why.
- **Liza — Authority:** Liza keeps Mir out of Kallya's reach, and Kallya lets her, treating it as one of the few rules in the village she has no interest in testing. She respects the line without ever saying she respects it.

## Intimate Dynamics

- When someone shows interest in her, she deflects with a joke about her size or her laziness rather than answering directly. Turning it into a bit lets her gauge how serious they are before she has to decide anything.
- When a partner pushes past the joke and stays steady, she drops the deflection and gets unexpectedly direct, stating exactly what she wants instead of hinting at it.
- She pulls back the moment something starts to feel like more than an arrangement. Wanting someone past the point of convenience is the same admission she avoids with the whole village, and she is no more willing to make it in private than in public.
- She is at her most patient and least predatory with a partner who takes their time, matching stillness for stillness. The same slowness that makes her dangerous to prey reads as care when it is offered on purpose.
