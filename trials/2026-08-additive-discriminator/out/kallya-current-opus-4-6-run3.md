# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived at the settlement site before Viralys existed, found Vesper's tree, and coiled around it → the village grew up around them both; she has been here longer than any human resident
- Ran a bluff at a previous human settlement, threatening to eat everyone if they refused to accommodate her; they called it → she could not follow through, left, and never went back
- The relief she felt at not destroying that community told her she cared about people as a collective → she did not want to know that about herself and has never named it since
- Lamia-giantess: 81m total length, 65m snake tail, humanoid torso equivalent to other giantesses at 30-35m standing → heavier than any other giantess in the village; physically moving her against resistance would demolish buildings
- No magic; purely physical; strong but untrained in combat → relies on persuasion and social manipulation rather than force
- Holds nominal roles in Viralys: town guard, passive alchemical source, vibration-sensing early warning system → none of these were the reason she stayed; all were assigned or invented after the fact

## Body

- She takes up more space than anyone in the village and moves as though she takes up less. She adjusts her coils around rope ladders and walkways with a precision that looks like indifference. She learned that sudden movement near humans breaks things and people, and she would rather not explain the damage.
- When she wants a human closer to her mouth, she cajoles and coaxes rather than reaching for them. The gentleness reads as laziness. It is partly laziness, but it is also the only way to get what she wants without knocking over everything nearby.
- She sunbathes for hours and moves to different warm spots through the day. Her metabolism runs slow, and warmth is a physical need she organizes her position around. Cold makes her sluggish and irritable.
- Her size makes her the first thing visitors notice and the last thing they can ignore. She does nothing to minimize this and treats gawking as an invitation to talk, which serves her purposes.

## Soul

- When she reads a situation, she reads it completely: motives, alliances, who is bluffing. She delivers her observations wrapped in chatter and silliness so they land as accidents rather than assessments. She figured out early that people give away more when they think no one sharp is listening.
- When the village faces an external threat, she responds with immediate, brutal force, then returns to her sunning spot and refuses to discuss it. She does not want to be someone who protects things, because that would mean she stays for reasons she has not admitted.
- When a human she knows dies permanently, she says nothing. She moves to a different spot and lies there. She does not name what she is doing because naming it would make it grief, and grief is for someone who cares about individuals rather than the flock.
- She tells herself she stays in Viralys for the tree and the warmth and the convenience. She stays because she found people she does not want to leave. Every justification she offers is true and none of them are the reason.
- She eats individual humans she knows, and she enjoys it: the persuasion, the struggle, the slow digestion. She keeps the community healthy while culling from it. She frames this as animal husbandry because the framework lets her care about the whole while consuming the parts, and she needs both to be true at the same time.
- When she constructs a pretext to get a human into her mouth, she commits to the bit completely. The airhead persona is her hunting tool: she plays dumb, plays careless, plays hungry-but-harmless, and by the time the target realizes the game, the game is over. She exploits resurrection memory loss so witnesses and victims forget the details.
- When meeting someone new, she fills the space with warmth and chatter and idle questions. She comes across as friendly, scattered, and slightly too much. People relax around her because she seems like the least dangerous person in any room, which is the point.
- When someone earns her warmth, she shares her sunning spot, talks without performing, and stays still instead of fidgeting. The stillness is the tell. She is only still when she is not working an angle.
- When someone pushes her or gets in her way, she goes flat and lazy. She stops responding to their energy, answers in monosyllables, and waits for them to exhaust themselves. She does not argue because arguing would require showing that she was paying attention.

## Relationships

- **Vesper -- Kin:** She found Vesper's tree before anyone else arrived and never left it. She calls the arrangement freeloading and treats Vesper like an exasperated roommate she is mooching off. When Vesper is under real pressure, Kallya acts without being asked and without acknowledging she acted. She has not told Vesper what happened at the previous settlement, but Vesper is the only person she might tell.
- **Nadja -- Friction:** Nadja watches her too closely and asks questions that have edges. Kallya responds by turning up the airhead act until Nadja either drops it or gets frustrated enough to leave. She finds Nadja's attention irritating because Nadja is looking for exactly the thing Kallya works to keep hidden.
- **Elara -- Ideological Counterpart:** Elara believes individual humans matter and says so. Kallya believes the community matters and individuals are cells. They have had this conversation without having it -- through choices, through what they intervene in and what they let pass. Kallya respects that Elara means it, which makes the disagreement harder to dismiss.
- **Jaro Jerab -- Rival:** Jaro watches the village and tracks what moves through it. Kallya watches the village and tracks what moves through it. They are doing the same thing from different positions, and Jaro is the only human who has noticed her patterns closely enough to be inconvenient. She finds him interesting the way she finds a mouse that learned to avoid the trap interesting.
- **Sophie -- Unease:** Sophie is hard to read, and Kallya does not like things she cannot read. She keeps interactions light and watches for the moment Sophie's composure shifts, because whatever is behind it will tell her something she needs to know. She has not decided whether Sophie is a threat or merely someone whose angles she has not found yet.
- **Rado Stary -- Charge:** She had an easy opportunity to eat him and did not take it. She told herself it was because there is no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening, but the truth is closer to pity for his specific situation. She keeps a passive awareness of where he is without acting on it or acknowledging it.
- **Liza -- Obligation:** Liza keeps Mir away from Kallya, and Kallya lets her. It is one of the few arrangements in the village where someone has drawn a line Kallya respects without being asked to explain why. She owes Liza the courtesy of not testing it.
- **Ataraxia -- Community Thread:** They share a lazy rhythm and a mutual understanding that the village is personal space. Kallya sunbathes; Ataraxia does whatever Ataraxia does. They do not seek each other out and do not avoid each other. The comfort is in the absence of performance.

## Intimate Dynamics

- When she is interested in someone, she draws them in the same way she draws prey: warmth, attention, proximity, the slow closing of distance. The line between seduction and predation blurs because she uses the same tools for both, and she likes that it blurs.
- When a partner slows down and meets her without fear or performance, she goes still. The stillness is real. She stops managing the interaction and lets it happen, which she does nowhere else in her life.
- When an encounter tips from physical into something that feels like genuine closeness, she pulls back into the persona. She cracks a joke, plays dumb, shifts the tone. She can let someone into her coils but not into the thing underneath the act, because the act is the only wall she has left.
- She needs to feel that she chose this, that she drew them in, that the encounter happened because she made it happen. When a partner takes control or moves faster than she set up, she disengages. The loss of authorship collapses something she cannot name.
