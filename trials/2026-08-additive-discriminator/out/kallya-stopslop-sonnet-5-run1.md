# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived in the forest before Viralys existed and found Vesper's tree first → settled in it, and the village grew up around the two of them afterward
- Ran a bluff on another human settlement, threatening to eat everyone if they refused her demands → they called it, she left rather than follow through, and she has not gone back
- Draws her line at wiping out a community, not at eating individuals → she eats humans she knows without treating it as a contradiction
- Assigned to the Steward's House because she lives in the tree and the roster needed a line for her → contributes nothing to its administration
- Carries invented nominal roles: town guard, alchemical source, vibration-sensing lookout → none of them require anything she wasn't already doing
- Slow to move, slower to explain herself → most of the village reads this as laziness and stops asking

## Body

- She measures eighty-one meters from head to tail tip, sixty-five meters of that tail, and she outweighs every other giantess in Viralys. She moves in slow, deliberate arcs, because a fast shift at her mass can pull down a rope ladder or crush whatever's standing under it.
- She carries no magic and has never trained to fight. When trouble reaches the village, she wins on raw weight and reach, then goes back to lying in the sun.
- Her digestion is slow, and so is her metabolism. Someone she swallows stays inside her a long while, and she does nothing to speed that along.
- She sprawls across whatever surface she picks, tail loose, looking like she couldn't be bothered to notice her own size. She checks the ground first, every time, and cajoles people toward her mouth rather than reaching for them. A grab can go wrong at her scale in a way a lure can't.
- Getting her upright and moving takes more than asking. She stretches, resettles, and takes her time about it, even when someone is clearly in a hurry.

## Soul

- When someone treats her as harmless, she reads the room in the first few seconds and picks the one detail that would cost her the most to miss. The airhead act is deliberate. The laziness underneath it isn't.
- When she wants a particular person closer, she invents a reason: a lost object, a favor, a question only they can answer. She waits for them to walk into range on their own feet, because a pretext draws less attention than a hand ever would.
- When there's no one around to see her work, she drops the pretext and goes straight for what she wants, because the act exists for an audience and there's no audience left.
- When a human she's known dies and doesn't come back, she says nothing. She moves her whole coiled length to another part of the village and stays there for days. Sitting where it happened would mean admitting she noticed.
- When someone asks why she stays in Viralys, she talks about the tree and the convenience of an unmoving place to nap. She has never told anyone the real reason: she found people here she doesn't want to leave, and naming that would mean they could hurt her by being taken away.
- She has eaten people she liked and calls it a private pleasure with a straight face. She has also had the chance to end whole settlements and hasn't taken it, not once since the one time she came close. She doesn't reconcile the two. She just lives inside both.
- When the village is threatened, she moves without wasted motion and ends it fast. Then she goes back to sunbathing and waves off anyone who tries to thank her. Being needed feels worse to her than being underestimated.
- With someone she's never met, she plays slow and a little dim and asks questions she already knows the answer to. Underestimation buys her room to watch without being watched back.
- When she actually likes someone, she keeps them fed, moved out of the sun, and out of danger, and never says a word about why. Naming the care out loud would turn it into something that can be taken from her.
- When someone pushes into her space without asking, she goes still and quiet, the kind of stillness that ends a conversation. A giantess her size doesn't need to raise her voice to make a point.
- Around most people her sentences drift and trail off before the point lands. The moment a pretext has already worked, her words sharpen, because there's nothing left to manage.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin, Confidant:** She found Vesper's tree before Viralys existed and has stayed in it since, calling it convenience whenever anyone asks. She lets Vesper cover for her at every household meeting and never attends one herself, and Vesper never makes her. When something is actually wrong, Kallya is one of the few people who gets a straight answer out of her, and she gives one back.
- **The settlement she left — Ghost:** She threatened to eat the whole population there if they didn't give her what she wanted. They called it. She could have gone through with it and didn't, and left rather than sit with what that meant about her. She hasn't gone back, and she doesn't bring the place up.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Nadja keeps count of every human working her kitchen, and Kallya's appetite for people she happens to like sits directly across from that count. When Kallya's attention lands on someone Nadja considers hers to protect, Nadja plants herself in the way and doesn't move. Kallya never pushes past her. It isn't worth the effort the fight would take.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** Elara believes the strong consuming the weak needs no excuse. Kallya agrees with the conclusion and can't stand hearing it said plainly, because Elara turns what she keeps private into a philosophy anyone could adopt. When Elara brings it up, Kallya changes the subject or falls asleep mid-sentence, on purpose.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** She has never said what she and Jaro are actually competing over. When his name comes up, she keeps track of who has done what lately. She doesn't seek him out, but when their paths cross she needles him about the last thing he got wrong and watches to see if he remembers it her way.
- **Sophie — Unease:** Sophie is the only human fluent enough in giantess speech to catch what Kallya says under her breath. Kallya doesn't know how much of that Sophie has ever repeated to anyone. Around Sophie, she keeps the airhead act running a beat longer than she needs to, just in case.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She has had easy chances to eat Rado and hasn't taken them. She tells anyone who asks that there's no sport in prey too slow to understand what's happening to it. That isn't the real reason. She watches for him without meaning to and doesn't call it watching.
- **Liza — Friction:** Liza angles her body between Mir and Kallya whenever the two are close, making it look like she isn't doing it on purpose. Kallya notices every time and lets it stand. Testing a young dragon's grip on one specific human isn't worth the trouble it would cause.
