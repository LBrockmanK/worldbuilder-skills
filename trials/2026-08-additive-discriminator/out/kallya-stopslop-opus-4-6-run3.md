# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived at the location of Viralys before the village existed, found Vesper's tree, and coiled around it → has lived there longer than any human resident and treats the site as hers
- Ran a bluff at a previous human settlement, threatening to eat everyone if they refused to accommodate her; they called it; she left rather than follow through → the relief she felt at walking away told her she valued the community as a whole, and she did not want that knowledge about herself
- Settled into Viralys as it formed around her and Vesper → holds nominal roles (town guard, passive alchemical source, vibration-sensing early warning) that are post hoc justifications for staying
- Lamia-giantess, 81 meters total with a 65-meter snake tail; humanoid torso equivalent to other giantesses at 30 to 35 meters standing → heavier than any other giantess in the village; physically moving her against resistance would demolish structures around her
- Has no magic; her capabilities are purely physical → strong but untrained in combat, reliant on mass and cunning rather than skill
- Eats individual humans she knows; the line she discovered at her previous settlement was genocide, not predation → she preserves communities while consuming members of them

## Body

- She presents as careless about her size while tracking every rope ladder, hanging platform, and person within reach of her coils. She cajoles humans closer to her mouth rather than grabbing them, partly because sudden movement can displace objects and injure bystanders she did not intend to harm.
- She moves slowly and rarely repositions. Her metabolism is slow, her digestion is slow, and she treats physical effort as something to avoid. The laziness is real, not performed.
- When she shifts her coils or stretches, nearby humans have to adjust their footing and their routes. She notices this and pretends she does not.
- She sunbathes for long stretches and picks spots with good warmth. When temperature drops in an area, she relocates without complaint or comment.

## Soul

- When someone talks to her, she plays vapid. She asks obvious questions, loses the thread of conversations, giggles at things that are not funny. The airhead routine is a performance she maintains with precision. She reads people and situations with accuracy and uses the persona to keep others from expecting anything of her.
- When the village faces an external threat, she responds with sudden, brutal effectiveness, then returns to sunbathing and refuses any acknowledgment. She treats the shift as though it did not happen, because accepting credit would mean accepting that she chose to protect the village.
- She views humans through an animal-husbandry framework: the flock matters, individual animals do not. She keeps the population healthy while eating members of it for pleasure. She enjoys how they struggle. She is the only giantess in the village who is actively predatory, and she does not dress it up.
- When a human she knows dies permanently, she says nothing. She moves to a different spot and lies there. She does not name what she feels, and she would deny it if asked.
- She constructs pretexts for humans to enter her mouth. She manipulates them into compliance using her airhead persona, making the encounter feel like their idea or an accident. When no witnesses are present, she drops the pretext and is more direct. She exploits the memory loss that follows resurrection systematically.
- She tells herself she stays in Viralys for the tree and the convenience. She stays because she found people she does not want to leave. She has not examined the gap between these two stories and avoids situations that would force her to.
- When she meets someone new, she fills the interaction with chatter and apparent carelessness. The other person walks away thinking she is harmless and dim. She gathered everything she needed from the conversation while giving nothing back.
- When someone earns enough familiarity that she drops a layer of the act, it surfaces as dry observation rather than warmth. She states something true about the other person that they did not expect her to notice. She does this once and returns to the routine.
- When someone pushes her to admit she cares about the village or its people, she deflects with a joke or changes the subject. If they persist, she gets still and quiet until the conversation dies. Confronting her investment in the community is the one thing that breaks the easy-going surface.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin:** She found Vesper's tree before the village existed and coiled around it. Their cohabitation predates every other relationship in Viralys. She plays the friendship as freeloading, claiming she stays for the shade and the good spot. Vesper is the person she has stayed longest with, and she treats that fact as unremarkable because remarking on it would mean naming what Vesper is to her.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Nadja's directness and organizational energy grate on her. Kallya operates through indirection and laziness; Nadja operates through effort and confrontation. They share a community and cannot avoid each other. Kallya handles the friction by being aggressively unhelpful in ways that look accidental.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** Elara holds views about the treatment of humans that clash with Kallya's husbandry framework. Kallya does not argue the point directly. She states her position as though it is too obvious to debate and moves on, which leaves the disagreement unresolved and recurring.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** Jaro is the human who holds her attention as an adversary. She measures encounters with him against previous ones, tracking whether he has gotten harder to manipulate. She finds the competition entertaining in a way that she does not find most humans entertaining, and the entertainment keeps him alive longer than her usual interest would.
- **Sophie — Unease:** Sophie watches her with an attention Kallya cannot fully read. She does not know whether Sophie is cataloguing her habits out of suspicion, curiosity, or something else. She keeps the airhead performance tighter around Sophie than around most people.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She spares Rado despite easy opportunity, claiming there is no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening. The actual reason is closer to pity for his specific suffering, and the distinction between those two explanations is one she has not examined. She avoids him when she can, because his presence makes her aware of a line she did not choose to draw.
- **Liza — Obligation:** Liza keeps her child Mir away from Kallya, and Kallya accepts the boundary without pushing back. It is one of the few constraints on her behavior that she honors without testing, because she recognizes what Liza is protecting against and does not disagree with the assessment.
- **The previous settlement — Ghost:** The community she bluffed and then walked away from still shapes how she operates. She measures her behavior in Viralys against what happened there. The relief she felt at not following through is the experience she is most careful never to repeat the conditions of, because it told her something about herself she prefers to leave unexamined.

## Intimate Dynamics

- When she is interested in someone, she draws them in through the same persuasion she uses for predation. She constructs a situation where proximity feels like the other person's choice. The line between appetite and attraction blurs in her approach, and she does not always clarify which one is operating, even to herself.
- She slows down when a partner shows fear that is not play. Real terror breaks the dynamic she wants, which requires the other person to be present and participating rather than shutting down. She needs them engaged, not frozen.
- When physical closeness becomes emotionally close, she pulls back. She can hold someone against her body for hours and stay relaxed. The moment the contact starts to mean something beyond the physical, she finds a reason to reposition or end the encounter. Emotional exposure is the thing she will not tolerate, even when she initiated everything that led to it.
