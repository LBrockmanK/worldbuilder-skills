# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived before Viralys existed and settled into Vesper's tree → the village formed around the two of them
- Ran a bluff at a prior human settlement, threatening to consume the whole population if not accommodated → they called it, and rather than deliver on it she left
- Failing to go through with that threat → confirmed she cared about the community as a whole, not just herself, a fact she has not repeated to anyone
- Draws no magic and never trained in combat, relying on raw size and weight → the heaviest giantess in Viralys, capable of leveling the village by accident
- Metabolism and digestion run slow → anyone she swallows stays inside her for a long stretch
- Currently settled in Viralys with food, sun, and a place in Vesper's tree → calls the arrangement convenient and leaves it there

## Body

- At 81 meters stretched out, tail included, she is impossible to miss and makes no effort to be missed. She picks open, visible ground to sunbathe on rather than shade or a corner, because avoiding notice was never an option her size gave her.
- She lets her tail sprawl wherever it lands without checking first, but she never puts real weight into a step without checking the ground under it, because a wrong shift here can knock down rope ladders and hurt people who never got near her mouth.
- When she wants someone specific to come close, she talks them in rather than reaching for them. A sudden grab moves too much mass at once, and she has broken things by accident before.
- She reads a stranger's fear, greed, and bluff in the time it takes them to cross the square, and has never once misjudged when a human is about to run. She has no read at all on giantesses — Vesper, Nadja, any of them stay unreadable to her, and she does not try.

## Soul

- She plays dim in front of an audience — repeating herself, losing the thread, asking questions she already knows the answer to — because a target who thinks she isn't paying attention walks closer than one who knows she is.
- When she wants someone near her mouth, she invents an errand for it: something lost between her teeth, a task that only works from up close. She would rather build the reason than force the walk.
- She keeps a running sense of how many people Viralys can afford to lose. When the count feels thin, she goes longer between meals without announcing why; when it's not, she indulges more openly.
- When the village faces a real threat, she moves fast and finishes it before anyone else arrives, then goes back to the sun and refuses any thanks offered for it.
- When a human she knows dies for good, she says nothing about it and doesn't go looking for who did it or why. She picks a different patch of ground to lie on for a while, because staying in the same spot means noticing it's empty.
- She acts out the village's most useless resident — dodges every duty, naps through emergencies until the moment she doesn't — while reading every person who crosses her ground closely enough to know which one is lying and which one is about to bolt. The performance and the read never show at the same time.
- She tells anyone who asks that she stays in Viralys for the tree and the easy food, and repeats it even when no one asked. She has not once tried leaving to see whether the story holds up.
- With someone she's never met, she talks too much about nothing — the weather, the sunlight, whatever's underfoot — and lets them decide on their own that she isn't worth taking seriously.
- When someone she's decided she likes needs something small done, she gets it done without being asked twice, then complains loudly about the effort the whole time she's doing it.
- When someone pushes past the dim act and starts treating her like she's paying attention, she goes duller, not sharper. Attention is the one thing she won't reward.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin, Authority:** She settled into Vesper's tree before Viralys existed and calls staying there a matter of convenience to anyone who asks, including Vesper. When the tree needs something — room, quiet, a threat handled before it reaches the roots — she moves on it before anyone finishes explaining the problem, and refuses to call it help.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Nadja pushes for the village to move faster on everything, in ways that would put Kallya's stillness on public display. Kallya slows down further every time Nadja tries to speed her up, and calls it a coincidence.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** When Elara argues that giantesses owe humans protection without cost, Kallya doesn't argue back. She eats someone in front of her within the week instead. She isn't trying to make a point, but she isn't not making one either.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** Jaro treats every interaction like a contest over who can seem more useless while doing less. Kallya has never lost that contest and rearranges her whole day around not losing it if he's within sight.
- **Sophie — Unease:** Sophie asks careful questions and remembers the answers longer than anyone else in Viralys does. Kallya can't work out if that's caution or hunger dressed up as friendliness, and gives her wide berth without saying why.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She has had easy chances to eat him and hasn't taken any of them. She tells him there's no sport in prey too dull to know what's happening, but she has only ever given him that excuse — she has never spared anyone else and so never needed it again. Watching him specifically taken apart, and understanding every second of it, is the part she can't sit with.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** The place she left after her bluff got called doesn't get a name if it comes up. She measures every threat she makes now against whether she could actually deliver on it, because the last time she couldn't, she found out something about herself she didn't want to know.
- **Ataraxia — Confidant:** Ataraxia doesn't ask her to be anything other than motionless, and Kallya doesn't run the dim act around her — there's no one for it to work on. Ataraxia already knows and doesn't care enough to use it. It's the closest either of them gets to being honest with someone.
