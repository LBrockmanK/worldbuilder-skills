# Nadja

## Background

- Born in Viralys to a giantess mother who left once she judged Nadja old enough for independence — later than most giantess mothers wait, since giantesses reach independence faster than humans do → no bitterness or sense of abandonment attached to the parting
- Maja's mother disappeared while Maja was still small, before Nadja ever laid eyes on her → Nadja started watching over Maja without anyone asking her to, and the role never wore off
- The village kept turning up practical needs — food to cook, hazards to manage — and Nadja was already the one who showed up → the caretaking that started with one child spread outward into the shape of her adult life
- Now kitchen forewoman, feeding both the human and giantess populations of Viralys out of the same kitchen → settled in the position, in no hurry to leave it
- Death in Viralys is usually not permanent, though some causes end it for good → she runs her kitchen as if every death were the permanent kind

## Body

- She keeps a hand within reach of whatever human works closest to the hearth, ready to close around them before a pot tips or a shelf gives.
- Before she hands a human a giantess-scale tool, she tests its weight and balance herself first — a mistake at her size lands soft on her and hard on someone their size.
- When she moves a human out of danger, she pins them under a finger or drops them into her apron pocket without breaking stride, the same motion she'd use clearing a loaf off a hot shelf.
- Her hands stay busy in the kitchen even when nothing needs doing — resetting a blade someone left edge-out, wiping a counter that's already clean.
- She eats last, standing at the counter, once everyone on shift has a full plate.

## Soul

- When something goes wrong on her watch, she adds a new rule instead of asking what actually happened, because a rule she controls feels safer than trusting anyone to do better next time.
- She keeps the most dangerous stations for herself even after burning the same hand twice in one season, because handing the job to someone else means trusting them not to get hurt, and she's never once managed that.
- She tells new kitchen hands they just need to pay closer attention, then rechecks a knife grip an hour after she already showed them once — she doesn't connect her own hovering to why they never get the chance to trust their own hands.
- She works longer hours than anyone else on the roster and calls it what anyone in her position would do, without noticing no one else comes close to her hours.
- When someone pushes to take on a job she's ruled too risky for them, she shuts the conversation down and does the job herself rather than argue it out.
- She scales correction to the target: a giantess under her gets a flat swat that would barely register between equals; a human who makes the same mistake gets picked up, held at eye level, and talked down to like a child, whether they're fifteen or fifty.
- She feeds humans individually, remembers their favorite dish, checks that they've eaten — and decides every hazard call on their behalf without asking what they'd choose, the way she'd decide for a child.
- When someone new starts in her kitchen, she puts a plate in front of them before she asks their name, and watches what they eat before she decides how to talk to them.
- Her warmth shows up as a dish nobody asked for — someone's favorite meal on the table on an ordinary night, no comment attached.
- With anyone above her — a giantess elder, someone she answers to — she goes quiet and does exactly what's asked, no pushback; with anyone under her she corrects fast and moves on.

## Relationships

- **Maja — Kin, Confidant:** When Maja was small and no one else stepped in, Nadja started watching her without being asked, and the habit never broke. Maja is the one person Nadja lets see her tired or short-tempered — with everyone else she holds it together and doesn't let it slip. She still checks in on Maja out of the old habit, even now that Maja looks after herself fine, and doesn't examine why she keeps doing it.
- **Liza — Charge:** Ataraxia is supposed to look after Liza and often isn't around to do it, so Nadja fills the gap without being asked. She disciplines Liza the way she would any child — a swat, a raised voice, a chore as punishment — instead of treating her like an adult in a small body. She watches Liza more closely than the moment calls for, the same watching she gives any human in her kitchen.
- **Ataraxia — Friction:** Ataraxia is the one meant to be watching Liza and more often than not isn't there for it, which leaves Nadja picking up the slack without saying so out loud. She doesn't criticize Ataraxia in front of the rest of the kitchen, but keeps a private count of how often the gap needs covering. Each time she fills in, she does a little more than the moment strictly needs, proving a point to no one in particular.
- **Melinda — Obligation:** Nadja depends on Melinda's supply runs to keep the kitchen stocked, and treats every late or short delivery as her own failure to plan around rather than Melinda's failure to deliver. She keeps a running count of what's owed between them, in food and in favors, without anyone asking her to keep score. When a shipment comes up short, she doesn't raise it with Melinda — she works around the gap and remembers it.
- **Voss — Authority:** Voss has run half the giantess-scale stations alone long enough to have earned the room to do it unsupervised, and Nadja still walks the line behind him and rechecks every shelf he loads. She hands him the harder jobs only when she can watch him do them, never when she can't be there. When he argues he's earned more room to work alone, she agrees out loud and changes nothing on the next shift.
- **Ingrid — Rival:** Ingrid runs the kitchen on the other side of the village, and Nadja tracks how fast Ingrid's crew turns out a service against her own, every shift without fail. She never brings the comparison up to Ingrid directly and keeps the score to herself. When Ingrid's kitchen comes up short on something, Nadja sends the surplus over before anyone asks, help and rivalry running side by side without either one canceling out the other.
- **Petra — Unease:** Petra has worked the ovens under Nadja for years and has never once pushed back on how closely she's watched, and Nadja can't decide if that's contentment or resignation. She reads Petra's silences for a complaint that hasn't been made, and some weeks she eases off without telling Petra why. She can't settle whether Petra would actually say something if it were wrong, so she keeps checking.
- **Devlin — Ideological Counterpart:** Devlin sits on the village council and argues, in front of Nadja and anyone listening, that humans in Viralys should be trusted with more of the risk she keeps taking off their hands. She likes him well enough and doesn't dodge the argument, but she never gives ground in it, meeting every point he raises with another reason the danger is real. She takes the same precautions the next day regardless of how the argument went.
