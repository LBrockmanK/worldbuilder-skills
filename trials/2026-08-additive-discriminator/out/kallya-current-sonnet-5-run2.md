# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived in the region before Viralys existed and settled into the tree that later became Vesper's home → the village grew up around the two of them, not the reverse
- Ran the same bluff at a settlement before this one, threatening to eat everyone who wouldn't take her in → they called it, she couldn't go through with wiping out a community she knew, and she left without ever explaining why
- Grew up drawing a hard line between eating individuals and destroying a whole community → she still eats humans she knows one at a time without a second thought
- Her town-guard duty, her role as an alchemical-ingredient source, and her vibration-sensing watch all started as pretexts to get individual humans within reach of her mouth → the pretexts stuck around and became her recognized jobs
- No magic, no training in combat, nothing but size and raw strength → she has never needed either because no one has ever forced the question
- Heaviest giantess in Viralys by a wide margin, 81 meters from head to tail-tip, 65 of that tail → the village has never once moved her anywhere she didn't choose to go on her own
- Formally housed under the Steward's House because she lives in the tree and the paperwork needed somewhere to put her → she does none of the work that title implies and no one has ever pressed her on it

## Body

- She lets herself be moved slowly and only when she agrees to it. Anyone who tries to rush her out of a doorway waits instead, because a sudden shove against something her size does more damage to the building than the argument was worth.
- She keeps her tail coiled around her own sunning spot rather than laid across a path, and uncoils it herself the moment someone needs to get by, before they have to ask.
- She never grabs. When she wants a person closer to her mouth she talks them there — a dropped item, a joke, an excuse to lean in — because one fast motion from a hand her size snaps a rope ladder or crushes whatever's underneath.
- She eats without hurrying and doesn't try to speed up what comes after. Her digestion runs as slow as she does, so there's no reason to rush and she doesn't pretend otherwise.
- She sunbathes stretched out in the open, limbs loose, looking too heavy and too idle to bother watching. The stillness is genuine. Everything she does on top of it is not.

## Soul

- When someone underestimates her because she looks lazy, she lets the mistake stand and files away exactly how they got it wrong, for the next time the mistake is useful to her.
- When a stranger is easy to steer toward her mouth, she does it without hesitation. When it's someone she's actually spent time with, she pauses half a beat longer than the excuse needs — the flock-not-the-cell logic she runs on only stays clean while the person in front of her is still a stranger.
- She had Rado Stary within easy reach once and let him walk away, telling him afterward there was no sport in prey too confused to know what was happening to it. That wasn't the real reason. Watching him fail to understand made her feel sorry for him in a way she doesn't let herself feel toward most people, and she hasn't put him in that position since.
- She tells herself she stays in Viralys because of the tree, the sun, and how little effort it takes. She's turned down chances to leave that would have cost her nothing, because she found people here she doesn't want to lose, and that's not a fact she lets herself sit with for long.
- She runs the useless-airhead act on the whole Steward's House — forgets names, wanders off mid-task, lets people write her off — and underneath it she keeps a running account of who passes near her, who's alone, who wouldn't be missed for a day. The laziness is real. The airhead is not.
- When the village faces a real threat, she moves faster and harder than anyone who's watched her sunbathe for a week would expect, and the instant it's over she goes back to her spot and waves off anyone who tries to thank her.
- With strangers she plays a beat behind, asking a question everyone else already answered and letting the silence sit until they fill it, because people say more to someone they don't think is really listening.
- When someone she's decided she likes is struggling, she gets up from her sun-spot for them unprompted and won't give a reason if asked.
- When someone she knew dies permanently, she says nothing about it. She gets up, moves to a different part of the village, and stays there a while. She doesn't explain the move and no one presses her on it.
- She talks slow and lets sentences trail off before finishing them, then echoes back the last few words someone else said, like she's only just catching up. It buys her an extra beat to read the room before she answers for real.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin, Authority:** She found Vesper's tree before there was a village around it, and she's lived in it ever since on Vesper's terms, not her own. She calls it freeloading to anyone who asks and has never once corrected herself in front of Vesper, even though the friendship underneath the joke has outlasted every excuse she's given for staying.
- **Nadja — Friction:** When Kallya wanders into the kitchen to steal food and lie down across the doorway, Nadja works around her in silence and complains loudly to everyone else the second she's gone. Kallya finds this funny enough to do it again the following week.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** When Elara argues that a human taken by wildlife has simply met the natural order, Kallya doesn't argue the eating — she argues the waste. She'll defend the flock's survival to Elara at length and has never once defended an individual human's right not to be eaten.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** Jaro takes pride in being first to raise the alarm at the gate. Kallya's vibration-sensing duty started as a joke and she's never told him how often she felt trouble coming before he called it. She keeps her own tally and checks it every time he beats his chest about a warning.
- **Sophie Liska — Unease:** Sophie doesn't flinch around her the way most humans do, and the slow-to-catch-on act has never once worked on her. Kallya can't tell if Sophie has worked out what she actually is or simply doesn't scare, and she hasn't found a way to ask without answering the question herself.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She keeps track of where he works without meaning to and steers the conversation away from him whenever another giantess jokes about eating him. She's never told him why and has no plan to.
- **Strelitzia — Friction:** Strelitzia keeps Mir at a distance whenever the two of them are anywhere near each other, and Kallya lets it happen, mostly because watching a young dragon try to out-territory a lamia three times her size without saying so is worth the entertainment. She's never gone near Mir and has never told Strelitzia that.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** She ran the same bluff there and they called it. She left rather than follow through and hasn't said the settlement's name out loud since. When something in Viralys echoes it — a debt left uncollected, a warning left unheeded — she goes quiet for the rest of the day.
