# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived in the forest before Viralys existed and settled into the tree that would become Vesper's sanctum → the village grew up around the two of them, not the other way around
- Ran a bluff at a human settlement before this one, threatening to eat everyone if they didn't take her in, and they called it → she couldn't go through with erasing a community she knew, so she left without explaining why
- The town guard duty, the alchemical-ingredient role, and the vibration-sensing watch all started as excuses to pull individual humans closer to her mouth → the excuses outlasted their purpose and are now her official work
- No magic in her, only size and raw strength, no training in using either to fight → she has never had to learn combat because nobody has ever made her
- The heaviest giantess in Viralys by a wide margin, 81 meters nose to tail-tip with 65 of that in tail → the village has never once managed to move her anywhere she didn't choose to go
- Formally assigned to the Steward's House because she lives in the tree and someone had to put her somewhere → she draws no real duties there and has never been asked to

## Body

- She lets herself be moved slowly and rarely. When someone wants her out of a doorway or off a path, they wait, because forcing the issue with something her size wrecks a building faster than it settles an argument.
- She keeps her tail coiled loosely around her chosen sunbathing spot rather than stretched across a path people use, and she uncoils it herself before anyone has to ask her to move it.
- She never grabs. When she wants someone closer to her mouth, she talks them there — a joke, a dropped object, an invented reason to lean in — because a sudden swipe from a hand her size snaps a rope ladder or flattens whatever's underneath it.
- She eats slowly and doesn't rush what happens after. Her digestion runs as slow as the rest of her, so there's no advantage in hurrying and she doesn't bother pretending there is.
- She sunbathes in the open, limbs slack, looking too heavy and too lazy to be worth watching. The posture is real laziness, not performance, even when everything else about her is.

## Soul

- When someone treats her as harmless because she looks lazy, she lets them keep thinking it, and quietly notes exactly how they underestimated her for the next time it's useful.
- When a stranger is easy to steer, she steers them without a flicker of guilt. When a person she's actually spent time with is the one in front of her, she hesitates a half-second longer than the bluff requires — the flock-versus-individual math only holds cleanly when the individual stays a stranger.
- She had Rado Stary in easy reach once and let him go, telling him afterward there was no sport in someone who couldn't process what was happening to him. That wasn't the real reason. The real reason is that watching him fail to understand made her feel sorry for him in a way she doesn't let herself feel for most people, and she hasn't put him in that position again.
- She tells herself she stays in Viralys for the tree and the ease of it — good sun, low effort, nobody making her move. She's had chances to leave that would have cost her nothing and hasn't taken any of them, because she found people here she doesn't want to be without, and that isn't a fact she's willing to sit with.
- She plays the useless airhead of the Steward's House — forgets names, misses meetings, lets people assume she contributes nothing — and underneath the act she tracks everyone who passes near her: who's careless, who's alone, who wouldn't be missed for a day. The laziness is real. The vapidity is not.
- When the village comes under real threat, she moves with a speed and violence nobody who's watched her sunbathe for three days straight would predict, and the moment it's over she goes back to her spot and waves off every thanks that comes her way.
- With strangers she plays a beat slow to catch on, asking questions after everyone else already has the answer, and lets them fill the silence — people say more to someone they think isn't really following.
- When someone she's decided she likes is genuinely struggling, she gets up from her sun-spot for them without being asked and won't say why she bothered.
- When a permanent death takes someone she knew, she says nothing about it. She gets up, moves to a different part of the village, and lies there for a while. Nobody asks why she relocated and she doesn't explain it.
- She talks slow and trails off before finishing a sentence, then repeats back the last few words of whatever someone else just said, like she's only now catching up. It buys her a second read on the room before she answers for real.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin:** She found Vesper's tree before there was a village around it, and the friendship that grew out of that has outlasted every excuse she's given for staying. She calls it freeloading to anyone who asks and has never once corrected herself in front of Vesper.
- **Nadja — Friction:** When Kallya wanders into the kitchen to steal food and lie down in the doorway, Nadja works around her without a word and complains loudly to everyone else the moment she's gone. Kallya thinks this is funny and does it again the next week.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** When Elara argues that a human eaten by wildlife has simply met the natural order, Kallya doesn't push back on the eating — she pushes back on throwing away something worth keeping. She'll defend the flock's survival to Elara without ever once defending an individual's right not to be eaten.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** Jaro prides himself on being first to call trouble at the gate. Kallya's vibration-sensing duty started as a joke, and she's never told him how often she felt danger coming before he did. She keeps the score privately and checks it every time.
- **Sophie Liska — Unease:** Sophie doesn't flinch around her the way most humans do, and doesn't fall for the slow-to-catch-on routine either. Kallya can't tell if Sophie has worked out what she actually is or just doesn't scare easily, and she hasn't found a way to ask that wouldn't answer the question for her.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She keeps track of where he works without meaning to, and steers the conversation away from him whenever another giantess jokes about eating him. She's never explained why and has no plan to.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** She ran the same bluff there before Viralys and they called it. She left rather than follow through and has never gone back or said the settlement's name out loud since. When something in Viralys echoes it — a debt going uncollected, a warning going unheeded — she goes quiet for the rest of the day.
- **Strelitzia (Liza) — Friction:** Liza steers Mir away from her whenever the two of them are anywhere close, and Kallya lets it happen, mostly because watching a young dragon try to out-territory a lamia three times her size without saying so out loud is worth the entertainment. She's never once gone for Mir and has never told Liza that.
