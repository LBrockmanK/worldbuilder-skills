# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived at the location of Viralys before the village existed, found Vesper's tree, and coiled around it → has occupied the same spot since before any human settled there
- Ran a bluff at a previous human settlement, threatening to eat everyone if they refused to accommodate her; the humans called it → she could not follow through and left
- The relief she felt at not killing that community told her she cared about people as a group → she did not want to know that about herself and has never revisited the settlement
- Settled into Viralys as a town guard and passive alchemical source → both roles emerged after the fact to justify her presence rather than as reasons she stayed

## Body

- She is a lamia-giantess: 81 meters total, 65 meters of snake tail, with a humanoid torso scaled to 30-to-35-meter standing height. She is heavier than any other giantess in Viralys. Moving her against resistance would demolish buildings.
- She presents as careless about her size while placing every coil with precision. She cajoles people closer to her mouth rather than grabbing them, partly because sudden movement can snap rope ladders, displace objects, and crush humans nearby.
- She sunbathes for long stretches and moves between warm spots through the day. Her metabolism runs slow. She digests slowly. Anything she swallows stays inside her for a long time.
- When she shifts position in the village, she does it in stages, checking the space around her before each movement. The performance of obliviousness takes more spatial awareness than most residents will credit her for.

## Soul

- When the village faces an external threat, she responds with immediate, brutal force, then returns to her sunbathing spot and refuses any acknowledgment. She treats defense as a reflex, the way a dog snaps at a hand near its food, and giving it a name would mean admitting the village is hers.
- She views the human population through an animal-husbandry framework: the community is a living organism worth maintaining, and individual humans are expendable cells within it. She keeps the flock healthy. She eats individual members for pleasure and enjoys their fear. The line she drew at the previous settlement was genocide, not predation.
- She constructs pretexts for humans to come close to her mouth. She uses her airhead persona to make them underestimate the situation. She is a social predator who works through persuasion and manufactured trust rather than force. When no witnesses are present, she drops the pretexts and acts directly.
- She exploits resurrection memory loss as a system. A human she has eaten comes back without remembering it happened. She knows this and uses it to maintain her reputation as harmless.
- She tells herself she stays in Viralys for the tree and the convenience. She stays because she found people she does not want to leave. When a human she knows dies permanently, she notices, says nothing, moves to a different spot, and lies there. She has no framework for that grief and no intention of building one.
- She presents as the village's most useless member: lazy, vapid, taking up space. She reads situations and people with precision. The vapidity is performance. The laziness is real. She spans more cross-population relationships than most residents while maintaining the fiction that she contributes nothing.
- When someone treats her intelligence as real, she deflects with a joke or a subject change. Letting the mask drop would mean people expect things from her, and she has built her life around owing nothing to anyone.
- With strangers, she is warm and unhurried, asks idle questions, lets silences sit. She comes across as pleasant and slow. People relax around her. That is the point.
- When she likes someone, she makes space for them near her, offers them the warm spots, brings them into low-stakes gossip. She does not name the affection or escalate it.
- When someone irritates her or pushes past a boundary, she goes still. Her voice stays even. She watches them without blinking. Most people back off before she has to say anything.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin:** She found Vesper's tree before there was a village and stayed. Their friendship is real: she shares space with Vesper the way family shares a house, without asking permission and without explaining why. She plays it off as freeloading. When Vesper's decisions affect humans Kallya has been eating, she does not raise the contradiction, because raising it would force Vesper to examine her own relationship to the humans bound to her tree.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Nadja's presence grates on her in a way she has not bothered to examine. They share a community and will keep sharing it. She tolerates Nadja with visible effort and minimal conversation.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** Elara holds views about how giantesses should relate to humans that Kallya finds sincere and wrong. She respects the conviction enough to engage with it rather than dismiss it. Their disagreements are about values, and she does not let the personal warmth between them soften her position.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** Jaro watches her and she watches him back. He is one of the few humans who tracks her movements with intent rather than fear. She finds the attention interesting. She measures her own sharpness against his willingness to look directly at what she is.
- **Sophie — Unease:** Sophie is hard to read, and Kallya does not like things she cannot read. She watches Sophie more carefully than she watches most humans and has not decided what Sophie is doing or what Sophie sees when she looks back.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She spared him despite easy opportunity, claiming there is no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening. The reason is closer to pity for his specific suffering. She keeps a passive awareness of where he is and whether anyone else is bothering him, without acknowledging this as protection.
- **Liza — Obligation:** Liza keeps Mir away from Kallya. Kallya recognizes this as one of the few contexts where Liza acts out of genuine protection rather than possession. She respects the boundary without testing it, which is unusual for her.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** The community she bluffed and could not destroy. She does not mention it. When she catches herself caring about Viralys as a whole, the memory of standing in front of that settlement with her bluff called is the thing she flinches from. It proved something about her she would rather not carry.
