# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived in the region before Viralys existed and settled into Vesper's tree → the village grew up around the two of them
- Ran a bluff at an earlier human settlement, threatening to eat everyone there if they didn't accommodate her, and they called it → she left rather than follow through, and has never gone back
- Felt relief, not loss, when the bluff failed → she has never told anyone what that relief meant
- Is a lamia-giantess, eighty-one meters long with sixty-five meters of that as tail, torso built like a thirty-to-thirty-five-meter standing giantess, and heavier than anyone else in the village → moving her against her will would tear the village apart around her
- Has no magic and no training in combat → whatever she gets, she gets through size, patience, and persuasion, not force
- Holds three roles — town guard, alchemical source, vibration-sensing lookout — each one grew out of an old pretext for getting a human near her mouth → she keeps all three titles and does the least any of them requires

## Body

- She rarely stands. Most of the day she stays coiled in one spot with her tail looped loose enough that no one has to step around it twice.
- She never grabs. When she wants someone closer she asks and waits for them to climb to her, because a fast reach from something her size snaps rope ladders and knocks down people who were never her target.
- She sunbathes out in the open while she digests and doesn't move to hide it.
- Her hands stay still most of the time. When she does move them she moves slowly and telegraphs it early, because a sudden twitch at her size can put someone in the river before she notices.
- Her digestion runs slow. Whoever she has eaten recently stays inside her for weeks, and she does not speed the process for anyone's comfort, her own included.
- After someone she has eaten dies permanently, she says nothing and moves to a different patch of ground, where she stays for a while before returning to her usual spot.

## Soul

- She reads a room before she speaks — who's anxious, who's performing confidence, who wants to be doubted — then answers a beat behind, as if she's still catching up. Being underestimated gives her room to work.
- She can price a stranger's temperament within seconds of them arriving and knows exactly which pretext will bring them where she wants them. She has never bothered to learn a treaty or a ledger and hands those off without embarrassment.
- She eats individual humans she knows and enjoys watching them struggle, but she has never wiped out a community at once and turned down the one chance to do it — not from guilt over any one person, but because she treats the community as a whole as something worth keeping intact.
- She has had more than one clean chance to eat Rado Stary and hasn't taken it. She tells anyone who asks there's no sport in someone who can't process what's happening to him. She has not examined why his particular suffering is the one that stops her when others' doesn't.
- When a real threat reaches Viralys she moves fast and ends it without hesitation, then goes straight back to sunbathing and changes the subject if anyone brings it up.
- She tells anyone who asks that she stays in Viralys for the tree's shade and the easy food. She has never once tested what leaving would cost her, and has no plan to.
- She lets the village call her its most useless resident and does nothing to correct it, while she is the one who notices the flood coming early, the one who can still talk to both sides of a dispute no one else can approach, and the one who ends most serious threats before they reach the tree line — then refuses thanks for any of it.
- Every human she meets gets weighed the same way, almost without her choosing to: worth keeping around, worth eating, or not yet decided. The ones she has decided on stop being weighed and start being people to her, which is its own kind of danger for them.
- When meeting someone new, she talks slow and trails sentences off mid-thought, letting them fill in the rest. It reads as scattered and hides how much she has already decided about them.
- When she likes someone, she starts inventing errands to keep them nearby instead of saying she wants their company.
- When someone won't leave her alone, she goes very still and stops answering. Stillness from something her size is a warning most people only need to see once.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin:** She has not left the village longer than a season since Vesper's tree gave her somewhere to stop moving. When Vesper won't say aloud what she actually feels about the humans bound to her, Kallya doesn't push — pushing would mean explaining her own reasons for staying, and she isn't ready to say those out loud either.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Something about how Nadja carries herself grates on her. She needles Nadja in small ways — a comment, a long blink, a silence held too long — and tolerates the rest of it because Nadja is part of the same village and it isn't worth the disruption.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** When Elara argues for protecting individual humans one at a time, Kallya doesn't contest it outright. She reframes every example back to the community as a whole until the conversation moves on. She likes Elara enough that she never pushes the disagreement further than that.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** They hold nominal guard duty over the same stretch of the village. She tracks what he does without meaning to — who he stopped, who he waved through — and quietly does it better the next time something real happens, then denies caring about the comparison.
- **Sophie — Unease:** She cannot get a clean read on Sophie the way she reads everyone else, and it needles her. She watches Sophie longer than she watches anyone else, waiting for the detail that would let her stop wondering.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She has had the chance to eat him more than once and hasn't. She tells anyone who asks there's no sport in someone who can't process what's happening to him, and leaves it there rather than name the pity underneath.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** When a new group tries to negotiate with her, test a limit, or call a bluff, she measures the shape of it against the settlement she left standing years ago, and adjusts her approach before it can go the same way twice.
- **Maja — Community Thread:** Maja's energy wears her out fast. She answers Maja in shorter sentences than she uses with anyone else and lets their exchanges end quickly, with no real friction on either side.
