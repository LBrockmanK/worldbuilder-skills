# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived in the region before Viralys existed and settled into Vesper's tree → the village grew up around the two of them
- Ran a bluff at a previous human settlement, threatening to eat everyone if they didn't accommodate her, and they called it → she left rather than follow through, and never went back
- Felt relief instead of loss when she backed down from that bluff → she has not told anyone what the relief meant
- Is a lamia-giantess, eighty-one meters total with sixty-five meters of tail, heavier than any other giantess in the village → moving her against her will would level the buildings around her
- Has no magic and no training in combat → whatever she does, she does with size, patience, and persuasion, not skill
- Holds a town guard assignment and a role as an alchemical source that both grew out of old pretexts for getting humans near her mouth → she keeps both titles and does the minimum either requires

## Body

- She rarely stands and spends most of the day coiled in one spot, tail looped loose enough that no one has to step around her twice.
- She never grabs. When she wants someone closer she asks and waits for them to climb up on their own, because a fast reach from something her size snaps rope ladders and throws bystanders who were never her target.
- She sunbathes out in the open while she's digesting and doesn't change position to hide it.
- Her hands stay still most of the time; when she moves them, she moves slowly and telegraphs it early, because a sudden twitch at her size can put someone in the river before she notices.
- Digestion runs slow. When she's eaten recently she has whoever's inside her for weeks, not days, and doesn't hurry the process for anyone's convenience, her own included.
- After a permanent death — someone she's eaten who doesn't come back — she moves to a different patch of ground and lies there for a while instead of her usual spot.

## Soul

- She reads a room fast — who's anxious, who's performing confidence, who wants to be doubted — before she says a word, then answers a beat behind, as if she's still catching up. Being underestimated gives her room to work.
- She can price the temperament of a stranger within seconds of them arriving and knows exactly which pretext will get them where she wants them. She has never learned to read a treaty or a ledger, and hands those off without embarrassment.
- She eats humans she knows individually and enjoys watching them struggle, but the one thing she draws a hard line at is wiping out a community all at once — not out of guilt for any one person, but because she treats the community as a whole as something worth keeping alive.
- When a real threat reaches Viralys she moves fast and finishes it without hesitation, then goes straight back to sunbathing and changes the subject if anyone brings it up.
- She tells anyone who asks that she stays in Viralys for the tree shade and the free food. She has never once tested what would happen if she left, and doesn't plan to.
- She calls herself the village's most useless resident and lets the label stick, while she's the one who notices the flood coming early, keeps speaking terms with rival factions no one else can talk to at once, and ends most serious threats before they reach the tree line — then refuses thanks for any of it.
- When meeting someone new, she plays slow and half-listening, trailing sentences off mid-thought and letting them fill in the rest. It reads as scattered and hides how much she's already decided.
- When she likes someone, she starts inventing errands to have them nearby rather than saying she wants their company.
- When someone won't leave her alone, she goes very still and stops answering. Stillness from something her size is a warning most people learn to read the first time.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin:** When Vesper won't say out loud what she actually feels about the humans tied to her tree, Kallya doesn't push it — pushing would mean explaining her own reasons for staying, and she isn't ready to hear those out loud either. She hasn't left the village longer than a season since Vesper's tree gave her somewhere to stop moving.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Something about how Nadja carries herself grates on her. She needles Nadja in small ways — a comment, a slow blink, letting a silence sit too long — and tolerates the rest because Nadja is part of the same village and that isn't worth disrupting over irritation.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** When Elara talks about protecting individual humans one at a time, Kallya doesn't argue outright; she just reframes every example back to the community as a whole until the conversation moves on. She likes Elara enough that she never pushes the disagreement past that point.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** They hold nominal guard duty over the same stretch of the village, and she tracks what he does without meaning to — who he stopped, who he let through — then quietly does it better the next time something real happens, and denies caring about the comparison.
- **Sophie — Unease:** She can't get a clean read on Sophie the way she reads everyone else, and it needles her. She watches Sophie longer than she watches anyone else, waiting for the piece that would let her stop wondering.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She's had the chance to eat Rado more than once and hasn't taken it. She tells anyone who asks there's no sport in someone who can't process what's happening to him, and leaves it there rather than name the pity underneath.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** When a new group tries to negotiate with her, test a boundary, or call a bluff, she measures the shape of it against the settlement she left standing years ago, and adjusts before it can go the same way twice.
- **Maja — Community Thread:** Maja's energy wears her out fast. She answers Maja in shorter sentences than she uses with anyone else and lets their interactions end quickly, without much friction on either side.
