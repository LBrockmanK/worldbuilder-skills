# Nadja

## Background

- Born in Viralys; her mother left once she judged Nadja ready to manage alone, later than some giantesses wait but far sooner than a human parent would → she holds no resentment about it
- Maja's mother vanished before anyone in the village saw her raise Maja → Nadja took up a big-sister role with Maja that widened into caretaking for the whole village
- The village's daily needs kept growing while the caretaking habit took hold → she never set out to become the person who feeds everyone; the role built itself around her
- Runs the kitchen as forewoman, feeding both humans and giantesses → she holds together the daily operation that keeps both populations fed and safe

## Body

- When a human strays into danger, she plucks them up between two fingers before they finish protesting, because at her size the correction takes half a second and a shout takes longer.
- She sets a misbehaving human on a shelf or tucks them into her apron pocket while she finishes what she's doing, then pulls them back down for a talking-to.
- She keeps one hand near the stove and the other free, so she can grab anyone who gets too close without looking away from what she's cooking.
- Her hands rarely stop moving. She kneads, stirs, and ties things off between sentences, and she notices when a knife grip or a chopping angle doesn't match how she'd do it.

## Soul

- When someone under her watch takes a risk she considers avoidable, she takes over the task herself instead of explaining the danger, because she believes only her own hands can guarantee nobody gets hurt.
- She tells new cooks to use their own judgment, then corrects their knife work before they finish the first cut, because watching someone learn by getting it wrong costs more than she can stand to risk.
- When a mistake happens on her watch, she adds another rule and another checkpoint the same week the last round of rules produced the mistake, because she trusts tighter control over the pattern that keeps failing.
- She believes everyone around her is careless and needs more oversight. She is the one who volunteers for the tasks she won't hand off, and she is the one who runs short on sleep most nights.
- She fills a bowl with someone's favorite dish and sets it down without comment, then walks off before they can thank her.
- Her discipline scales with the person in front of her. Maja gets a shove or a smack because she can take it, a child gets a scolding and a swat, and a human gets pinned under a finger and held up for a talking-down.
- When someone new starts in her kitchen, she gives them the safest task in the room and watches them do it twice before she lets them near anything sharper.
- When workers start laughing and playing during a shift, she barks at them to get back to work, then hands out a lighter punishment than she thinks she gave.

## Relationships

- **Maja — Kin, Confidant:** When Maja teases her in front of the staff, she lets it land and doesn't correct it, because Maja is the one person she doesn't manage. She tells Maja things she edits out for everyone else, and she still swats Maja on the shoulder for messing around near the stove the same way she did when Maja was small.
- **Strelitzia-Liza — Charge:** She checks on Liza's chores without being asked and steps in before Liza finishes explaining a plan she doesn't like. She disciplines Liza the way a parent handles a child, not a subordinate, because no one else has claimed the job.
- **Melinda — Obligation:** She tracks every delivery Melinda's suppliers owe the kitchen against every favor she's called in to cover a shortfall. When a shipment runs late, she says nothing to Melinda directly and reworks the week's meals around the gap instead, keeping the debt unspoken but counted.
- **Her mother — Ghost:** When she decides someone in her care is finally ready to be left alone, she measures the decision against how long her mother waited before leaving her. She hasn't matched that patience yet, and she doesn't say why she keeps trying.
- **Ataraxia — Authority:** She fills the gaps Ataraxia leaves without complaint or acknowledgment, taking on decisions that aren't hers to make. When someone asks where Ataraxia is, she answers the question and changes the subject before anyone can ask if the arrangement bothers her.
- **Tomas — Friction:** She won't let Tomas run the stove alone even after three years of proving himself, and she takes the pot back from him mid-task more often than his actual mistakes warrant. He stops arguing about it every time. She never explains why.
