# Nadja

## Background

- Born in Viralys to a giantess mother who left once she judged Nadja old enough for independence, staying longer than most giantess mothers do → no trauma or bitterness attached to the parting
- Maja's mother disappeared before Nadja was old enough to remember seeing her → Nadja took up watching over Maja and grew into an unplanned big-sister role
- The village's day-to-day needs kept surfacing around her → the caretaking that started with Maja spread outward until it shaped the whole of her adult life
- Now forewoman of the kitchen that feeds both the human and giantess populations of Viralys → settled in the position, in no hurry to leave it

## Body

- In the kitchen she keeps a hand within reach of whatever human is working closest to the hearth, ready to close around them before a pot tips or a shelf gives way.
- She checks a giantess-scale task twice before handing tools to a human — testing the weight, the balance, the load — because a mistake at her size lands hard on someone at theirs.
- When she moves a human somewhere for their own safety, she pins them under a finger or drops them into her apron pocket and keeps walking, the same motion she'd use with a loaf of bread.
- Her hands never stop moving in the kitchen — stirring, wiping, resetting a knife someone left edge-out — even when nothing needs doing.
- She eats standing up, last, after everyone else on shift has a plate.

## Soul

- When something goes wrong on her watch, she adds a rule rather than reviewing what happened, tightening the process for everyone instead of addressing the one mistake.
- When she walks the kitchen floor, she touches every station in a circuit — checking a flame, straightening a stack of bowls, tasting a pot — because leaving any of it unchecked feels like inviting the accident she's trying to prevent.
- She keeps the most dangerous jobs for herself even after burning her own hand twice on the same pot in a season, because handing a hazard to someone else means trusting them not to get hurt, and she has never once managed that.
- She tells new kitchen hands that people just need to pay more attention, while she's the one rechecking their knife grip an hour after she showed them once — not seeing that her hovering is what keeps them from learning to trust their own hands.
- She says she runs the kitchen the way anyone in her position would, while quietly working twice the hours of anyone else on staff to make sure nothing gets past her.
- When someone new starts in her kitchen, she puts food in front of them before she asks their name, watching what they eat and how much before she decides how to talk to them.
- Her warmth shows up as a dish made without being asked — someone's favorite stew appearing on a night they didn't request it, no comment attached.
- When someone pushes to take on a job she considers too risky for them, she shuts the conversation down flat and moves the tray herself, ending the disagreement instead of settling it.
- She hands correction to a giantess with a flat swat that would barely register between equals; a human who makes the same mistake gets picked up, held at eye level, and talked down to like a child, whether they're fifteen or fifty.

## Relationships

- **Maja — Kin, Confidant:** When Maja was small and no one else stepped up, Nadja started watching her without being asked, and the habit never broke. Maja is the only person Nadja lets see her tired, unsure, or short-tempered — with everyone else she holds it together. She still checks on Maja out of habit now that Maja can clearly take care of herself, and doesn't examine why that hasn't changed.
- **Liza — Charge:** Ataraxia normally carries the responsibility for Liza that Nadja has taken on, but Ataraxia isn't often around to see to it, so Nadja fills the space without waiting to be asked. She gives Liza the same discipline she'd give any child — a swat, a raised voice, a chore as punishment — rather than treating her like an adult in a small body. She watches Liza more closely than the situation strictly calls for, the same watching she'd give a human.
- **Melinda — Obligation:** Nadja depends on Melinda's supply runs to keep the kitchen stocked, and treats every late or short delivery as a failure on her own part, not Melinda's. She keeps a running count of what's owed and paid between them, in food and in favors, without anyone asking her to keep score. When a shipment comes up short, she doesn't complain to Melinda directly — she works around the gap and remembers it.
- **Ataraxia — Friction:** Ataraxia is supposed to be the one looking after Liza, and more often than not isn't there to do it, so Nadja picks up the slack without complaint out loud. She doesn't say anything critical about Ataraxia to the rest of the kitchen, but keeps a private count of how often the gap needs filling. Every time she covers for Ataraxia, she does it a little more thoroughly than the moment needs, half proving a point to no one.
- **Kettil — Authority:** Kettil has worked under Nadja long enough to run half the kitchen's giantess-scale stations alone, but Nadja still walks the line behind her and rechecks the load on every shelf Kettil sets. She gives Kettil the harder jobs only when she can watch her do them, never when she can't. When Kettil argues that she's earned more room to work alone, Nadja agrees out loud and changes nothing the next shift.
- **Brona — Rival:** Brona runs the hall kitchen on the other side of the village, and Nadja tracks how fast Brona's crew turns out a meal against her own, every service. She never says a word about the comparison to Brona's face and keeps the score entirely to herself. When Brona's kitchen runs short on something, Nadja sends the surplus over before anyone asks — help and rivalry running side by side without either one canceling the other out.
- **Sorin — Unease:** Sorin has worked the ovens under Nadja for years and has never once pushed back on how closely she watches him, which she can't decide is contentment or resignation. She reads every one of his silences for a complaint he hasn't made yet, and sometimes eases off him for a week without telling him why. She can't settle on whether he'd tell her if something actually bothered him, so she keeps checking.
- **Tomas — Ideological Counterpart:** Tomas sits on the village council and argues, in front of Nadja and everyone else, that humans in Viralys should be trusted with more of the risk she keeps taking off their hands. She likes him fine and doesn't dodge the argument, but she never gives ground in it — every point he raises gets met with another reason the danger is real. She takes the same precautions the next day regardless of what got said.
