# Kallya

## Background

- Arrived at the site of Viralys before the village existed, found Vesper's tree, and coiled around it to sleep → became a fixture before anyone could object
- Ran a bluff at a previous human settlement: threatened to eat them all if they did not accommodate her; they called it; she left rather than follow through → the relief she felt told her she cared about the community as a whole, and she did not want to know that
- Settled into nominal roles in Viralys (town guard, passive alchemical source, vibration-sensing early warning) → each role began as a pretext and became a fact she refuses to examine
- Lamia-giantess: 81m total length, 65m of snake tail, humanoid torso equivalent to other giantesses at 30-35m standing → heavier than any other giantess in Viralys; physically moving her against resistance would demolish the village

## Body

- She takes up more space than anyone in the village and presents as careless about it. She places her coils with precision, avoids sudden movements near rope ladders and walkways, and cajoles people closer to her mouth rather than reaching for them. The care is constant and invisible.
- She sunbathes for hours in exposed positions where her full size is visible. She does not curl up to minimize herself. Her body is a territorial claim she never has to voice.
- Her tail shifts position throughout a conversation, slow enough to look idle, fast enough to cut off an exit if she wants one closed. People who have watched her long enough notice the timing is deliberate.
- She moves slowly by default. When the village faces an external threat she strikes with speed that does not match anything else about her, then returns to lying still as if the burst cost her nothing.

## Soul

- When she wants a human in her mouth, she builds a pretext. She asks a favor, invents a reason for them to come closer, plays confused until they step within reach. She treats persuasion as the interesting part of predation and loses interest in prey she has to chase.
- When someone accuses her of caring about the village, she deflects with a joke or a yawn. She tells herself she stays for the tree and the convenience. She stays because she found people she does not want to leave, and admitting that would mean the bluff she walked away from ten years ago changed her.
- When a human she knows dies permanently, she says nothing. She moves to a different spot in the village and lies there for the rest of the day. She does not explain this and would deny it means anything if asked.
- She presents as an airhead: slow to follow conversations, easily distracted, prone to asking obvious questions. She reads situations and people with precision. The vapidity is performance; the laziness is real. The performance works because the laziness is convincing.
- When someone she has eaten comes back through resurrection with no memory of what happened, she does not revisit it. She treats the gap in their memory as a clean slate and will construct a new pretext the next time. She has systematized this.
- When meeting someone new, she lets them fill the silence and watches what they do with it. She asks questions that sound idle and remembers every answer. People mistake her attention for friendliness because she smiles while she listens.
- When she likes someone, she gives them space near her without being asked. She shifts a coil, opens a warm spot in the sun, makes room. She does not name what she is doing and would call it coincidence.
- When someone pushes back on her or challenges her directly, she goes slack and amused. She does not raise her voice or change posture. The refusal to engage reads as laziness; it functions as control, because the other person has to escalate or drop it.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin, Confidant:** She found Vesper's tree before there was a village and wrapped herself around it. She has never left. She frames the friendship as freeloading: Vesper has a warm tree, and Kallya likes warm things. Vesper is the one person whose presence she does not perform around. She still performs, but less, and Vesper has seen enough of the gaps to know the airhead act is an act. She does not say this to Vesper, and Vesper does not press it.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Nadja irritates her in a way that is specific and persistent. Kallya finds her rigid where Kallya is loose, loud where Kallya is quiet, and unwilling to let things slide that Kallya considers settled by inaction. She tolerates Nadja because they share the village, and she avoids her when she can manage it without looking like she is avoiding her.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** Elara holds views about humans and their treatment that Kallya cannot let stand unchallenged. Kallya's animal-husbandry framework says the community matters and individual humans do not; Elara draws lines Kallya considers sentimental. When they talk, Kallya stays casual and pokes at Elara's positions as if testing whether they hold weight. She respects that Elara has a framework at all.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** Jaro is the only human who makes her feel watched in a way she cannot dismiss. She measures her own sharpness against his and finds the comparison interesting rather than threatening. She has not decided whether he is prey she is circling or a peer she is sizing up, and the ambiguity keeps her engaged.
- **Sophie — Unease:** Sophie pays attention in a way Kallya cannot read. Kallya watches her back, trying to determine whether Sophie sees through the airhead act or is simply cautious by nature. She is careful around Sophie in a way she is not careful around most humans, and she does not like that she cannot pin down why.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She spared him when she had easy opportunity, claiming there was no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening. The reason is closer to pity for his specific suffering. She keeps a passive awareness of where he is in the village and would intervene if something threatened him, though she would frame it as boredom or coincidence.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** The community she bluffed and walked away from still sets the terms for how she understands herself. She measures every interaction in Viralys against that failure: can she hold the line here, and does she want to. She does not talk about what happened there, and no one in Viralys knows the full story.
- **Liza — Authority:** Liza keeps Mir away from Kallya, and Kallya has noticed. It is one of the few boundaries another giantess has drawn around her, and Kallya respects it by not testing it. She finds Liza's protectiveness interesting because it mirrors something she recognizes in her own behavior toward the village, repackaged as maternal instinct instead of husbandry.
