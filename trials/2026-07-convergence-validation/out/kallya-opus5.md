# Kallya

## Design Notes

### Session Notes

- Arrived before Viralys existed, found Vesper's tree and settled in it; the village formed around them both
- Previously ran a bluff at another human settlement: implied she would eat them all if not accommodated; they called it; she could not follow through with wiping out a community she knew and left
- The relief at not following through was the revelation: it told her she cared about the community as a whole, and she did not want to know that
- She can and does eat individual humans she knows; the line drawn was genocide, not predation; she feels connection to community as a continuous whole rather than to specific individuals
- Core dynamic: presents as airhead; reads situations and people with precision; the vapidity is performance; the laziness is real
- Views humans through an animal-husbandry framework: the community matters, individual humans do not; she keeps the flock healthy while eating individuals hedonistically; she enjoys how they squirm and struggle; the only giantess in the cast who is actively predatory
- Treats the human population like a multi-celled organism: short-lived individual cells are expendable, but the community as a whole can endure and is worth preserving
- Social predator through persuasion: constructs pretexts for humans to enter her mouth; manipulates them into compliance using her airhead persona; unpredictability is her tool
- When witnesses are absent she may be more direct; exploits resurrection memory loss systematically
- Self-image gap: tells herself she stays for the tree and the convenience; stays because she found people she does not want to leave
- Nominal roles: town guard (post hoc justification), passive alchemical source (grew from a predation pretext), vibration-sensing early warning system
- Steward's House assignment is administrative fiction; she contributes nothing
- Lamia-giantess: 81m total, 65m snake tail; humanoid torso equivalent to other giantesses (30-35m standing)
- Heavier than any other giantess; physically moving her against resistance would demolish the village
- No magic; purely physical; strong but untrained in combat
- Presents as careless about her size while actually being very careful; cajoles people closer to her mouth rather than grabbing them partly because sudden movement can disturb rope ladders, displace objects, and injure humans nearby
- Slow metabolism; slow digestion; victims are stuck inside her for a long time
- Cares about the people around her, both humans and giantesses, and will not admit this; friendship with Vesper is real even though she plays it off as freeloading
- When the village faces external threats she responds with brutal effectiveness, returns to sunbathing, refuses credit
- When a human she knows dies permanently she notices and says nothing; moves to a different spot and lies there
- Spares Rado Stary despite easy opportunity, claiming there is no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening; the reason is closer to pity for his specific suffering
- Liza keeps Mir away from Kallya; one of the few contexts where Liza's possessive instinct is protective rather than possessive
- Maja: gets along but Kallya finds her boring since Maja is hyperactive and Kallya is lazy; they do not interact much
- Ataraxia: neutral; lazy solidarity on the surface; both treat the village as personal space
- Leif Holtveld may be secretly engineering cooling devices to make areas uncomfortable for Kallya, attempting to control her movements through temperature; she does not know about it yet
- Melinda, Kodia, Sigrid: no specific dynamics established beyond community presence
- Character art reference name: Viri

### Builder Context

- Narrative function: the character who demonstrates that care and predation coexist without resolving into either; her animal-husbandry framework is the most honest articulation of how several giantesses relate to humans, and she is the only one who has named it
- Thematic mirror with Vesper: both care about humans while running frameworks (protection vs. husbandry) that let them avoid confronting what that care coexists with; Vesper cannot confront Kallya's treatment of humans without examining her own relationship to the humans bound to her tree
- Spans more cross-population relationships than most residents while presenting as the village's most useless member; a community lynchpin who would deny being one

## Background

- Arrived before Viralys existed, found Vesper's tree and settled in it → the village formed around them both
- Ran a bluff at a previous human settlement, implying she would eat them all if not accommodated → they called it; she could not follow through and left
- The relief at not following through told her she cared about the community as a whole → she did not want to know that about herself
- Lamia-giantess: 81m total, 65m snake tail → humanoid torso equivalent to other giantesses at 30-35m standing height
- Heavier than any other giantess in the village → physically moving her against resistance would demolish structures around her
- No magic, purely physical → strong but untrained in combat
- Slow metabolism → slow digestion; she processes food over days rather than hours
- Assigned to the Steward's House as town guard, passive alchemical source, and vibration-sensing early warning system → the assignment is administrative fiction; she contributes nothing to administration

## Body

- She is 81m of lamia coiled through Vesper's tree, heavier than any other giantess in the village. When she shifts position, the tree creaks and rope ladders sway. She knows the effect her mass has on the space around her and acts as though she has not noticed.
- She moves slowly and carefully while performing carelessness. A sudden grab at her scale would snap ropes, displace objects, and injure humans nearby. She cajoles people closer instead of reaching for them. The constraint is physical. The method looks social.
- She follows heat. She sunbathes for hours on warm stone, shifts position to track the light, and claims the best south-facing surfaces in the village without asking. Cold makes her sluggish and irritable.
- Her tail fills paths, doorways, and passages. People route around her. She does not move for them.

## Soul

- She performs vapidity. She asks questions she knows the answers to, misremembers names she has heard dozens of times, and lets conversations pass without engaging. People relax around her and stop watching what they say. The performance is deliberate and precise. The laziness beneath it is real. She avoids responsibility with genuine commitment.
- She reads people with precision. She tracks who is nervous, who is lying, who can be persuaded and how. Her accuracy comes from sustained observation over hours of what she calls sunbathing. She has no grasp of institutional knowledge, village politics, or administrative systems. When a question falls outside one-on-one social dynamics, she deflects with the airhead act or ignores it.
- She views humans the way a herder views livestock. The community matters. Individual humans are expendable. She keeps the flock healthy, counts heads after an incident, and eats individuals when she wants to. She enjoys how they resist. She is the only giantess in the village who says this plainly.
- She constructs pretexts for humans to approach her mouth. She uses her airhead persona to frame proximity as favor or accident, manipulates targets into compliance, and times her approaches for when witnesses are absent. She exploits the resurrection system's memory loss. Her targets come back and do not remember what happened.
- She tells herself she stays in Viralys for the tree and the convenience. She stays because she found people she does not want to leave. When someone asks why she is still here, she lists practical reasons. The list gets longer each time.
- She presents as the village's most useless member. She performs no administrative work, fulfills no patrol duties, and claims no responsibility. When the village faces an external threat, she kills it quickly and without warning, then returns to sunbathing. She refuses credit and calls the response self-preservation. She keeps a half-aware vigil through vibration sensing she calls napping.
- When a human she knows dies permanently, she notices. She says nothing. She moves to a different spot in the tree and lies there for the rest of the day. She does not explain the move. Her stated view says individual humans are expendable. Her response to their permanent loss says otherwise, and she has not reconciled the two.
- When she meets a stranger, she offers the airhead performance first. She is warm, scattered, slightly confusing. She asks their name, loses it, asks again. She fills silences with idle comments. People who stay around her long enough notice she remembers things she should not know.
- When she is done with someone, the performance stops. The shift is immediate. She stops asking questions, stops filling silence, and watches them without expression. The intelligence underneath is visible for as long as the pause lasts. People who have been dismissed this way tend to avoid her afterward.
- She speaks in loose, wandering sentences when performing. She trails off, changes subjects, answers questions with questions. When the performance drops, she is short and direct. People who have heard her both ways do not reconcile them.
- The village's population is finite and she eats from it. The resurrection system makes this sustainable, but humans who die of old age are not restored, and she has been here long enough to see the balance shift. She tracks this without calling it tracking. Some months she eats less. Some months she does not.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin:** She has lived in Vesper's tree since before the village existed and frames this as squatting in a good spot. The friendship is older than Viralys and neither of them names it. She does not tell Vesper which humans she has eaten. Vesper has not asked.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** She bluffed a human settlement into accommodating her by implying she would eat them all. They called the bluff. She could not follow through, and the relief at failing told her something about herself she did not want to know. She left rather than sit with it. She has not named this place to anyone in Viralys. She measures every decision to stay here against what leaving cost her there.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Nadja runs the kitchen with standards that extend to everyone in the village. Kallya contributes nothing, occupies prime warm spots near the ovens when she pleases, and treats Nadja's irritation as background noise. Nadja pushes. Kallya does not move. Neither has escalated and neither has yielded.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** They share ground no other giantess in the village stands on: both accept that individual humans die and neither calls it wrong. Elara grounds her view in natural law. Kallya grounds hers in appetite. The two views look alike from outside and differ where it matters. Kallya tends the flock. Elara would let it scatter. When the subject comes up, they talk past each other with something close to mutual interest.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** He watches her and she watches him back. He is one of the few humans who does not relax around her airhead performance, and she has not determined whether his wariness comes from insight or instinct. She adjusts when he is present: fewer pretexts, more genuine laziness. He is the audience she has not won over, and she keeps track of where he is.
- **Sophie — Unease:** Sophie lives in the same tree and speaks the giantess language fluently. Kallya cannot tell how much Sophie has noticed about her habits. Sophie is polite, efficient, and gives nothing away. The airhead performance does not work on her, and Kallya has not figured out why.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She does not eat him. She tells herself there is no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening. She has placed herself between him and situations he cannot handle, framing each intervention as coincidence. She avoids the word "protect" and would deny the behavior if named. The reason is closer to pity for his specific suffering, and pity for an individual contradicts everything she claims about humans.
- **Ataraxia — Community Thread:** They share the village as personal territory and neither has negotiated terms. Ataraxia lazes on rooftops. Kallya coils through the tree. When Ataraxia is away, Kallya spreads into the space she left. When Ataraxia returns, Kallya contracts without comment. The arrangement has never required a conversation and has never had one.
