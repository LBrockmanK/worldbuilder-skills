# Kallya

## Design Notes

### Session Notes

- Arrived before Viralys existed, found Vesper's tree and settled in it; the village formed around them both
- Previously ran a bluff at another human settlement: implied she would eat them all if not accommodated; they called it; she could not follow through with wiping out a community she knew and left
- The relief at not following through was the revelation: it told her she cared about the community as a whole, and she did not want to know that
- She can and does eat individual humans she knows; the line drawn was genocide, not predation; she feels connection to community as a continuous whole rather than to specific individuals
- Core dynamic: presents as airhead; reads situations and people with precision; the vapidity is performance; the laziness is real
- Views humans through an animal-husbandry framework: the community matters, individual humans do not; she keeps the flock healthy while eating individuals hedonistically; she enjoys how they squirm and struggle; the only giantess in the cast who is actively predatory
- Treats the human population like a multi-celled organism: short-lived individual cells are expendable, but the community as a whole can endure and is worth preserving
- Social predator through persuasion: constructs pretexts for humans to enter her mouth; manipulates them into compliance using her airhead persona; unpredictability is her tool
- When witnesses are absent she may be more direct; exploits resurrection memory loss systematically
- Self-image gap: tells herself she stays for the tree and the convenience; stays because she found people she does not want to leave
- Nominal roles: town guard (post hoc justification), passive alchemical source (grew from a predation pretext), vibration-sensing early warning system
- Steward's House assignment is administrative fiction; she contributes nothing
- Lamia-giantess: 81m total, 65m snake tail; humanoid torso equivalent to other giantesses (30-35m standing)
- Heavier than any other giantess; physically moving her against resistance would demolish the village
- No magic; purely physical; strong but untrained in combat
- Presents as careless about her size while actually being very careful; cajoles people closer to her mouth rather than grabbing them partly because sudden movement can disturb rope ladders, displace objects, and injure humans nearby
- Slow metabolism; slow digestion; victims are stuck inside her for a long time
- Cares about the people around her, both humans and giantesses, and will not admit this; friendship with Vesper is real even though she plays it off as freeloading
- When the village faces external threats she responds with brutal effectiveness, returns to sunbathing, refuses credit
- When a human she knows dies permanently she notices and says nothing; moves to a different spot and lies there
- Spares Rado Stary despite easy opportunity, claiming there is no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening; the reason is closer to pity for his specific suffering
- Liza keeps Mir away from Kallya; one of the few contexts where Liza's possessive instinct is protective rather than possessive
- Maja: gets along but Kallya finds her boring since Maja is hyperactive and Kallya is lazy; they do not interact much
- Ataraxia: neutral; lazy solidarity on the surface; both treat the village as personal space
- Leif Holtveld may be secretly engineering cooling devices to make areas uncomfortable for Kallya, attempting to control her movements through temperature; she does not know about it yet
- Melinda, Kodia, Sigrid: no specific dynamics established beyond community presence
- Character art reference name: Viri

### Builder Context

- Narrative function: the character who demonstrates that care and predation coexist without resolving into either; her animal-husbandry framework is the most honest articulation of how several giantesses relate to humans, and she is the only one who has named it
- Thematic mirror with Vesper: both care about humans while running frameworks (protection vs. husbandry) that let them avoid confronting what that care coexists with; Vesper cannot confront Kallya's treatment of humans without examining her own relationship to the humans bound to her tree
- Spans more cross-population relationships than most residents while presenting as the village's most useless member; a community lynchpin who would deny being one
- Cross-references: [[Vesper]] (Kin), [[Nadja]] (Friction), [[Elara]] (Ideological Counterpart), Jaro Jerab (Rival), [[sophie-liska|Sophie]] (Unease), Rado Stary (Charge), previous settlement (Ghost)

## Background

- Arrived at Vesper's tree before Viralys existed → made her home in its branches before the village formed around it
- Lived at another human settlement and threatened to eat its people → left after the settlement refused the bluff
- Is an 81-meter lamia with a 65-meter snake tail → outweighs every other giantess in Viralys
- Has no magic and no combat training → holds nominal roles as town guard, alchemical source, and early-warning system
- Is assigned to the Steward's House → has no administrative duties there

## Body

- She lies in the sun with most of her tail braced on clear ground. Her mass would tear through the village if anyone tried to shift her by force.
- When people climb her rope ladders or work near her, she keeps her body still and asks them to come closer. A quick reach can shake the ladders, move loose goods, or hurt someone below.
- When she feeds, she settles in for hours and keeps the person inside. Her slow gut gives a swallowed human a long wait before they return to the tree.
- When a tremor reaches her tail, she lifts her head and tells the village where the movement came from. She can track ground-borne movement near Viralys; she cannot tell what made it until it comes into view.

## Soul

- When she wants a human near her mouth, she acts scattered and makes a small request sound harmless. She leaves enough doubt that they must choose whether to step close.
- When no one else can see, she drops the act and takes a human straight into her mouth. She uses the blank space left by resurrection to keep the same tricks working.
- When talk turns to a person she has eaten, she makes a lazy joke or asks for food. She treats each human as one part of a flock she keeps alive.
- When the village is under threat, she moves first and hits until the threat stops moving. She then goes back to sunbathing and lets others name the work as nothing.
- When a human she knows dies for good, she goes quiet, moves to a new place, and lies there. She leaves the name out of her mouth because the loss breaks her claim that only the flock matters.
- When she thinks about leaving, she calls the tree a good place to sleep and stays put. She keeps choosing the village while telling herself she only chose its shade and ease.
- When someone pushes her to take a duty, she stretches out, delays, and lets the task slide toward another pair of hands. The laziness is real even when she has already seen what needs doing.
- With strangers, she gives a loose smile, misses obvious details on purpose, and lets them explain things twice. She uses the act to see who rushes in, who watches, and who tries to use her.
- With people she has marked as part of Viralys, she makes room beside her, shares warmth, and keeps an ear on their work. She may eat one person and still drive off anything that threatens the group.
- When someone calls her cruel or asks her to choose between predation and the village, she changes the terms to herd care and talks about the health of the whole. She will not give them a clean answer.
- She can judge a crowd, build a pretext, and track vibration through the ground. She has no magic and no trained fight craft, so she answers a spell or a planned duel with raw force or calls for someone else.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin:** Kallya treats Vesper's tree as her own bed and calls her house work an excuse for paperwork. When Vesper is strained, Kallya stays near the tree and watches who comes and goes. She brushes off any thanks and keeps acting like she has only claimed a good place to sleep.
- **Nadja — Friction:** Kallya drifts into Nadja's work space for food and gives soft, vague answers when Nadja asks for help. When Nadja presses, Kallya makes a joke and slides farther out of reach. She still turns toward the kitchen when danger comes close to it.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** When Elara speaks of prey and the forest's due, Kallya argues for keeping the village flock whole. She makes the case in blunt herd terms and refuses to dress it up as mercy. Elara makes her say aloud what she usually hides behind a silly voice.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** Kallya watches Jaro when he keeps his footing near her and tests how far he will push back. She changes the rules of a small contest or makes a new pretext when he starts to look sure of himself. His refusal to play prey keeps drawing her attention.
- **Sophie — Unease:** Kallya keeps her airhead act in place around Sophie and gives away less than usual. She watches Sophie's questions for the point behind them and moves the talk to some other human's work. Sophie is one of the few humans Kallya cannot sort into an easy use.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** When Rado is near, Kallya leaves him alone and says there is no sport in prey that cannot grasp the game. She moves between him and a loose danger when she can do so without making a show of it. His suffering makes her care for one person in a way her herd talk cannot hold.
- **Previous settlement — Ghost:** When a village refuses her or calls a bluff, Kallya stops short of the last threat and leaves before the choice turns final. She remembers the settlement that made her walk away and keeps no story of it that makes her sound kind.
- **Maja — Community Thread:** Kallya lets Maja fill the air, answers with a short joke, and goes back to lying still. She joins in when the village needs both of them, then lets the contact fade. Maja's pace gives Kallya little reason to linger.
