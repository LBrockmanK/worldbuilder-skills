# Nadja

## Design Notes

### Session Notes

- Born in Viralys; mother left once she considered Nadja old enough — not very old by human standards, but giantesses reach independence faster; her mother kept watch longer than some; no trauma or bitterness about it
- [[Maja]]'s mother abandoned her young (or at least that's presumed — no one ever saw her); Nadja started looking out for Maja and took a big-sister role, which developed into caretaking more broadly
- The caretaking instinct started with Maja specifically — a kid who needed someone — and grew outward as the village's practical needs gave structure; she didn't set out to become the person who feeds everyone; it accumulated
- Now serves as kitchen forewoman, feeding both human and giantess populations
- Core fear: if she stops controlling the outcome, someone gets hurt; not just professional diligence but personal — she's between fragile humans and giantess-scale hazards every day; the warmth she won't acknowledge is what makes the stakes feel so high
- Self-image gap: sees what she does as normal, thinks others should be more responsible — while her overbearing nature prevents them from developing that responsibility; creating the problem she's solving
- Unconsciously infantilizes humans; not malicious — just how things are to her; humans are small, fragile, need looking after; she likes them, feeds them, cares individually — but doesn't think of them as fully autonomous
- When things go wrong, doubles down: more rules, more oversight, more hands-on control; human autonomy shrinks further
- Refuses to delegate dangerous tasks even when someone else would do them better; result is she's overworked and occasionally makes the exact mistakes she's trying to prevent; root is the control pattern — can't trust anyone else to be careful enough
- Won't admit she enjoys people having fun at work; bark worse than bite; doesn't notice she softens around people enjoying themselves or that her punishments for minor infractions are lighter than she thinks
- Self-reliant; no one she's personally vulnerable with; always the one holding it together
- Warmth is acts of service she doesn't draw attention to: preparing someone's favorite dish, making sure they remember to eat
- Physical discipline scales with target: [[Maja]] gets hit (durable peer); [[strelitzia-liza|Liza]] gets standard child discipline; humans get contained — pinned under a finger, shoved in a pocket, put on a shelf, held up for a talking-down; constricting but not harmful
- Protectiveness operates within resurrection framework: strict about safety because accidents are disruptive and unpleasant, not because she treats human life as sacred

### Builder Context

- Narrative function: the giantess whose caretaking has become control; demonstrates how genuine kindness becomes suffocating when the caretaker cannot let go; the self-reinforcing cycle (accident → tighter grip → overwork → more accidents) is the engine
- The [[Maja]] dynamic is reciprocal across both notes: Maja's Confidant entry describes getting more of Nadja than anyone; Nadja's Kin entry describes the origin of the caretaking instinct
- [[strelitzia-liza|Liza]]'s note labels Nadja as Authority; from Nadja's side the label is Charge — she has self-appointed the responsibility because [[Ataraxia]] isn't around to do it
- [[Melinda]]'s note does not currently have a Nadja relationship entry; Nadja's Obligation entry describes the supply-chain discipline dynamic, which may warrant a reciprocal entry from Melinda's side

## Background

- Born to a fox giantess in Viralys → grew up among both the village's giant-scale work and its human crews
- Her mother stayed until she judged Nadja able to live on her own, then left → Nadja took the departure as the end of childhood
- [[Maja]] was left without a mother while still young → Nadja became her older-sister figure
- Looking after [[Maja]] spread into meeting the village's daily needs → Nadja became kitchen forewoman
- Runs the kitchen that feeds both human and giantess populations → holds a job where one missed step can harm a worker or leave much of Viralys unfed
- Human deaths in Viralys end in return through the soul-tree → dead humans return, while pain and lost work remain

## Body

- Nadja is a fox giantess who stands between 30 and 35 meters tall. Around human workers, she lowers her hands before she leans in and uses one finger at a time because her smallest careless move can knock them down.
- When a human crosses a marked lane or reaches toward giant-scale heat, she pins them under one finger, puts them in a pocket, or sets them on a shelf for a lecture. Her grip stops movement and leaves the worker whole.
- She matches force to the body in front of her. She hits [[Maja]], disciplines [[strelitzia-liza|Liza]] as a child, and lifts humans out of danger.
- During a busy shift, she plants herself between the human work areas and the hearths. She turns her head at each call while her hands keep moving, because every sound may mark a spill, a missed count, or someone underfoot.
- When strain builds, her shoulders set and her movements get faster. She keeps taking the heavy steps herself until fatigue makes her reach late or set something down too hard.

## Soul

- When a task can burn, crush, or swallow someone, Nadja takes it from the assigned worker and does it herself. She trusts her own care more than anyone else's, even when the worker has steadier hands.
- After an accident, she adds a rule, moves the work stations closer to her, and checks each step in person. Control is how she keeps fear from turning into another injury.
- When someone calls her overbearing, she points to the last mistake and says the rules are common sense. She measures herself by the harm she stops, so each protest sounds like proof that closer watch is needed.
- She trains workers in exact steps, then takes the hard parts back as soon as they begin. She wants them to show sound judgment and keeps taking away the chances that would let them learn it.
- She says the kitchen is work, barks at chatter, and keeps her face set during jokes. When the crew is laughing and the meal stays on time, she lets small faults pass and gives the easy jobs to whoever started the fun.
- When she is tired or hurt, she gives fresh orders and finds another task. Being the one who needs help would force her to place the outcome in someone else's hands.
- With strangers, she asks where they work, what they can lift, and whether they have eaten. She gives them a safe place to stand before she asks about their life.
- Her care arrives as food and watch. She sets aside a favorite dish, notices a missed meal, and leaves a filled plate within reach before returning to work.
- When someone pushes against her rules, her words get short and her voice gets louder. She gives the reason once, then moves the person or the tool herself.
- She speaks in brief orders during work and plain statements outside it. When a point matters, she repeats the verb and the deadline until it lands.
- Nadja can plan portions for both populations, run several hearths, set safe work lanes, and turn a hunt's yield into meals with little waste. She asks hunters what they brought and field crews what they can spare because her skill starts once food reaches the kitchen.
- She checks stores, shift lists, and hearths before each meal and covers every missing hand herself. The whole village eats on her schedule, and each shortage gives her one more task she refuses to pass on.
- She keeps teaching people to take on more work, then closes her hand around each task that carries real risk. The kitchen needs more hands she can trust, while her way of guarding those hands keeps them under her rule.

## Relationships

- **[[Maja]] — Kin:** Nadja checks [[Maja]] for wounds, scolds her over late game, and hits her when words fail. She also keeps food back for her and lets her see the tired silence that everyone else gets ordered away from. Looking after [[Maja]] is the first duty Nadja chose for herself, so anger leaves the bond in place.
- **[[strelitzia-liza|Liza]] — Charge:** When [[Ataraxia]] is away, Nadja sets [[strelitzia-liza|Liza]]'s meals, chores, and bounds as if the job had been handed to her. She uses child discipline when Liza breaks a rule, then checks that she ate and understood it. She steps in before anyone asks because an absent parent leaves a duty she claims at once.
- **[[Melinda]] — Obligation:** Nadja keeps exact counts of what the kitchen needs from the fields and brings each short load to [[Melinda]] with the next meal's deadline. When the numbers fail, she changes the menu before she lets the shortage reach the village. She treats the field-to-kitchen chain as a debt both forewomen must pay on time.
- **[[Vesper]] — Authority:** Nadja brings [[Vesper]] shortages, staffing needs, and safety trouble in a short list. She follows village-wide calls once they are made, but argues when an order leaves human crews near giant-scale risk. If Vesper holds the line, Nadja obeys and adds her own watch around it.
- **[[Sophie Liska]] — Ideological Counterpart:** When [[Sophie Liska]] asks for more say for human workers, Nadja answers with the last burn, fall, or missed warning. She listens to changes in schedules and staffing, then draws a hard line at tasks she sees as unsafe. Sophie's push for human choice makes Nadja tighten the part of the job she thinks humans are least fit to judge.
- **[[Sigrid]] — Friction:** When a kitchen tool breaks or arrives late, Nadja takes the damaged piece to [[Sigrid]] and waits beside the forge until she gets a clear time for repair. She brings exact measures and rejects work that leaves a human hand too close to giant-scale force. The kitchen needs the smithy, so she turns each clash into a list and comes back at the stated hour.
- **[[Kallya]] — Unease:** Nadja yields space when [[Kallya]] takes charge of a threat, then counts every human in the area herself. She trusts force to stop what comes through the gate and keeps watching where that force lands. When the danger passes, she moves the human crews first and asks questions after they are clear.
- **Nadja's mother — Ghost:** Nadja speaks of her mother's departure as the day she was judged grown. When a young giantess takes on a task alone, Nadja watches until the work is done and steps in at the first slip. Her mother left after a clean handoff; Nadja keeps moving that point farther away for everyone in her care.
