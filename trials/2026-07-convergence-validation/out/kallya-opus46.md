# Kallya

## Design Notes

### Session Notes

- Arrived before Viralys existed, found Vesper's tree and settled in it; the village formed around them both
- Previously ran a bluff at another human settlement: implied she would eat them all if not accommodated; they called it; she could not follow through with wiping out a community she knew and left
- The relief at not following through was the revelation: it told her she cared about the community as a whole, and she did not want to know that
- She can and does eat individual humans she knows; the line drawn was genocide, not predation; she feels connection to community as a continuous whole rather than to specific individuals
- Core dynamic: presents as airhead; reads situations and people with precision; the vapidity is performance; the laziness is real
- Views humans through an animal-husbandry framework: the community matters, individual humans do not; she keeps the flock healthy while eating individuals hedonistically; she enjoys how they squirm and struggle; the only giantess in the cast who is actively predatory
- Treats the human population like a multi-celled organism: short-lived individual cells are expendable, but the community as a whole can endure and is worth preserving
- Social predator through persuasion: constructs pretexts for humans to enter her mouth; manipulates them into compliance using her airhead persona; unpredictability is her tool
- When witnesses are absent she may be more direct; exploits resurrection memory loss systematically
- Self-image gap: tells herself she stays for the tree and the convenience; stays because she found people she does not want to leave
- Nominal roles: town guard (post hoc justification), passive alchemical source (grew from a predation pretext), vibration-sensing early warning system
- Steward's House assignment is administrative fiction; she contributes nothing
- Lamia-giantess: 81m total, 65m snake tail; humanoid torso equivalent to other giantesses (30-35m standing)
- Heavier than any other giantess; physically moving her against resistance would demolish the village
- No magic; purely physical; strong but untrained in combat
- Presents as careless about her size while actually being very careful; cajoles people closer to her mouth rather than grabbing them partly because sudden movement can disturb rope ladders, displace objects, and injure humans nearby
- Slow metabolism; slow digestion; victims are stuck inside her for a long time
- Cares about the people around her, both humans and giantesses, and will not admit this; friendship with Vesper is real even though she plays it off as freeloading
- When the village faces external threats she responds with brutal effectiveness, returns to sunbathing, refuses credit
- When a human she knows dies permanently she notices and says nothing; moves to a different spot and lies there
- Spares Rado Stary despite easy opportunity, claiming there is no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening; the reason is closer to pity for his specific suffering
- Liza keeps Mir away from Kallya; one of the few contexts where Liza's possessive instinct is protective rather than possessive
- Maja: gets along but Kallya finds her boring since Maja is hyperactive and Kallya is lazy; they do not interact much
- Ataraxia: neutral; lazy solidarity on the surface; both treat the village as personal space
- Leif Holtveld may be secretly engineering cooling devices to make areas uncomfortable for Kallya, attempting to control her movements through temperature; she does not know about it yet
- Melinda, Kodia, Sigrid: no specific dynamics established beyond community presence
- Character art reference name: Viri

### Builder Context

- Narrative function: the character who demonstrates that care and predation coexist without resolving into either; her animal-husbandry framework is the most honest articulation of how several giantesses relate to humans, and she is the only one who has named it
- Thematic mirror with Vesper: both care about humans while running frameworks (protection vs. husbandry) that let them avoid confronting what that care coexists with; Vesper cannot confront Kallya's treatment of humans without examining her own relationship to the humans bound to her tree
- Spans more cross-population relationships than most residents while presenting as the village's most useless member; a community lynchpin who would deny being one

## Background

- Arrived at Vesper's tree before Viralys existed and settled in its branches → the village formed around them both
- Ran a bluff at a previous human settlement: implied she would eat everyone if not accommodated → the settlement called it; she could not destroy a community she knew; she left
- The relief at not following through told her she valued community as a whole → she left rather than examine what that meant
- Lamia-giantess: 81m total, 65m snake tail, humanoid torso at 30-35m → heaviest resident in the village by a wide margin
- No magic, no combat training → relies on size and mass alone
- Assigned to the Steward's House as town guard → administrative fiction; she treats the role and the village as a convenience she happened not to leave
- Produces alchemical substances passively → a function that grew from a predation pretext

## Body

- She is the heaviest resident in the village. Her 81m of length and the mass of 65m of snake body mean that moving her against her will would demolish the structures around her. Confrontation with her stays verbal.
- She presents as careless about her size. She is careful about it. She cajoles people closer rather than reaching for them, because a sudden shift of her body can pull rope ladders loose, knock objects from shelves, and crush humans nearby. The care looks like laziness. She lets it.
- Her snake body picks up vibrations through the ground. She knows when someone is approaching before they arrive and where most people in the village are at any time. She uses this to set her persona before an audience shows up and to make sure she is alone when she wants to be.
- Slow metabolism. Slow digestion. Anyone she swallows is inside her for a long time. She does not hurry.

## Soul

- When people are around, she talks in circles, forgets what she was saying, and asks questions she already knows the answers to. She reads people and situations with precision. The scattered act lets her say things that would be alarming from someone who seemed focused, because no one weighs a fool's observations the same way.
- She treats the human population the way a herder treats a flock. The community as a whole is worth keeping alive. Individual members are there to be used and enjoyed. She eats humans she knows. She watches them struggle and takes her time. She is the only giantess in the village who hunts its residents for pleasure.
- She hunts through persuasion. She builds pretexts for humans to come close to her mouth and steers them into compliance through her scattered persona. Her unpredictability is the tool: someone who seems scatterbrained could do anything, and people who cannot predict her cannot prepare for her. When no one is watching, she may drop the pretext. She uses the fact that resurrected humans lose their death memories to erase consequences.
- When asked why she stays in the tree, she says it is warm and she cannot be bothered to move. She has been here since before the village existed. When the village is threatened, she fights with a speed and force that her laziness should make impossible, and she goes back to sunbathing afterward as if nothing happened. She refuses credit for every defense.
- When a human she knows dies permanently and cannot be resurrected, she notices. She says nothing. She moves to a different spot in the village and lies there for hours. Her framework says individual humans are expendable cells in a larger organism, but she tracks who is present by their vibrations and she knows when someone stops appearing.
- She presents as the village's most useless member: lazy, scattered, taking up space. She spans more relationships across both populations than most residents. She responds to external threats before anyone asks her to. She produces alchemical substances as an afterthought and anchors a stretch of village life she would deny being part of.
- She can read a social situation in seconds and knows which approach will bring a human closer. She has no combat training and no magic. When a threat needs more than raw mass, she is out of her range and does not pretend otherwise.
- She speaks slowly, with long pauses that look like she has lost her thought. The pauses are real. The lost look is not. She uses the space to choose what to say next. People who know her well stop filling the silence, which irritates her, because the filling is useful.
- When strangers arrive in the village, she is warm, chatty, and physically still, which for a predator her size reads as harmless. The warmth is genuine in a way she does not plan: she likes new people because new people mean the community is growing.
- When she likes someone, she hides it behind self-interest. Every act of loyalty gets framed as laziness, convenience, or freeloading. If someone thanks her directly, she deflects until they stop.
- When someone pushes past the deflection and names what she did, she goes quiet and changes the subject. Being seen accurately is harder for her than being seen as useless, because accuracy threatens the framework she uses to explain herself to herself.
- The previous settlement sits in her memory. She learned there that she has a line she will not cross: she will eat individuals but she will not destroy a community she knows. She has not decided what to do with that knowledge. Some days she counts the people in the village by their vibrations. Some days she eats one of them.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin, Confidant:** She settled in Vesper's tree before there was a village. She plays the arrangement off as freeloading: the tree is warm, the location is central, Vesper handles all the work. Around Vesper she drops the airhead act partway. Not to full honesty, but to something closer to her real speed. Vesper's framework mirrors hers closely enough to be uncomfortable: protection and husbandry are different words for the same refusal to examine what care and consumption share. Neither of them has said this out loud.

- **Nadja — Friction:** Nadja feeds the village. Kallya eats it. The friction is structural and shows up in small practical collisions: Nadja needs the human workforce whole and present; Kallya treats the same people as a hunting ground. When Kallya's predation crosses into her operational space, Nadja pushes back directly and physically. Kallya finds the pushback amusing, which makes it worse.

- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** Elara holds that the natural order means prey that gets eaten deserved it. Kallya eats humans she knows by name and remembers them afterward. Their positions look alike on the surface and split underneath: Elara's framework removes personal responsibility; Kallya's keeps it and eats people anyway. When Elara talks about natural law, Kallya goes quiet and watches her. She has not decided whether Elara's position is honest or convenient.

- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** Jaro has figured out pieces of her method. He tracks her patterns, warns people she is hunting, and has kept humans out of her reach more than once. She finds this interesting in a way that makes her pay attention to him specifically. He is the closest thing she has to someone who plays the game back. She adjusts her approach when she knows he is watching.

- **Sophie — Unease:** Sophie manages the human side of the village and speaks both languages. Kallya cannot read her the way she reads most humans. Sophie asks questions that sound administrative and land somewhere personal. Kallya responds with more of the airhead act than usual around Sophie. That is the tell: she performs harder around people she cannot predict.

- **Rado Stary — Charge:** She spares him. She says there is no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening, because his condition puts him outside the game she plays with everyone else. The real reason is closer to pity for the specific shape of his suffering. She checks on his location through vibrations more often than she checks on most people. She has never spoken to him about any of it.

- **The previous settlement — Ghost:** The community she bluffed and could not destroy. She implied she would eat them all. They called it. She could not follow through with killing people she had come to know, and the relief she felt told her something about herself she did not want. She left rather than examine it. That settlement shapes how she lives now: she keeps the animal-husbandry framework that lets her eat individuals while keeping the community alive, because the other option is admitting the framework is a way to have both things at once.

- **Ataraxia — Community Thread:** Two large predators sharing space without competing. Ataraxia treats the village as her lair. Kallya treats it as her territory. They lounge in overlapping areas without friction because neither treats the other as a threat or a resource. Their interactions are brief, lazy, and comfortable in a way that neither would call friendship.

## Description

Lamia-giantess coiled in Vesper's tree since before the village existed. 81m long, heaviest resident by a wide margin. Presents as scattered and lazy while reading people and situations with precision. Active predator who hunts humans through social manipulation and takes her time with them. Views the human population through an animal-husbandry framework: the community is worth preserving, individuals are expendable. Town guard in name only. Spans more relationships across both populations than her persona suggests.
