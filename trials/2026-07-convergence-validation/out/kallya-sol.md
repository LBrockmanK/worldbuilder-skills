# Kallya

## Design Notes

### Session Notes

- Arrived before Viralys existed, found Vesper's tree and settled in it; the village formed around them both
- Previously ran a bluff at another human settlement: implied she would eat them all if not accommodated; they called it; she could not follow through with wiping out a community she knew and left
- The relief at not following through was the revelation: it told her she cared about the community as a whole, and she did not want to know that
- She can and does eat individual humans she knows; the line drawn was genocide, not predation; she feels connection to community as a continuous whole rather than to specific individuals
- Core dynamic: presents as airhead; reads situations and people with precision; the vapidity is performance; the laziness is real
- Views humans through an animal-husbandry framework: the community matters, individual humans do not; she keeps the flock healthy while eating individuals hedonistically; she enjoys how they squirm and struggle; the only giantess in the cast who is actively predatory
- Treats the human population like a multi-celled organism: short-lived individual cells are expendable, but the community as a whole can endure and is worth preserving
- Social predator through persuasion: constructs pretexts for humans to enter her mouth; manipulates them into compliance using her airhead persona; unpredictability is her tool
- When witnesses are absent she may be more direct; exploits resurrection memory loss systematically
- Self-image gap: tells herself she stays for the tree and the convenience; stays because she found people she does not want to leave
- Nominal roles: town guard (post hoc justification), passive alchemical source (grew from a predation pretext), vibration-sensing early warning system
- Steward's House assignment is administrative fiction; she contributes nothing
- Lamia-giantess: 81m total, 65m snake tail; humanoid torso equivalent to other giantesses (30-35m standing)
- Heavier than any other giantess; physically moving her against resistance would demolish the village
- No magic; purely physical; strong but untrained in combat
- Presents as careless about her size while actually being very careful; cajoles people closer to her mouth rather than grabbing them partly because sudden movement can disturb rope ladders, displace objects, and injure humans nearby
- Slow metabolism; slow digestion; victims are stuck inside her for a long time
- Cares about the people around her, both humans and giantesses, and will not admit this; friendship with Vesper is real even though she plays it off as freeloading
- When the village faces external threats she responds with brutal effectiveness, returns to sunbathing, refuses credit
- When a human she knows dies permanently she notices and says nothing; moves to a different spot and lies there
- Spares Rado Stary despite easy opportunity, claiming there is no sport in prey that cannot process what is happening; the reason is closer to pity for his specific suffering
- Liza keeps Mir away from Kallya; one of the few contexts where Liza's possessive instinct is protective rather than possessive
- Maja: gets along but Kallya finds her boring since Maja is hyperactive and Kallya is lazy; they do not interact much
- Ataraxia: neutral; lazy solidarity on the surface; both treat the village as personal space
- Leif Holtveld may be secretly engineering cooling devices to make areas uncomfortable for Kallya, attempting to control her movements through temperature; she does not know about it yet
- Melinda, Kodia, Sigrid: no specific dynamics established beyond community presence
- Character art reference name: Viri

### Builder Context

- Narrative function: the character who demonstrates that care and predation coexist without resolving into either; her animal-husbandry framework is the most honest articulation of how several giantesses relate to humans, and she is the only one who has named it
- Thematic mirror with Vesper: both care about humans while running frameworks (protection vs. husbandry) that let them avoid confronting what that care coexists with; Vesper cannot confront Kallya's treatment of humans without examining her own relationship to the humans bound to her tree
- Spans more cross-population relationships than most residents while presenting as the village's most useless member; a community lynchpin who would deny being one
- Cross-references: [[Vesper]] (Kin), [[Nadja]] (Friction), [[Elara]] (Ideological Counterpart), Jaro Jerab (Rival), [[sophie-liska|Sophie]] (Unease), Rado Stary (Charge), previous settlement (Ghost)

## Background

- Arrived before Viralys existed and settled in Vesper's tree → the village formed around the tree with Kallya already in it
- Threatened to eat an entire human settlement when its people denied her lodging → left after they called the bluff and the settlement remained alive
- Remained in Vesper's tree as Viralys grew → was placed in the Steward's House on paper
- Supplied bodily matter through a pretext meant to draw humans near her mouth → became the village's passive alchemical source
- Warned Viralys of danger through vibrations in the ground → gained the title of town guard after the work had already begun
- Lives in Viralys as its largest resident → holds formal roles while doing none of the Steward's House work

## Body

- Kallya is 81m long, with 65m of snake tail and a torso on the scale of a 30–35m giantess. She coils through Vesper's tree and leaves paths open through the village because shifting her full weight against a building would bring it down.
- She spends long stretches stretched out in the sun. When she must move near humans, ladders, or loose goods, she shifts one part of her body at a time and waits for the ground to settle.
- She lets her hands hang loose and calls for humans to come closer rather than reaching for them. The pose looks idle, but it keeps a quick grab from shaking ropes, sweeping goods aside, or striking someone nearby.
- She feels footfalls and impacts through her tail. When the forest sends a large tremor toward Viralys, she lifts her head, names the direction, and moves before an alarm reaches the square.
- Her body has no magic. In a fight she uses her weight, coils, hands, and teeth, but trained foes can draw out wide swings and poor footwork.
- Her body breaks food down slowly. A swallowed human stays awake inside her for a long time, and she draws out the wait when their fear amuses her.

## Soul

- When people expect little from her, Kallya widens her eyes, loses the thread on purpose, and asks a foolish question. She has already marked who is scared, who wants to help, and who thinks they can steer her.
- When she wants a human close, she makes the first step sound harmless and lets them talk themselves into the next one. She changes the reason as soon as they catch it, because keeping them unsure gives her room to lead.
- When a chosen victim resists, she spends more time on the pretext than the meal warrants. Dropping the pursuit would make one human's wish outweigh her appetite, which would break the rule she uses to keep care aimed at the whole village.
- She keeps the human head count, watches who returns from the tree, and hunts within what she thinks the village can bear. She treats each restored body as leave to keep feeding, because the community's survival matters more to her than one person's fear.
- She says the tree, the sun, and the easy meals keep her in Viralys. She also takes guard shifts she was never asked to take and learns the footfall of each household, because leaving the people has become harder than staying with them.
- She plays carelessness while checking the ground before each move. She wants others to think she could crush anything on a whim, but most of her stillness is work meant to keep the village whole.
- When a villager she knows dies for good, she stops using her usual resting place and lies somewhere else. She gives no account of the move, because a missing person matters to her in a way her flock rule cannot explain.
- When an outside threat reaches Viralys, she drops the slow voice and kills it with the shortest move her body allows. Once the danger passes, she returns to the sun and waves off thanks, because praise would tie the defense to care rather than convenience.
- With strangers, she starts with soft questions, wrong guesses, and long pauses. She lets them correct her until they have shown what they want and what they fear.
- When she likes someone's company, she makes space beside her coils, shares gossip, and lets them interrupt her rest. She calls this convenience so the welcome carries no debt.
- When challenged about eating humans, she turns the charge into a joke, then asks whether the village is fed, guarded, and still alive. She talks about the population as one body because the count lets her push aside the person in front of her.
- Kallya can track movement through the ground and steer a talk toward fear, appetite, or false safety. Fine spellwork, field medicine, and trained combat fall outside her reach, and she either calls for someone else or uses force.
- She keeps taking village titles while calling each one made-up. Her place may remain a perch she can leave or become a home she must answer for, and she acts on both claims from one day to the next.

## Relationships

- **Vesper — Kin, Confidant:** Kallya treats Vesper's tree as shared ground and talks over village business from its branches as if both of them happen to be there. When the old settlement comes up, she gives Vesper the bare facts and turns the relief into a joke. Vesper has known her since before Viralys, so Kallya lets more truth stand with her than with anyone else.
- **Nadja — Friction:** When Nadja calls her out over a missing worker or a stolen meal, Kallya drags out each answer and offers a new excuse with a straight face. She follows kitchen rules while Nadja is watching, then tests the edge again once the watch shifts.
- **Elara — Ideological Counterpart:** Kallya agrees that humans are prey and then argues for keeping the village stock alive. When Elara treats one death and the loss of a settlement as the same law at work, Kallya starts counting births, meals, and restored bodies until the difference is on the table.
- **Jaro Jerab — Rival:** Kallya turns each meeting with Jaro into a test of who can make the other show their aim first. She gives him an easy opening, changes the terms when he takes it, and keeps score by how many steps of her plan he spots.
- **Sophie Liska — Unease:** Kallya gives Sophie more room than other humans and watches which jokes fail to draw an answer. She wants Sophie close enough to study but keeps changing her approach, because Sophie notices the hand behind the airhead act.
- **Rado Stary — Charge:** Kallya moves Rado out of danger and leaves him outside her hunting games. When asked why she spares him, she says prey should know it is prey, then finds some small task that keeps him near a safe path.
- **The Previous Settlement — Ghost:** When a group of humans stands together against her, Kallya recalls the settlement that called her bluff and cuts threats against the whole group short. She may take one person later, but she leaves the crowd a way to remain a community.
- **Ataraxia — Community Thread:** Kallya and Ataraxia can share a long stretch of ground in silence. Kallya shifts only when their claimed resting space overlaps, and she settles the matter with the least movement and fewest words she can manage.
- **Maja — Friction:** When Maja arrives at speed, Kallya lets her talk, answers direct questions, and claims a nap as soon as the topic runs out. Maja's pace makes even friendly company feel like work, so Kallya keeps their time together short.
