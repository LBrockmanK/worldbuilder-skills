# Nadja

## Design Notes

### Session Notes

- Born in Viralys; mother left once she considered Nadja old enough — not very old by human standards, but giantesses reach independence faster; her mother kept watch longer than some; no trauma or bitterness about it
- [[Maja]]'s mother abandoned her young (or at least that's presumed — no one ever saw her); Nadja started looking out for Maja and took a big-sister role, which developed into caretaking more broadly
- The caretaking instinct started with Maja specifically — a kid who needed someone — and grew outward as the village's practical needs gave structure; she didn't set out to become the person who feeds everyone; it accumulated
- Now serves as kitchen forewoman, feeding both human and giantess populations
- Core fear: if she stops controlling the outcome, someone gets hurt; not just professional diligence but personal — she's between fragile humans and giantess-scale hazards every day; the warmth she won't acknowledge is what makes the stakes feel so high
- Self-image gap: sees what she does as normal, thinks others should be more responsible — while her overbearing nature prevents them from developing that responsibility; creating the problem she's solving
- Unconsciously infantilizes humans; not malicious — just how things are to her; humans are small, fragile, need looking after; she likes them, feeds them, cares individually — but doesn't think of them as fully autonomous
- When things go wrong, doubles down: more rules, more oversight, more hands-on control; human autonomy shrinks further
- Refuses to delegate dangerous tasks even when someone else would do them better; result is she's overworked and occasionally makes the exact mistakes she's trying to prevent; root is the control pattern — can't trust anyone else to be careful enough
- Won't admit she enjoys people having fun at work; bark worse than bite; doesn't notice she softens around people enjoying themselves or that her punishments for minor infractions are lighter than she thinks
- Self-reliant; no one she's personally vulnerable with; always the one holding it together
- Warmth is acts of service she doesn't draw attention to: preparing someone's favorite dish, making sure they remember to eat
- Physical discipline scales with target: [[Maja]] gets hit (durable peer); [[strelitzia-liza|Liza]] gets standard child discipline; humans get contained — pinned under a finger, shoved in a pocket, put on a shelf, held up for a talking-down; constricting but not harmful
- Protectiveness operates within resurrection framework: strict about safety because accidents are disruptive and unpleasant, not because she treats human life as sacred

### Builder Context

- Narrative function: the giantess whose caretaking has become control; demonstrates how genuine kindness becomes suffocating when the caretaker cannot let go; the self-reinforcing cycle (accident → tighter grip → overwork → more accidents) is the engine
- The [[Maja]] dynamic is reciprocal across both notes: Maja's Confidant entry describes getting more of Nadja than anyone; Nadja's Kin entry describes the origin of the caretaking instinct
- [[strelitzia-liza|Liza]]'s note labels Nadja as Authority; from Nadja's side the label is Charge — she has self-appointed the responsibility because [[Ataraxia]] isn't around to do it
- [[Melinda]]'s note does not currently have a Nadja relationship entry; Nadja's Obligation entry describes the supply-chain discipline dynamic, which may warrant a reciprocal entry from Melinda's side

## Body

- In the kitchen, she keeps humans in sight on counters, shelves, and marked work paths. When one strays near a hot pan or a falling load, she moves them with a fingertip or closes them in her palm until the danger passes.
- When she gives a human a reprimand, she lifts them to eye height or sets them on a shelf beside her. Her finger holds them in place while she lists each rule they broke.
- She works with her hands close to the surface and keeps her tail and hips clear of human paths. A loose elbow, a swinging pot, or one missed step can crush a workday.
- She stands over a station until she has checked its heat, tools, and people. By the end of a long shift, flour and grease mark her hands and forearms.

## Soul

- When a batch burns, a worker is hurt, or a delivery runs late, she writes a new rule and checks the next shift herself. She treats tighter control as the way to keep the next mistake from landing on someone small.
- She takes the knife, the hot pan, and the high shelf task from other hands even when another cook has the better skill. She stays at the station until fatigue makes her miss a count or leave a pot too long.
- When people wait for her to solve each small problem, she scolds them for making her do it. Her own hands have already taken the task before they can learn it.
- When someone says they can manage a risk, she cuts them off and does it herself. A bad outcome feels like proof that she should have held tighter.
- She says a meal is only part of the work, then sets aside a favorite dish and calls a worker back when they miss it. She leaves before anyone can thank her.
- When a crew laughs while it works, her voice stays sharp but her punishments shrink to extra cleaning or a short lecture. She keeps watching until the task is done.
- With strangers, she asks what job brought them to the kitchen and gives them one clear task. She watches their hands before she gives them access to heat, blades, or stores.
- She speaks in short orders, names the tool or shelf she means, and repeats a rule word for word. A person who asks for help gets a task broken into steps and a place beside her worktable.
- Before each service, she counts stores, checks the hunt intake, and shifts portions between the human and giantess meals. Feeding both populations leaves little slack when a hunt comes in thin or a pot goes wrong.
- She can turn a giant kill into meals, set heat across the kitchen, and judge how much food a shift needs. For sickness, crop loss, and changes in the village ledger, she sends people to the healer, field forewoman, or Vesper instead of making a call herself.

## Relationships

- **[[Maja]] — Kin:** Nadja checks Maja for cuts and missed meals before she asks about the hunt. When Maja takes a risk that puts humans near danger, Nadja hits her hard enough to make the point, then has food waiting when the argument ends.
- **[[strelitzia-liza|Liza]] — Charge:** When Ataraxia is away, Nadja gives Liza a place by the kitchen and keeps her out of hot work. She uses the same child rules each time, sits Liza aside after rough play, and gives her a task she can finish under watch.
- **[[Melinda]] — Obligation:** Nadja meets Melinda's deliveries with a list, a scale, and a count of what the kitchen used. A short load brings Nadja to the smithy or fields with the list in hand, because a missed supply shifts the burden onto her crew.
- **[[Vesper]] — Authority:** Nadja brings Vesper the kitchen's shortages after she has counted them twice. She keeps the report brief, asks for a clear answer, and returns to the kitchen with a new work plan even when no answer comes that day.
- **[[Ataraxia]] — Unease:** When Ataraxia enters the kitchen, Nadja clears humans from the nearest worktops and keeps Liza within reach. She speaks to Ataraxia in work terms and steps in faster around Liza when Ataraxia leaves the room.
- **[[Kallya]] — Rival:** When Kallya praises a new way to run a shared task, Nadja tests it against her own counts before she lets it into the kitchen. She keeps the better result and works harder the next day to show that her line can match it.
- **[[Sigrid]] — Ideological Counterpart:** When Sigrid argues that grown humans should bear their own risks, Nadja points to the heat, blades, and giant-sized loads around them. She gives humans more rules after these talks and watches for the first mistake that will prove her case.
- **[[Sophie Liska]] — Friction:** When Sophie pushes back on a kitchen rule, Nadja gives her the reason, then puts her on the safest part of the shift. She checks Sophie's work more often after an argument and sends her home with food when the shift runs late.
