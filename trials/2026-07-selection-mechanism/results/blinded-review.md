## Version X

### X.1
Her operating code says humans are individually expendable. When a specific human's suffering registers — not a category but this person, this moment — she makes an exception and explains it away: unsporting, inconvenient, not worth the effort. The excuses get thinner each time, but as long as she has one, the code holds.

### X.4

### X.5
She arranges her life to avoid a single conversation: the one where someone says plainly that she cares about these people as people, and she cannot deflect with a joke. ### X.7

She named what other giantesses avoid naming: individual humans are expendable, the community is not. The naming is what makes them uncomfortable around her.

Each explanation is factually true and none of them are the reason. Ask her what would make her leave and the answer comes slower, full of qualifications, and ends with a subject change.

### X.9
