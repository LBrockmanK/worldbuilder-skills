# Nadja — Design Notes

## Design Notes

### Session Notes

- Born in Viralys; mother left once she considered Nadja old enough — not very old by human standards, but giantesses reach independence faster; her mother kept watch longer than some; no trauma or bitterness about it
- [[Maja]]'s mother abandoned her young (or at least that's presumed — no one ever saw her); Nadja started looking out for Maja and took a big-sister role, which developed into caretaking more broadly
- The caretaking instinct started with Maja specifically — a kid who needed someone — and grew outward as the village's practical needs gave structure; she didn't set out to become the person who feeds everyone; it accumulated
- Now [FLAGGED: copula_avoidance] kitchen forewoman, feeding both human and giantess populations
- Core fear: if she stops controlling the outcome, someone gets hurt; not just professional diligence but personal — she's between fragile humans and giantess-scale hazards every day; the warmth she won't acknowledge is what makes the stakes feel so high
- Self-image [FLAGGED: ai_vocabulary]: sees what she does as normal, thinks others should be more responsible — while her overbearing nature prevents them from developing that responsibility; creating the problem she's solving
- Unconsciously infantilizes humans; not malicious — just how things are to her; humans are small, fragile, need looking after; she likes them, feeds them, cares individually — but doesn't think of them as fully autonomous
- When things go wrong, doubles down: more rules, more oversight, more hands-on control; human autonomy shrinks further
- Refuses to delegate dangerous tasks even when someone else would do them better; result is she's overworked and occasionally makes the exact mistakes she's trying to prevent; root is the control pattern — can't trust anyone else to be careful enough
- Won't admit she enjoys people having fun at work; bark worse than bite; doesn't notice she softens around people enjoying themselves or that her punishments for minor infractions are lighter than she thinks
- Self-reliant; no one she's personally vulnerable with; always the one holding it together
- Warmth is acts of service she doesn't draw attention to: preparing someone's favorite dish, making sure they remember to eat
- Physical discipline scales with target: [[Maja]] gets hit (durable peer); [[strelitzia-liza|Liza]] gets standard child discipline; humans get contained — pinned under a finger, shoved in a pocket, put on a shelf, held up for a talking-down; constricting but not harmful
- Protectiveness operates within resurrection framework: strict about safety because accidents are disruptive and unpleasant, not because she treats human life as sacred
