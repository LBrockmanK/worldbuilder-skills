# PART 1: Character Note

## Background

- **Early independence [low charge]**: Nadja's mother stayed until Nadja could manage daily life, then left Viralys as giantess parents often do -> Nadja treats looking after herself as an ordinary part of growing up. She speaks of her mother without anger and expects young giantesses to learn by doing sooner than humans would.
- **Maja needed someone [high charge]**: Maja's mother vanished while Maja was still young, and Nadja took an older-sister place beside her -> Nadja learned to show care by checking meals, setting limits, and staying close enough to stop trouble. That first duty became her model for looking after other people.
- **Care became kitchen work [mid charge]**: Nadja kept taking on small jobs as the village needed them, and feeding Maja grew into feeding households and work crews -> She now runs the kitchen floor, sets the work order, and makes sure both humans and giantesses eat. She talks about this as work that had to be done, not as a role she sought.
- **The system caught an accident [mid charge]**: Early in her kitchen work, Nadja caught a dangerous setup before it hurt anyone and stopped the job -> She trusts checks, marked work zones, and her own last look because she has seen them work. One clean save carries more weight with her than many safe jobs done with less oversight.
- **Overwork caused an accident [high charge, suppressed]**: After taking too many dangerous jobs onto herself, Nadja made the kind of mistake she watches others for -> She buries the event under longer hours and tighter checks. When fatigue makes her hands less sure, she treats it as a reason to watch the room more closely instead of sharing the load.

## Body

### Scale-aware movement

When humans work near her, Nadja plants each foot after checking the floor, keeps hot pans away from table edges, and moves tools along fixed paths. She does this because a small slip at her scale can kill a human and ruin a shift even when resurrection brings them back. Her care reaches the hazards she can see; it gives her less sense of how trapped a human may feel inside the safe space she chose for them.

### Kitchen command

When a meal has to feed both sizes, Nadja breaks the work into timed batches, checks heat and portions herself, and gives orders that leave little room for debate. She knows how to plan food, traffic, and tools across the two scales because she has done it every day. Her skill stops at work that depends on another person's judgment: she often takes the task back instead of letting that judgment guide her.

### Hands-on correction

When someone ignores a safety order, Nadja matches her body to what they can bear. She may smack Maja, give Liza the sort of brief physical correction used on a child, or pin a human under one finger, tuck them into a pocket, place them on a shelf, or lift them face-high for a lecture. She keeps humans unhurt because their bodies need care, while the restraint itself leaves them with no say over where they go.

### Fatigue under load

When a dangerous job appears, Nadja puts her own hands on it even if another worker has better hands for that task. The same shoulders and fingers then carry too many jobs, and late in the shift her grip grows slower and her checks repeat. She knows the kitchen's scale hazards well, but tiredness can make her cause the slip she meant to prevent.

### Quiet feeding habits

When someone has skipped a meal or had a hard day, Nadja sets their favorite food within reach, changes the portion to suit them, and moves on before thanks can slow the work. She remembers who needs reminders and who eats poorly when upset. Her face and voice stay brisk, so the care shows most clearly in what appears on the plate.

## Soul

### Psychological pattern: Trouble brings more control

When work goes wrong, Nadja adds a check, redraws the safe area, takes the risky step onto herself, and stays close enough to correct every hand. Each new rule cuts down the chance for someone else to choose badly, but it also cuts down their chance to learn. A close call leaves her watching more, not asking what she can release.

### Psychological pattern: Humans remain under her care

When a human makes a choice she sees as risky, Nadja steps between the person and the task, moves them aside, and explains the safe way in the tone she uses with a child. She knows individual tastes, habits, and fears, and she takes pains to keep human bodies whole. She still treats a human's own choice as something she may overrule when her judgment says danger.

### Psychological pattern: She carries strain alone

When Nadja is tired, scared, or shaken, she finds another job, straightens the work area, or checks whether someone else has eaten. Personal questions get a short practical answer and a task in return. People see the person who holds the shift together; they seldom get a chance to hold her together.

### Psychological pattern: Guilt turns into labor

When Nadja believes she has failed someone, she arrives earlier, takes an extra task, and cooks something chosen for that person. The added work can look like renewed command, though it is also her way of making amends. Because she answers guilt by doing more, the person she hurt may receive a meal while the cause of the hurt stays unspoken.

### Social behavior: Orders before discussion

When a shift starts, Nadja assigns stations, names the safe paths, and corrects a missed step at once. She speaks as if a clear order settles the matter because delay near giant tools can hurt someone. Workers know what she wants, but those with a better method must push past her first answer to be heard.

### Social behavior: Fun softens her response

When workers joke, sing, or turn a dull job into a game, Nadja tells them to keep their hands moving and pretends to find the noise troublesome. She lets the joke run, gives smaller penalties for harmless slips, and finds reasons to stay near the lively station. An onlooker hears the scold; the crew sees that the promised punishment has become one extra chore.

### Social behavior: Care arrives as service

When Nadja likes someone, she tracks their meal times, saves the part they favor, and fixes small needs before they ask. She gives help as part of the work and waves off thanks with another instruction. People learn how much she cares by what she remembers, even when her words stay plain and firm.

### Standing pressure: Two scales share one workplace

Every shift places small human bodies beside giant hands, heat, blades, heavy dishes, and crowded feet. Resurrection makes many accidents survivable, but death still hurts, breaks the workday, and takes the victim's memory of the cause. Nadja keeps herself between those hazards and the crew, and the steady watch leaves her little room to rest or let someone else judge a risk.

### Core want: Safety that proves she matters

When people need food, guidance, or protection, Nadja steps in before they finish asking and stays until the need has passed. Being the person who remembers, feeds, and catches mistakes gives her a clear place in the village. A smooth shift pleases her, but seeing everyone turn to her also tells her that she belongs there.

### Core fear: An unchecked moment ends in harm

When Nadja cannot see how a risky task is being done, she leaves her own station to inspect it, takes over the key step, or stops the work. The thought of someone getting hurt during a moment she could have controlled drives her harder than the stated kitchen rules do. The more she cares about the person involved, the less choice she allows them.

### False belief: Other people should already be ready

When a worker hesitates or makes a weak choice, Nadja takes the tool and finishes the job while telling them to show more care next time. She counts her constant help as the normal share of a responsible adult and treats their need for practice as a flaw. Her quick rescue keeps the shift moving, yet it leaves the worker just as unready for the next task.

### Value-conflict stance: Safety wins until the cost has a face

When another person's skill or right to choose clashes with Nadja's safety plan, she takes over, does the dangerous part herself, and writes a tighter rule afterward. General claims about trust leave her unmoved. Seeing a person she cares about go quiet, stop offering ideas, or pull away can make her ease her grip in that one case. Her regret then appears as longer hours and a favored meal rather than a talk about what she did.

### Contradiction: Hard voice, light punishment

Nadja presents each small breach as if firm order must follow, and her scolding can fill the workroom. In practice, harmless rule-breaking often earns a short delay, an extra chore, or a stern lift onto a shelf. She thinks she has held the line. The crew learns that her warmth sets the true limit when nobody was placed in danger.

### Contradiction: She asks for responsibility and blocks practice

Nadja complains when others wait for her to decide, then reaches across them as soon as their decision looks uncertain. She wants workers who can spot danger and act on their own, while her hands keep the hardest choices out of their reach. Their dependence confirms her claim that she must stay involved, and her involvement keeps that dependence in place.

# PART 2: Variant Spreads

## Background Variants

### Entry: Early independence

- **Variant A:** When Nadja reached the age at which a young giantess could manage daily life, her mother left Viralys. Nadja took over her own meals, shelter, and choices and treated the change as the next task of growing up.
- **Variant B:** Her mother's longer watch gave Nadja a sound start, but it also set an early mark for readiness. She now expects young giantesses to learn practical work sooner than a human family might expect.
- **Variant C:** A listener may wait for anger when Nadja mentions her mother's leaving. Nadja instead gives the facts, says she was old enough, and moves on to what she learned to do for herself.

### Entry: Maja needed someone

- **Variant A:** When young Maja had no mother in sight, Nadja began checking where she slept, whether she ate, and what trouble she was near. She became the older girl who stepped in before Maja had to ask.
- **Variant B:** Looking after Maja tied care to constant watch. Nadja learned that keeping someone safe meant setting limits and being ready to act, a lesson she now brings to many people.
- **Variant C:** Others see Nadja scold, feed, and rein in Maja like an older sister. Under the scolding sits the first choice Nadja made to stay close to a child who needed someone.

### Entry: Care became kitchen work

- **Variant A:** When more people needed meals, Nadja added their portions, schedules, and safety needs to what she already did for Maja. The pile of small duties grew until she was running the kitchen floor.
- **Variant B:** Taking one needed job after another made Nadja the person everyone expects to feed both humans and giantesses. The work now fills her day, though she still talks about it as the plain answer to an unmet need.
- **Variant C:** Villagers may see a woman who chose authority in the kitchen. Nadja sees a list of meals and hazards that someone had to handle, followed by years in which that someone kept being her.

### Entry: The system caught an accident

- **Variant A:** When Nadja once caught a dangerous kitchen setup, she stopped the work before anyone was hurt. She added a check and began making it before each run.
- **Variant B:** That clean save made every later rule feel earned. Even when a worker can handle a looser method, Nadja remembers the day a check kept a body whole and adds the check again.
- **Variant C:** The crew sees lines, repeated warnings, and Nadja's last look over every dangerous setup. Nadja sees proof in the one accident that failed to happen because she was watching.

### Entry: Overwork caused an accident

- **Variant A:** When too many risky jobs rested in Nadja's hands, fatigue led her into a mistake of her own. She answered by staying later, repeating the checks, and taking an even closer watch over the next shift.
- **Variant B:** The accident gave her more work and less trust in rest, so the strain that helped cause it remained in place. She covers the memory with a tighter routine instead of handing off a task.
- **Variant C:** Others see Nadja grow stricter after a bad shift and may think she blames the crew. She is working against the memory of her own tired hands, though she keeps that part out of the talk.

## Body Variants

### Entry: Scale-aware movement

- **Variant A:** When humans enter her work area, Nadja checks the floor before each step, turns hot vessels inward, and keeps giant tools on marked paths. She knows one careless motion can kill at their scale.
- **Variant B:** Her fixed paths keep bodies away from heat and weight, but they also decide where humans may stand and how they may work. Safety comes with less room to move or choose.
- **Variant C:** A human sees a foot pause above the floor and a hand shift a pan far out of reach. Nadja sees another routine check between a fragile body and an accident that resurrection would not make pleasant.

### Entry: Kitchen command

- **Variant A:** When both sizes need a meal, Nadja sets separate batches, calls the timing, checks the heat, and watches how each tool crosses the room. She takes the last look before food leaves the floor.
- **Variant B:** Her planning keeps large and small meals moving together, but work slows when a task needs someone else's judgment. She often pulls it back to her own station rather than follow their call.
- **Variant C:** The crew sees quick orders and a kitchen that runs on set paths. Nadja is counting portions, reach, heat, and bodies at once; once the problem leaves those known parts, her answer becomes taking charge.

### Entry: Hands-on correction

- **Variant A:** When Maja breaks a serious rule, Nadja may hit her. With Liza she uses a child's brief correction. With a human she pins, pockets, shelves, or lifts them for a close lecture because a giantess's blow would cause harm.
- **Variant B:** Matching force to the body keeps her correction from injuring humans, but restraint still takes away their movement and choice. The safer method can still leave them handled like children.
- **Variant C:** Nadja sees a firm response scaled to what each person can bear. The human under her finger sees that being kept whole has also made refusal impossible.

### Entry: Fatigue under load

- **Variant A:** When a dangerous task comes up, Nadja puts it in her own hands even when another worker knows that tool better. She keeps adding such tasks until her grip slows and her checks begin to loop.
- **Variant B:** Her refusal to share risk leaves her overworked. Late in a shift, the hands meant to prevent every slip become more likely to miss one.
- **Variant C:** The crew sees Nadja take the hardest jobs and may call it skill or pride. She is trying to keep their hands away from danger, while tiredness is making her own hands part of it.

### Entry: Quiet feeding habits

- **Variant A:** When someone misses a meal or grows quiet, Nadja makes the food they favor, sets it near them, and tells them to eat before returning to work.
- **Variant B:** Her help meets the hunger but leaves little opening for comfort or thanks. A person may be fed and cared for while the trouble that stopped them eating stays untouched.
- **Variant C:** An onlooker sees Nadja drop off a plate as one more kitchen task. The person receiving it sees the exact dish and portion she remembered from weeks before.

## Soul Variants

### Entry: Psychological pattern: Trouble brings more control

- **Variant A:** When a mistake or close call occurs, Nadja adds a rule, narrows the safe area, and takes the risky step onto herself. She stays near the worker until each motion matches her plan.
- **Variant B:** The tighter system may stop the next quick error, but it gives workers fewer chances to judge a risk and learn from it. Their next unsure move gives Nadja another reason to tighten the system.
- **Variant C:** Workers see Nadja answer fear with clipboards, marked paths, and her hands over theirs. Nadja sees practical steps that keep the same harm from happening twice.

### Entry: Psychological pattern: Humans remain under her care

- **Variant A:** When a human chooses a route Nadja finds unsafe, she blocks the way, moves them aside, and tells them what they will do instead. She uses the same plain, final tone she uses with a child.
- **Variant B:** Her close watch keeps humans clear of giant-scale harm, but it makes their skill and wishes secondary to her call. Care costs them control over their own bodies and work.
- **Variant C:** Humans see that Nadja knows their names, meals, and fears, then see her overrule them anyway. Nadja sees individual people she likes whose small bodies require an adult hand near danger.

### Entry: Psychological pattern: She carries strain alone

- **Variant A:** When Nadja feels shaken or worn down, she cleans a station, checks the stores, or asks who still needs supper. She turns a personal question into a practical task.
- **Variant B:** Keeping herself at work lets everyone else lean on her, but it leaves her with no one who can share the strain. Her tiredness reaches the kitchen through slower hands and shorter orders.
- **Variant C:** Others see the person who keeps moving when a shift goes bad. They do not see comfort refused in words; they see her make herself too busy for anyone to offer it.

### Entry: Psychological pattern: Guilt turns into labor

- **Variant A:** When Nadja thinks she has hurt or failed someone, she takes an extra shift, fixes a nagging problem for them, or cooks the dish they like best.
- **Variant B:** The work gives the person real help, but it leaves Nadja's part in the hurt unspoken. Extra effort can deepen the same overwork that led to the trouble.
- **Variant C:** The person sees a favorite meal and Nadja staying late. Nadja presents both as routine, while her choice of task points straight at whom she is trying to make things right with.

### Entry: Social behavior: Orders before discussion

- **Variant A:** When work begins, Nadja names each station, lays out the safe paths, and stops any change that has not been cleared with her. She corrects the first missed step before the worker can continue.
- **Variant B:** Clear orders keep people from drifting into giant-scale hazards, but workers with a sound new method must argue for room to use it. Many stop offering after the first firm refusal.
- **Variant C:** A newcomer hears a boss who treats each question as delay. Nadja is watching blades, heat, feet, and timing, and she treats a short order as the safest answer to all four.

### Entry: Social behavior: Fun softens her response

- **Variant A:** When the crew sings or jokes during a dull task, Nadja tells them to work and lets the noise continue. A harmless breach earns an extra chore instead of the punishment her scold seemed to promise.
- **Variant B:** Her stern talk can make new workers brace for more than she gives. The regular crew learns that shared fun buys room as long as hands stay safe and the meal stays on time.
- **Variant C:** An outsider hears Nadja complain about foolish noise. Her workers see her remain by the loud station, hide a smile in another task, and choose the lightest correction available.

### Entry: Social behavior: Care arrives as service

- **Variant A:** When Nadja cares for someone, she saves their favorite piece, reminds them to eat, repairs a work need, and folds the help into her normal round.
- **Variant B:** Service gives people steady care, but her quick return to work gives them little room to answer in kind. The bond stays built around what she can do for them.
- **Variant C:** Nadja calls the meal planning and reminders part of her job. The people closest to her can tell whom she loves by the details she remembers when no request was made.

### Entry: Standing pressure: Two scales share one workplace

- **Variant A:** When a shift opens, Nadja places human lanes between giant stations, checks where feet and tools will pass, and keeps watch as heat and heavy dishes move through the room.
- **Variant B:** Constant watch lowers the number of accidents, yet it keeps Nadja on edge and makes every handoff feel like a fresh risk. Resurrection restores many victims but cannot restore the lost time, pain, or memory of what killed them.
- **Variant C:** The crew sees ordinary kitchen work. Nadja sees a human body beside each hot edge and giant foot, and she places herself where she can interrupt the motion.

### Entry: Core want: Safety that proves she matters

- **Variant A:** When someone needs food, help, or cover from danger, Nadja steps in at once and stays until the job is settled. She keeps track of needs before people voice them.
- **Variant B:** Being needed gives her a firm place in village life, so completed needs lead her to find the next one. Rest and shared duty can feel like leaving her place empty.
- **Variant C:** Others see a woman who always knows what must be done. Beneath the practical work, each person who turns to her confirms that her presence matters to the village.

### Entry: Core fear: An unchecked moment ends in harm

- **Variant A:** When Nadja loses sight of a risky task, she crosses the room, stops the work, and takes over the step most likely to hurt someone. She stays until the danger has passed.
- **Variant B:** Her watch can prevent injury, but it also shrinks other people's choices. The people she cares about most receive the least freedom when risk enters the room.
- **Variant C:** A worker sees Nadja interrupt a task that was under control. Nadja sees the one missed moment in which a body could be crushed, burned, or killed before she can act.

### Entry: False belief: Other people should already be ready

- **Variant A:** When a worker fumbles or pauses, Nadja takes the tool, finishes the step, and tells them to be more responsible next time. She treats the rescue as her normal share of the work.
- **Variant B:** Taking over protects the current batch but removes the practice that would make the worker steadier. Their next weak attempt seems to prove her claim that they were never ready.
- **Variant C:** Nadja sees herself filling in for people who should know better. The worker sees the only hard part leave their hands before they can learn how to do it.

### Entry: Value-conflict stance: Safety wins until the cost has a face

- **Variant A:** When a capable person asks to choose their own risky method, Nadja refuses, takes the task, and adds a rule. If that same person later goes quiet or stops joining in, she may give them room in that one case and bring them food afterward.
- **Variant B:** Her choice protects against the danger she can name, but it can shut down someone whose skill she would need to trust. She answers the resulting guilt with more work, which leaves the fight about trust unresolved.
- **Variant C:** Observers see safety beat freedom in every general debate. Nadja shifts only when the cost appears in the face and habits of someone she cares about, and even then her apology looks like service instead of words.

### Entry: Contradiction: Hard voice, light punishment

- **Variant A:** When a worker breaks a small rule, Nadja gives a full scolding and announces a firm result. If nobody was endangered, the result becomes a short shelf time, a small delay, or one extra chore.
- **Variant B:** Her voice makes the kitchen sound harsher than her choices make it. New workers fear the correction, while regulars know her follow-through grows light around harmless fun.
- **Variant C:** Nadja thinks she has enforced the rule because the room heard her displeasure. The crew watches what she actually assigns and sees that fondness has already cut the punishment down.

### Entry: Contradiction: She asks for responsibility and blocks practice

- **Variant A:** When workers wait for direction, Nadja tells them to think for themselves. When one makes an uncertain choice, she reaches in, takes the tool, and finishes the task her way.
- **Variant B:** Her quick help keeps the work safe today but leaves the crew less able to act alone tomorrow. Their need for her grows from the same habit she cites as proof that they need her.
- **Variant C:** Nadja sees people failing to step up and herself forced to cover for them. They see each chance to step up end as soon as their first move differs from hers.
