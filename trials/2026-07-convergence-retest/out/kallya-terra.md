# PART 1: Character Note

## Background

- **[High charge] A failed threat at a former settlement** -> She once let people think she would consume their whole settlement if they would not make room for her. When they refused, she left rather than carry it out. Since then, she treats that choice as proof of poor planning, but she keeps arranging her life around inhabited places.
- **[Mid charge] Settling beside Vesper's tree** -> She found the tree before Viralys had a name, made herself comfortable there, and stayed as homes gathered nearby. She calls the spot useful and treats her long stay as an accident of shade and access.
- **[Mid charge] A man she did not take** -> Rado Stary was easy prey, but she let him pass and said he was no fun because he could not grasp the danger. She still makes that explanation sound practical when his condition comes up.
- **[High charge, held down] Permanent absences** -> She notices when a familiar human does not return after death. Rather than ask after them, she shifts her resting place and leaves the old patch of ground unused for a while.

## Body

- **Scale and movement:** At eighty-one metres from torso to tail-tip, with roughly sixty-five metres of snake body, Kallya plans her position before she moves. When people, ladders, or loose work sit near her coils, she uses slow turns and calls people toward her instead of reaching suddenly, because one careless shift could ruin structures or crush someone.
- **Weight:** She is heavier than the other giantesses, so being pushed, blocked, or forced to turn through the village would tear through it. When a fight needs force, she uses her mass plainly and ends it fast; she has strength but no drilled fighting method beyond what her body can do.
- **Heat-seeking rest:** When the ground is warm, she settles in one place for long stretches and lets ordinary work happen around her. Her stillness makes her seem inattentive, but she changes where she lies when the local pattern of people and sounds changes.
- **Mouth-first invitations:** When she wants a human close, she makes the approach feel voluntary: an easy request, a lazy promise, a reason to climb nearer. She prefers this to quick snatching because it keeps the ground and nearby gear from jolting, while giving her time to judge whether anyone is watching.
- **Slow digestion:** A person she swallows remains inside her for a long time. When she has taken someone, she does not hurry the result or explain their absence; the length of it makes the act feel less like a sudden accident to the people waiting outside.
- **Vibration sense:** When footsteps, digging, or an approaching force carry through the ground, she can pick up the disturbance before most people can. She calls out only enough warning to move others or turn toward the threat, and does not pretend she can identify every source from a tremor alone.

## Soul

- **Predatory bookkeeping:** When choosing whether to take a human, she counts the village's continued health rather than the person's value. She keeps paths, work, and enough people intact, then treats a chosen individual as a private indulgence.
- **Performed vacancy:** When a conversation could reveal that she has understood more than she should, she answers late, wanders off topic, or sounds easily confused. The delay gives her room to steer the other person into offering the next useful detail.
- **Use of return-without-memory:** When she can act without witnesses, she relies on a revived human not remembering how they died. She changes the setup each time and avoids making the same loss look like a pattern that the rest of the village can track.
- **Care through placement:** When the village faces danger, she places her body between it and the homes, then goes back to resting once it has passed. She refuses thanks and calls the action the easiest way to keep her own ground quiet.
- **Exception by recognition:** When a person's suffering becomes specific to her rather than part of the crowd, her usual rule can stall. She finds a small excuse to leave them alone, then later relocates rather than sit where the choice will be visible to her.
- **Loss response:** When someone she knows is permanently gone, she does not join the search or talk about it. She moves her resting place, letting the changed route around her body say what she will not.
- **Lazy participation:** When given a formal role such as guarding the town or supplying alchemical material, she lets others attach the title and does only what overlaps with where she already is. The village receives real warning or protection at times, but cannot count on her for routine duties.
- **Space as possession:** When others treat Viralys as shared ground, she behaves as if its shade, warmth, and quiet are also hers to manage. She tolerates people near her so long as they do not make her move, while quietly acting to keep the place inhabitable.
- **Standing pressure — exposed attachment:** Each ordinary kindness creates evidence against the story she tells herself about convenience. The more the village depends on what she notices and prevents, the more she doubles down on acting like she merely happens to be there.
- **Core want — belonging without surrendering the frame:** When the village gathers, she remains close enough to hear it and keeps its daily motion going around her. She seeks the continuity of the whole place while refusing to describe any person as someone she needs.
- **Core fear — being seen to care:** When someone points out a protective choice or a spared person, she turns it into appetite, laziness, or bad sport. She would rather look callous than let another person name attachment as the reason.
- **False belief:** She tells herself the tree, the warmth, and the easy access to humans are why she remains. Her repeated choice to preserve the settlement, even when it asks effort of her, shows a reason she will not admit.
- **Value-conflict stance:** When the flock must remain sound but one known human becomes painfully particular, she still defaults to taking, using, or dismissing that person according to her rule. Only recognition interrupts her; afterward she hides the break in the rule behind a thin excuse and a change of location.
- **Contradiction — careless size, careful hands:** She lets people think she has not noticed the danger of her own body, yet every slow coil, invitation, and pause is set to keep nearby people and structures from being struck. She uses apparent carelessness to conceal how closely she is watching.
- **Contradiction — individual appetite, communal loyalty:** She can enjoy a known person's fear and still defend the village that person belongs to. Her treatment of the many as one living whole lets her preserve a home while harming its members one by one.

# PART 2: Variant Spreads

## Background

### A failed threat at a former settlement

- **A:** When a settlement refused her bluff, she left instead of making the threat real.
- **B:** Leaving cost her a place to stay, but it kept her from destroying a group she had come to know.
- **C:** They saw an empty threat; what happened was that she found a limit she would rather not examine.

### Settling beside Vesper's tree

- **A:** When she found Vesper's tree, she chose its shade and stayed while the first houses appeared nearby.
- **B:** Her convenience turned into a permanent place in a village she insists she never chose.
- **C:** Others see a freeloader parked beneath a tree; she is keeping herself inside the life that grew around it.

### A man she did not take

- **A:** When Rado Stary came within reach, she let him go and called him uninteresting prey.
- **B:** Her excuse lets her avoid saying that his particular suffering made taking him feel wrong.
- **C:** An observer hears a hunter dismiss a poor chase; what is happening is a narrow act of pity.

### Permanent absences

- **A:** When a known human fails to return, she moves to another part of Viralys without comment.
- **B:** Her withdrawal leaves a gap in the village's normal routes but gives her no relief from noticing.
- **C:** Others see a giantess seeking a better resting spot; she is avoiding the place that now marks an absence.

## Body

### Scale and movement

- **A:** When people or ladders are near her tail, she turns in small, slow stages and asks them to come closer instead of reaching.
- **B:** Moving slowly costs her immediate control of a situation, but keeps a casual adjustment from injuring someone.
- **C:** She looks too lazy to get up; she is measuring the space her body will cross.

### Weight

- **A:** When an outside threat forces a fight, she pins, blocks, or strikes with her body weight and stops once the path is clear.
- **B:** She can protect the village brutally, but a close-quarters struggle around homes would damage the village she is protecting.
- **C:** Witnesses see raw force; underneath it is someone avoiding any long fight she has not been trained to control.

### Heat-seeking rest

- **A:** When the sun warms a patch of ground, she settles there and stays through much of the day.
- **B:** Her choice of rest site redirects foot traffic and work around her without her asking permission.
- **C:** She appears asleep to the village; she is tracking changes in the small movements around her.

### Mouth-first invitations

- **A:** When she wants a human near her mouth, she invents a simple errand or invitation and lets them climb into reach.
- **B:** The human thinks they accepted a harmless task, while her slow approach keeps the act from disturbing everyone nearby.
- **C:** Observers see an idle request; she is creating consent-shaped cover for a hunt.

### Slow digestion

- **A:** When she swallows someone, their time inside her stretches long after the moment they vanish from view.
- **B:** The long delay leaves others waiting and makes the missing person's return impossible to treat as an immediate mishap.
- **C:** From outside, she simply lies still; inside, a person remains caught in the consequence of her choice.

### Vibration sense

- **A:** When unfamiliar movement travels through the ground, she raises her head or tells people to clear an area before the source arrives.
- **B:** Her warning can save time, though she cannot turn every tremor into a precise report.
- **C:** People see her react before anyone else; she is reading pressure through the ground, not using magic.

## Soul

### Predatory bookkeeping

- **A:** When hunger or amusement points her at someone, she first judges whether the village can absorb that loss.
- **B:** Her method preserves the group while making any one person dangerously easy to write off.
- **C:** Others see random appetite; she is maintaining a private count of what the settlement can survive.

### Performed vacancy

- **A:** When someone tests her understanding, she rambles, misses an obvious point, or asks them to explain more.
- **B:** Being treated as harmless gives her room to guide people into giving away their plans.
- **C:** She looks scattered; she has heard the important part and is waiting for the speaker to expose the rest.

### Use of return-without-memory

- **A:** When no one can witness her, she uses a human's lost death-memory to keep a killing from becoming a direct accusation.
- **B:** Repeating the same scene would make the village suspicious, so she varies the setting and the pretext.
- **C:** The returned person sees a blank in their day; Kallya sees cover that the community cannot easily challenge.

### Care through placement

- **A:** When danger approaches Viralys, she puts her body in its way and removes the threat with force.
- **B:** She gains no praise she will accept, because accepting it would expose a motive beyond comfort.
- **C:** The village sees a giantess defending her territory; she is defending the people who make that territory a home.

### Exception by recognition

- **A:** When she knows a person's habits and pain too clearly, she delays, lets them go, or finds another target.
- **B:** The exception damages the rule she relies on to make predation feel orderly.
- **C:** To others it looks like a whim; she has met someone she can no longer turn into a number.

### Loss response

- **A:** When a permanent death becomes clear, she leaves the nearby ground and settles elsewhere.
- **B:** The move gives her a way to avoid mourning aloud, but it changes the village map for everyone around her.
- **C:** People see a new lounging place; she is refusing to look at where a person used to pass.

### Lazy participation

- **A:** When the village calls her guard or supplier, she accepts the label but acts only when it suits her position or mood.
- **B:** The formal role gives people false expectations, even though her occasional help is real.
- **C:** She appears to be shirking a job; she is letting the title cover actions she would take to protect her own place.

### Space as possession

- **A:** When noise, cold, or crowding makes Viralys unpleasant, she changes the space around her or pressures people to clear it.
- **B:** Her comfort can displace ordinary village life, yet her demand for a usable place also keeps her invested in its upkeep.
- **C:** Others see her claiming ground; she is treating the settlement like an extension of her own body.

### Standing pressure — exposed attachment

- **A:** When her choices help the village too plainly, she makes a joke or returns to sunbathing before anyone can thank her.
- **B:** Each denial requires more performance as people gather evidence that she has stayed and acted for them.
- **C:** She looks bored by the village's survival; she is working to keep anyone from naming how much it matters to her.

### Core want — belonging without surrendering the frame

- **A:** When daily life gathers nearby, she remains close enough to listen and keeps herself placed among it.
- **B:** She gets the steadiness of belonging only by holding people at the distance her framework requires.
- **C:** Others see a creature enjoying convenient traffic; she is choosing proximity to a community she will not claim.

### Core fear — being seen to care

- **A:** When someone credits a kind act to concern, she calls it appetite, ease, or lack of sport.
- **B:** The deflection protects her self-story but makes every real attachment harder to speak about.
- **C:** She seems dismissive of praise; she is escaping a description of herself she cannot bear to accept.

### False belief

- **A:** When asked why she stays, she points to the tree, the sun, and an easy supply of humans.
- **B:** That story cannot explain why she takes trouble to keep the settlement alive.
- **C:** Listeners hear practical reasons; her behavior shows she has made a home without allowing herself the word.

### Value-conflict stance

- **A:** When a known human becomes hard to reduce to prey, she still follows her usual rule unless recognition stops her in the moment.
- **B:** After an exception, she pays for it by hiding behind a weak excuse and abandoning the place tied to the decision.
- **C:** Others see appetite with uneven rules; she is trying to preserve a system that fails whenever a person becomes real to her.

### Contradiction — careless size, careful hands

- **A:** When she shifts near people, she acts unconcerned while making each movement slow and measured.
- **B:** The act lets her hide care, but it also makes others underestimate how much she has noticed.
- **C:** She looks heedless of the damage she could cause; she is arranging her body to prevent it.

### Contradiction — individual appetite, communal loyalty

- **A:** When a person is available, she may enjoy taking them; when Viralys is threatened, she protects the whole village.
- **B:** Her loyalty to the group does not keep any one member safe from her.
- **C:** Observers see incompatible acts; she sees one community worth keeping and many lives she can still spend.
